<p>Looking for apps to track your cannabis use, find <a href="/en/">coffeeshops</a>, or get support to cut down or quit? Discover the best tools for a conscious and safe approach to cannabis.</p>
<h2>1. Coffeeshop Locators</h2>
<p>In countries like the Netherlands, coffeeshops can be found in most cities. However, apps that show nearby establishments, including opening hours and reviews, can be quite helpful. Examples include <a href="https://en.greenmeister.nl/">Greenmeister</a> and <a href="https://weeview.nl/">Weeview</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li>Greenmeister: filter by location, popularity, or product type.</li>
<li>Weeview: lists Dutch coffeeshops with user ratings.</li>
<li>Internationally: Leafly and Weedmaps show legal dispensaries in regulated countries.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Legal Note:</strong> Cannabis laws vary by country and region. In the Netherlands, sale in coffeeshops is tolerated under strict conditions. Always check local laws for current global policies.</p>

<h2>2. Strain Trackers</h2>
<p>Everyone reacts differently to cannabis. That’s why apps that help track what works for you are becoming increasingly popular.</p>
<ul>
<li>Releaf: log what you consume, the dose, and its effects.</li>
<li>Tetragram: combines usage tracking with personal notes and suggestions.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Health Claim Disclaimer:</strong> Effects like stress relief are highly individual and supported by limited scientific evidence. See this <a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6583762/">review on cannabis and mental health</a> for more context.</p>
<h2>3. Apps for Reducing or Quitting</h2>
<p>Some users wish to cut down or quit. Thankfully, supportive apps are available, such as:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="https://grounded420.com/">Grounded420</a></li>
<li><a href="https://www.samhsa.gov/find-help/national-helpline">SAMHSA - National Helpline</a></li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Medical Disclaimer:</strong> These apps are intended as support tools and <strong>do not replace professional help</strong>. If you have concerns, seek guidance from a healthcare professional.</p>

<h2>4. Social Apps</h2>
<p>For those who enjoy sharing experiences, there are social platforms like <a href="https://weedmaps.com/">Weedmaps</a> and Greenmeister, where users can post photos, reviews, and tips.</p>
<h2>5. Education and News</h2>
<p>Apps like <a href="https://www.leafly.com/">Leafly</a> offer comprehensive strain databases and user experiences. You can also follow news about legalization, medical applications, and scientific research.</p>
<h2>Disclaimer</h2>
<p>Use the mentioned apps responsibly. This article is for informational purposes only and <strong>does not promote illegal use</strong>. If your cannabis use becomes problematic, seek professional help from recognized health services.</p>

<blockquote><strong>"Many users experience that magic truffles work faster and more consistently when taken on an empty stomach."</strong></blockquote>

<h2>Why do people use truffles on an empty stomach?</h2>
<p>Many users plan their last meal several hours before taking magic truffles. A full stomach may slow down the absorption of <a href="/en/">psilocybin</a>.</p>
<p>As a result, it can take longer before the effects become noticeable. Some people therefore become uncertain about the dosage or think they have taken too little.</p>
<p>Also explore our range of <a href="/en/shop/mushrooms/magic-truffles">magic truffles</a>.</p>

<h2>Why is it better not to eat shortly before a trip?</h2>
<p>If someone eats a large meal just before a trip, it may influence the experience. Some users notice that the effects take longer to begin or feel less predictable.</p>
<blockquote><strong>"A full stomach may cause psychedelic effects to become noticeable later than expected."</strong></blockquote>
<p>As a result, some people unintentionally take additional truffles while their first dose has not yet fully taken effect.</p>
<h2>What do people eat during a truffle trip?</h2>
<p>Many people notice that they have less appetite during a psychedelic experience. Some therefore choose light foods such as fruit or a small snack.</p>
<p>A heavy meal during a trip may feel less pleasant for some people, while others hardly notice any difference. The experience varies from person to person.</p>
<h3>Why do some people choose fruit or sugary foods?</h3>
<p>Some people eat something sweet during a trip when their energy levels drop or when they start feeling light-headed.</p>
<ul>
<li>Fruit</li>
<li>Water</li>
<li>A light snack</li>
<li>Something sweet for a quick energy boost</li>
</ul>
<p>For this reason, many users keep a small snack nearby during a longer psychedelic experience.</p>

<h2>Combining alcohol and other substances with truffles</h2>
<p>Combining different substances can make the experience less predictable. According to many users, some combinations increase the risk of confusion, nausea or an unpleasant experience.</p>
<p>If you want to understand when it may be better to postpone use, read <a href="/en/">when to use or not use</a>.</p>
<p>Medication and other consciousness-altering substances can also influence a psychedelic experience. That is why many people choose to be cautious when combining substances.</p>

<h2>How important are setting and preparation?</h2>
<p>Food is not the only factor that influences how a truffle trip feels. Your mindset, surroundings and preparation also play a major role.</p>
<p>That is why many people consciously choose a calm environment where they feel safe and relaxed.</p>
<h3>According to users, what helps create a calm experience?</h3>
<ul>
<li>A familiar environment</li>
<li>Calming music</li>
<li>Plenty of water</li>
<li>A comfortable place to relax</li>
<li>A trip sitter for higher dosages</li>
</ul>

<h2>How dosage affects a truffle trip</h2>
<p>The intensity of a psychedelic experience depends not only on the type of truffle, but also on the dosage. Lower doses are often described as milder and easier to manage, while higher doses can feel much more intense.</p>
<p>Personal sensitivity also plays a role. Some people notice the effects sooner than others, even when taking the same amount of truffles.</p>
<h2>Conclusion</h2>
<p>Many people prefer to use magic truffles on an empty stomach because the effects may feel more predictable and consistent.</p>
<p>In addition to food intake, dosage, mindset, setting and preparation all play an important role in shaping a psychedelic experience.</p>

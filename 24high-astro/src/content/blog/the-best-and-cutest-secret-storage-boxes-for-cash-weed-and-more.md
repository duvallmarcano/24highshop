<h3>What the law says about drug possession</h3>
<p>The Netherlands has a tolerance policy for soft drugs, but possession of hard drugs remains punishable. If you are caught with a so-called user quantity, you will not be prosecuted for drug possession. That user quantity is probably less than you think, though. The Dutch <a href="https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0036356/2015-03-01">law</a> allows you to have this in your pocket:</p>
<ul>
<li>Hard drugs: a 'small quantity for personal use'. This amounts to a single XTC pill, half a gram of powder (such as speed, ketamine or MDMA) or up to 5 grams of GHB.</li>
<li>Soft drugs: less than 5 grams of weed or hash.<br/><br/></li>
</ul>

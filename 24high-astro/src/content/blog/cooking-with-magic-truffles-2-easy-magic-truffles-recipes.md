<h2>Food and truffles: can you combine?</h2>
<p>Before you start cooking elaborately with magic truffles, it is good to know that it is not recommended to eat heavily before tripping. In fact, it is best to trip on an <a href="/en/">almost empty stomach</a>. This way, the truffles work better, and you have much less chance of nausea.</p>
<p>Make sure you don't eat anything in the hours before the trip. Also avoid heavy meals earlier in the day. Lightly digestible, in other words. All the recipes with magic truffles we provide in this blog meet this criterion. They are light enough not to cause a fight with your stomach, but tasty enough to mask the taste of truffles.<br/><br/></p>

<h3>Recipe: Magic truffle Long Island Ice Tea</h3>
<p>The Long Island Ice Tea cocktail is known for making you pretty tipsy very quickly. Not surprising when you look at the ingredients.... Vodka, rum, gin, tequila, triple sec; no mean feat! Ironically, there is not even tea in this cocktail. The name refers purely to its colour. The mocktail version is a whole lot healthier and is anything but heavy on the stomach. It contains cola, lime or lemon juice and tea - all ingredients that can actually help with gastrointestinal problems.</p>
<p>For this recipe, follow these steps:</p>
<ul>
<li>Mix a portion of magic truffles with lemonade in the blender. Choose sugar-free lemonade with lemon flavour or iced tea concentrate, for example. Blend until it has become an even substance.</li>
<li>Add extra lime or lemon as desired, in the form of juice or slices of fresh fruit.</li>
<li>Finish with a splash of cola or Diet Coke and a heap of ice cubes.</li>
<li>You can mask the truffle flavour even better by adding cooled strong tea. Black tea is a good one as it is one of the strongest and most bitter teas.</li>
<li>Does this recipe still taste too much like magic truffles to you? Then add some extra lemonade syrup and/or fresh ginger and mint leaves.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Recipe: truffle chocolate</h3>
<p>This is the easiest magic truffle recipe you will find. All you need is truffles and a chocolate bar. Follow these steps to make magic truffle chocolates:</p>
<ul>
<li>After weighing, blend/mix/grind the truffles into as small a substance as possible. Preferably, it will be a powder so that it disappears easily into the recipe.</li>
<li>Melt the chocolate bar Au bain-marie. This goes like this: put a pan of hot water on the stove. Put a glass or metal bowl in this, containing the chocolate in pieces. This will allow the chocolate to melt slowly without burning.</li>
<li>Add the magic truffles to the melted chocolate and stir well.</li>
<li>Divide the melted chocolate mix over, for example, an ice cube tray and make sure all chocolates are the same size.</li>
<li>Store in the freezer. And again: label this batch so you know how strong each portion is.</li>
</ul>

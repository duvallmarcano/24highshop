<p><strong>Psilocybin truffles</strong> are legal psychedelic products in the Netherlands that contain the active compound psilocybin. This substance is converted in the body into psilocin and causes temporary changes in perception, emotions, and consciousness. In this complete guide, you will read exactly what truffles are, how they work, what effects you can expect, how dosing works, and what the risks are.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Psilocybin truffles are underground sclerotia of a fungus that contain psilocybin. The effects begin after 20–60 minutes, last on average 3–8 hours, and depend on dosage, mindset, and environment.</p>
</blockquote>
<h2>What is psilocybin?</h2>
<p>Psilocybin is a naturally occurring psychedelic substance. In the body, this substance is converted into <strong>psilocin</strong>, which affects the serotonin 5-HT2A receptors in the brain<a href="/en/">[1]</a>. This temporarily changes communication between brain regions.</p>
<p>This can lead to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Altered perception of color and shape</li>
<li>Deeper emotional experiences</li>
<li>Changed sense of time</li>
<li>Introspective thoughts</li>
</ul>
<h2>What are psilocybin truffles?</h2>
<p>Psilocybin truffles are the underground storage structures (sclerotia) of a fungus. Unlike mushrooms, they grow underground as compact masses of mycelium.</p>

<h2>Why do people use psilocybin truffles?</h2>
<p>People use psilocybin truffles for various reasons. The experience can be introspective and is often associated with reflection, creativity, and breaking habitual thought patterns. What someone experiences differs from person to person and strongly depends on set &amp; setting, dosage, and intention.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>The reason someone uses truffles is personal, but the experience is almost always strongly influenced by set (mindset) and setting (environment).</p>
</blockquote>
<h2>Effects of psilocybin truffles</h2>
<h3>1. Psychological effects</h3>
<ul>
<li>More intense colors and visual patterns</li>
<li>Sense of connectedness</li>
<li>Deep insights</li>
<li>Ego dissolution at higher dosages</li>
</ul>
<h3>2. Physical effects</h3>
<ul>
<li>Slight increase in heart rate<a href="/en/">[2]</a></li>
<li>Dilated pupils</li>
<li>Mild nausea at onset<strong></strong></li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Important:</strong> The experience is strongly influenced by your mindset (set) and your environment (setting).</p>
<h2>Set &amp; Setting: why environment and mindset are crucial</h2>
<p>With psilocybin truffles, <strong>set</strong> (your mental state) and <strong>setting</strong> (your environment) play a major role in how the experience unfolds. Two people with the same dosage can have completely different experiences.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Set:</strong> your mood, expectations, intention, and mental stability</li>
<li><strong>Setting:</strong> the physical environment, people around you, music, light, and comfort</li>
</ul>
<p>A calm, safe, and familiar environment reduces the likelihood of a difficult experience. Stress, conflicts, or an unsafe environment can contribute to anxiety or confusion during a trip.</p>
<h2>Dosage of fresh truffles</h2>
<h3>Indication for adults</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Light:</strong> 5–7 grams</li>
<li><strong>Moderate:</strong> 7–15 grams</li>
<li><strong>Strong:</strong> 15–22 grams</li>
</ul>
<p>Are you a beginner? Start low.</p>
<h3>What do light, moderate, or strong mean in practice?</h3>
<p>A light dosage can produce subtle changes in perception, such as more vivid colors and a mildly euphoric feeling. Many people experience this as accessible and manageable.</p>
<p>A moderate dosage usually leads to clear visual effects, altered thought patterns, and deeper emotional experiences.</p>
<p>A strong dosage can result in ego dissolution, more intense visual distortions, and a profoundly altered sense of reality. This requires experience, preparation, and a safe setting.</p>

<h2>Phases of a trip</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Come-up (0–60 min):</strong> physical sensations, mild tension</li>
<li><strong>Peak (1–3 hours):</strong> more intense visual and emotional effects</li>
<li><strong>Comedown (3–6 hours):</strong> gradual decrease, reflective phase</li>
</ul>
<h3>Integration: what do you do after the experience?</h3>
<p>The period after a psychedelic experience is often underestimated. Integration means taking time to process insights and apply them in daily life.</p>
<p>This can be done through:</p>
<ul>
<li>Writing or journaling</li>
<li>Reflective conversations</li>
<li>Meditation</li>
<li>Adequate rest</li>
</ul>
<p>Integration helps process the experience constructively and prevents insights from being lost.</p>
<h2>Safety and risks</h2>
<ul>
<li>Do not combine with alcohol or other drugs</li>
<li>Do not use in cases of psychotic or bipolar vulnerability</li>
<li>Do not use during pregnancy</li>
<li>Avoid use with antidepressants (SSRIs) without medical advice</li>
<li>Preferably use with a tripsitter</li>
<li>Do not participate in traffic</li>
</ul>
<h3>When is use discouraged?</h3>
<p>Use of psilocybin truffles is discouraged in cases of:</p>
<ul>
<li>A history of psychosis or schizophrenia</li>
<li>Bipolar disorder</li>
<li>Severe anxiety disorders without supervision</li>
<li>Pregnancy or breastfeeding</li>
<li>Use of certain medications such as SSRIs or MAO inhibitors</li>
</ul>
<p>Unsure about your situation? Always consult a doctor or professional before using psychedelic substances<a href="/en/">[3]</a>.</p>

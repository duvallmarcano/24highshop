<p>Wild lettuce (Wild lettuce or Lactuca Virosa) is a lettuce that secretes a whitish, pale milk. People use this substance because of the effect it can have on the central nervous system. Put 1 to 2 teaspoons of wild lettuce in a cup of boiled water and let it steep for 10 to 15 minutes. For a good night's sleep, take this before going to sleep.</p>

<p>Dream herb, also known as <a href="/en/shop/smartshop/dream-herbs">Calea zacatechichi</a>, is native to Mexico and Central America. The native people used the herb to get clairvoyant dreams. Hence the name, it is said that your dreams can become more intense and lucid. Taking Dream Herb during the day can make you feel sluggish and relaxed. Dream herb is suitable for making tea. To do this, pour half a liter of hot water over 3 to 25 grams of dried leaves. Let this steep for 15 minutes.

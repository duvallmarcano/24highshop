<h2>What is ginseng and where does it come from?</h2>
<p>In appearance, it resembles ginger, but with lots of thin branching roots. There are different types of ginseng, each with their roots in different parts of the world. Ginseng itself, by the way, is also a root, which is ground into powder or, like ginger for example, used pure. The most common types are Korean or Chinese ginseng and American ginseng. Eastern ginseng is also called Panax Ginseng. Panax is Latin for all-healer, 'gin' means 'man' and 'seng' can be translated as 'essence'. So you can conclude that this plant has a good reputation.</p>
<p>You might also have heard of Siberian ginseng. This is secretly not real ginseng; it belongs to a different plant species and does not contain the same substances.<br/><br/></p>

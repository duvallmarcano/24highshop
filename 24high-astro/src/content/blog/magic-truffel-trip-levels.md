<p>Every trip is different, with <a href="/en/">magic truffles</a> or magic mushrooms. Sometimes it is more intense than other times, and that has to do with the type of truffle you choose and the amount you consume. To indicate how deep you are in your trip, there are different truffle and mushroom trip levels that you can reach. This ranges from mild trips to severe trips and is divided into levels from 1 to 5. A mild trip is level 1 and level 5 is a severe trip. <br/><br/></p>
<h2>What psychedelic truffle or mushroom trip levels/dimensions are there?</h2>

<p>When you talk about trip level, we are talking about dimensions. Entering the first dimension will pass easily with a magic mushroom or truffle and will feel like a relaxed and happy high. As you get deeper into the trip (dose related of course) you will also transfer to a higher dimension. Each dimension has its own properties or effects. The amount of a dose of psychedelic truffle or <a href="/en/shop/mushrooms/growkits-mushroom">shrooms</a> is linked to the trip level or dimensions you want to discover.</p>
<h3>Truffle Trip Level 1</h3>
<ul>
<li>You feel completely relaxed and nice and high. You are cheerful, sociable, more social and you feel good. Colors and sounds are more intense. You have a mild trip that may give some visual effects.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Truffle Trip Level 2</h3>
<ul>
<li>New ideas, creativity and your focus increases. Also a level higher in experiencing colors and sounds. Your senses are affected. The visual effects are greater and even with your eyes closed you can see <a href="/en/">multi-dimensional patterns</a>. Objects around you may start to move.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Truffle Trip Level 3</h3>
<ul>
<li>Do you see three-dimensional patterns with your eyes closed? Then you are on truffle trip level 3. The visual effects come undulating towards you, one moment more intense than the next. The effect of time disappears and mild hallucinations may occur.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Truffle Trip Level 4</h3>
<ul>
<li>The world around you is distorted and your senses become one. You may be able to see sounds. An <a href="/en/">out of body experience</a> can occur at level 4. While tripping you lose sight of reality and time seems to no longer play a role. In any case, you have intense hallucinations at this level.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Truffle Trip Level 5</h3>
<ul>
<li>You feel enlightened. Everything around you melts together. Not only with each other, but also with yourself. Your ego disappears like snow in the sun and the world no longer functions the way you used to. Everything is one. Communicating becomes more challenging, and you lose touch with reality.<br/><br/></li>
</ul>

<h2>Amount of magic mushroom or truffle dose linked to the trip level</h2>
<p>Psychedelic mushrooms and truffles can be consumed fresh immediately. The amount you take determines your trip level. Are you from which dimension you want to visit? Then it is important to stick to the schedule below. It is possible that the dose we indicate is a bit too heavy for a light person (less than 70 kilos). We assume for a moment an adult person who weighs 80 kilos. If you weigh about 10 kilos or more less, subtract 5 grams from the dose for fresh mushrooms and 0.5 grams for dry mushrooms. Always use a scale because you want to know exactly what your dose is. As for the truffle, see below:</p>
<h3>Dosage mushroom trip level</h3>
<p>A psychedelic magic mushroom dosage trip level (Dimension) 1 is: 1 to 1.5 grams of dried or 10 to 15 grams of fresh magic mushrooms.<br/>A psychedelic magic mushroom dosage trip level (Dimension) 2 is: 1.5 to 2 grams dried or 15 to 20 grams of fresh magic mushrooms.<br/>A psychedelic magic mushroom dosage trip level (Dimension) 3 is: 2 to 3 grams dried or 20 to 30 grams fresh magic mushrooms.<br/>A psychedelic magic mushroom dosage trip level (Dimension) 4 is: 3 to 4 grams dried or 30 to 40 grams fresh magic mushrooms.<br/>A psychedelic magic mushroom dosage trip level (Dimension) 5 is: 4 to 5 grams dried or 40 to 50 grams fresh magic mushrooms.</p>
<h3>Dosage of truffle trip level</h3>
<p>A psychedelic fresh truffle dose trip level (Dimension) 1 is: 10 grams<br/>A psychedelic fresh truffle dose trip level (Dimension) 2 is: 13 grams<br/>A psychedelic fresh truffle dose trip level (Dimension) 3 is: 15 grams <br/>A psychedelic fresh truffle dosage trip level (Dimension) 4 is: 18 grams<br/>A psychedelic fresh truffle dosage trip level (Dimension) 5 is: 22 grams</p>

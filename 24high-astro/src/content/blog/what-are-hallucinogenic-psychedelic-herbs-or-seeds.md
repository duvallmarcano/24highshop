<h2>What are hallucinogens?</h2>
<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallucinogen">Hallucinogens</a> are substances in a particular substance that cause hallucinations. The word is derived from the Latin word hallucinere, meaning <em>"wandering in the mind"</em>. Drugs with hallucinogenic effects are also called psychedelic or mind-altering drugs. These include drugs such as LSD, LSA, mescaline, psilocybin, Dimethyltryptamine, ketamine and ibogaine. But there are also several herbs that have hallucinogenic effects.<br/><br/></p>

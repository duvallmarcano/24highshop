<p>Guarana comes from the tropical rainforest (the Amazon). It is a climbing plant and the seeds look a bit like our chestnuts. <p>Guarana gives your energy a powerful boost. It feels different from coffee because it has a more balanced effect. So you won't bounce, but just feel happy and energized. Guarana also acts as an antidepressant. You can make a tea from Guarana, but you can also take it as a capsule.<br/></p>

<p>Kratom is a stimulant and Thai kratom is especially popular. The tree grows in tropical areas such as Thailand, Myanmar, Papua New Guinea, Malaysia and Malaysia and Indonesia. <p>Cafe and theobromine are substances that give a person energy again. Theobromine is an alkalinity and increases alertness. This helps you to concentrate better, so you get more work done. <p>Ephedra is suitable for people who want a big boost in energy. The plant contains the substance ephedrine, which has a similar effect to caffeine. However, ephedrine is more powerful. <p>But be careful when using ephedra. Do not take too much ephedra. If you take too much, you can experience palpitations, agitation, insomnia and a shaky feeling.

<h3>Work sterile</h3>
<p>If you want to make sure your mushroom harvest is successful, work in a super clean environment. Wash your hands thoroughly and wear gloves before working with the cultivation kit, keep pets, food and bacterial sources away as much as possible. Why is this important? Mushrooms are very susceptible to contamination by unwanted invisible guests. This is because they grow on a so-called substrate, which the mushrooms feed on. That substrate is very susceptible to infection, partly because mushrooms have to grow in a fairly warm environment. And of course, that substrate is not just a tasty meal for your mushrooms. Want to know the best way to work sterile? Read all about it here: <a href="/en/">How do you work sterile when growing mushrooms? </a></p>

<p>With one of our shishas, you can have a great smoking experience at home, but actually it is even more fun to go to a dedicated bar. Also called a shisha lounge, this is simply a bar where you can enjoy a bong. In this article, you will find the best shisha bars in the world!</p>
<h2>What is shisha?</h2>
<p>Shisha is another word for water pipe. This is a device that allows you to smoke flavoured <a href="/en/">tobacco</a>, also known as shisha tobacco or molasses. The smoke is passed through water before being inhaled, which cools the smoke and makes it softer. Although you can smoke shisha only in your own home, it is more common to do so with friends, for example. Bongs have their origins in the Middle East, but now it has become popular worldwide. Shisha does have some health risks, which you should consider:</p>
<ul>
<li>Respiratory problems</li>
<li>Increased risk of lung diseases</li>
<li>Increased risk of cancer</li>
<li>Increased risk of cardiovascular disease</li>
<li>Nicotine addiction</li>
</ul>
<p>This is why experts advise you not to smoke shisha too often. Do it in moderation.</p>

<h2>What is a shisha bar?</h2>
<p>So shisha is usually smoked in groups. Meanwhile, special shisha bars have sprung up all over the world for this. In these catering establishments, you can enjoy a hookah together with your friends, for instance, in a relaxed atmosphere. You can also sometimes order snacks and drinks. In many cities today, the shisha bar is a popular meeting place for an evening out.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>The 5 best shisha bars in the world</h2>
<p>Thinking about going on holiday? You may then choose to go to a destination where you will find a nice shisha lounge. Luckily, you have a lot of choice in this regard, as the best shisha bars are distributed all over the world, according to experts!</p>
<h3>1. Mazazik in Amsterdam, the Netherlands</h3>
<p>We start in the capital of the Netherlands, because in Amsterdam you will find a great shisha lounge called Mazazik. Here, you can enjoy various water pipes and also order snacks and drinks. Mazazik is a good combination of the old-fashioned tradition of shisha smoking and the modern vibes of the Dutch capital.</p>
<h3>2. Harab'be Cafe in Istanbul, Turkey</h3>
<p>Harab'be Cafe, located in the trendy Karakoy district, is one of the best places to enjoy bong in Istanbul. The cafe has a cosy and inviting atmosphere, making it a popular spot for locals and tourists alike. The menu offers a variety of flavours, including apple, mint and grape, so there is always a shisha flavour you like.</p>
<h3>3. Alarabi in Berlin, Germany</h3>
<p>You can also find several great shisha lounges in Germany, but Alarabi is the best of them all, according to most visitors. This catering establishment is actually a restaurant, but so you can use a variety of hookahs here. They are not too expensive and you can choose from ten different flavours. Getting hungry? So Alarabi makes delicious food too!<br/><br/></p>
<h3>4. Kazuza Lounge in New York, the United States</h3>
<p>Kazuza Lounge describes their bongs on their own site as one of the best in the world. Visitors certainly agree with them, which is why this spot deserves a spot on our list of the best shisha bars in the world. Kazuza Lounge is beautifully decorated and offers all kinds of tasty drinks in addition to shisha. Unfortunately, this is not the cheapest shisha lounge, you have to take this into account.</p>
<h3>5. MY Hookah Room in Madrid, Spain</h3>
<p>MY Hookah Room is recommended by visitors as the best place in Madrid for bongs . This is partly because of the service and how the shishas are served. This place is also known for tasty cocktails. You can go to MY Hookah Room for more than just shisha, in other words!</p>

<h2>What is Yopo?</h2>
<p>Yopo is the well-known name for the large green plant called <a href="https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Anadenanthera_peregrina">Anadenanthera peregrina</a>. This refers to the word 'Ananda' from Sanskrit, meaning bliss. The seeds of this plant contain several psychoactive substances, including: DMT, bufotenine and 5-Meo-DMT. The fruits and bark of the plant also contain psychoactive substances, such as catechol, bufotenine, alpha-methyltryptamine and sarapontenine.</p>
<h3>History</h3>
<p>The seeds of the Yopo plant are best known in Central America. Early on, the seeds were also widely used by Indigenous peoples from Colombia, Venezuela and the southern part of the Brazilian Amazon. They made yopo snuff from these for spiritual healing. In the Caribbean, yopo used to be widely used in ceremonial contexts.<br/><br/></p>

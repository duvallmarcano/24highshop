<h2>What are magic truffles?</h2>
<p>Just in short, in case you're not familiar with <a href="/en/shop/mushrooms/magic-truffles">magic truffles,</a> we explain what magic truffles are. Magic truffles are part of the underground network (mycelium) that also grows magic mushrooms. A truffle is formed by the mycelium as an emergency ration storage. This ration is used when the mushrooms want to emerge, but the climate is not favourable. The mushroom will draw its nutrients from this tuber, the truffle. As a result, they contain psychoactive substances just like magic mushrooms. These psychoactive substances, psilocybin and psilocin, are substances that have provided interesting research for years.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>What is the Chaga mushroom?</h2>
<p>The Chaga mushroom is officially known as <em>Inonotus obliquus</em> and mainly grows on birch trees in cold climates. It is also known as the birch conk and is often referred to as the <strong>Mushroom of Immortality</strong>. On the outside, Chaga looks like a dark, almost charred fungus, while the inside has a warm orange colour.</p>
<p>Chaga is one of the most popular <a href="/en/shop/mushrooms/medicinal-mushrooms">medicinal mushrooms</a>, alongside Lion's Mane, Reishi, Cordyceps, Turkey Tail and Maitake.</p>
<p>The mushroom has been used for a long time in traditional practices and is now mainly sold as powder, extract or capsules.</p>

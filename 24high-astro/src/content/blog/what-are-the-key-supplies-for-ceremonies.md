<p>A ceremony is more than a ritual; it is a conscious journey towards connecting with yourself, nature and the spiritual. To enhance this experience, the right supplies are essential. In this article, you will discover which materials you can use to shape your ceremony - from cacao and herbs to smoking materials and accessories - and how to use them in a safe and meaningful way.</p>
<h2>Which supplies will enhance your ceremony?</h2>
<h3>1. Cacao: not just for making chocolate</h3>
<p><a href="/en/shop/smartshop/ceremonial-cacao">Ceremonial cacao</a> is pure, unsweetened cacao with a high energetic and spiritual value. Cacao is naturally a plant-based remedy, and it can therefore potentially help you get in touch with the earth's natural resources. You can drink the cacao during ceremonies. With a hot cocoa or cacao drink, you may be able to open your heart, promote emotional release and also <a href="https://search.proquest.com/openview/1f8173259c950422d5577907ace92132/1">deepen</a> the meditative process . Another option is to burn the ceremonial cacao so that the fragrance is diffused. According to people who believe in the spiritual power of cocoa, it can be used for the following spiritual journeys and ceremonies:</p>
<ul>
<li>Meditation</li>
<li>Spiritual cleansing</li>
<li>Connection with nature</li>
<li>Opening your heart</li>
<li>Manifestation</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. Herbs play an important role in ceremonies</h3>
<p>You can also <a href="/en/shop/smartshop/ceremonial-herbs">use herbs for ceremonies</a>. For this, plants with psychoactive properties are sometimes used, such as <a href="/en/">ayahuasca</a>. These substances can have strong mind-altering effects and lead to altered states of consciousness. Their use is regulated or banned in many countries. With it, you can potentially achieve a deep trance and spiritual experience. In this process, the ceremonial herbs are drunk as tea. This can lead to prolonged and profound experiences. The use carries risks and is not suitable for everyone. Some traditions use the following plants during rituals.</p>
<ul>
<li>Royal Rapé<br/>Royal Rapé and other herbs are used ritually in certain cultural contexts. They can have strong effects on the body and mind. Using them requires care and is not without risk.</li>
<li><a href="https://delamazonas.com/en/rainforest-plants/ayahuasca/">Chacruna</a></li>
<li>Jurema Coarse</li>
<li>Banisteriopsis Caapi</li>
</ul>
<p>Please note that these remedies have psychoactive effects and may pose health risks. Inform yourself well and if in doubt, consult a doctor or expert.</p>

<h3>3. Smells indispensable in spiritual ceremonies</h3>
<p>Scents can play a powerful role in rituals, according to experience experts. You can use  smokables for this during your ceremony. Fragrance allows you to come to yourself during the spiritual ceremony, for which you can use incense sticks, among other things. Herbal combinations can also be smoked so that they release a fragrance. Still, most people use incense, as you can create atmosphere and purification with it. This can be essential for energetic safety and focus, which is important in spiritual ceremonies.</p>
<h3>4. Accessories and incense ceremonies</h3>
<p>Chances are, you will also need various <a href="/en/shop/smartshop/ceremonial-accessories">accessories for your ceremony</a>. Incense burners may be needed, for example, as may stirrers. These allow you to mix cocoa before the ceremony. A pair of instruments is also handy to have in some cases, such as the Shipibo maraca rattle. An <a href="https://books.google.com/books">abalone shell</a> as a tray and carrier is also used by some people during ceremonies. With these supplies for ceremonies, you can support ritual acts. They can also complete the atmosphere.</p>
<h3>5. Tea</h3>
<p>You can also buy organic teas for ceremonies on our website. They can be used to prepare for your spiritual journey. This is because the tea can calm your mind and also cleanse your body. The tea can also be useful for connection, the hot drink possibly offering a gentle transition to deeper states of consciousness. You have several herbal teas to choose from, so choose the type that best suits your intention and ceremony.</p>

<li><strong>Prepare the ceremony:</strong> Buy all supplies for your ceremony, such as cacao, incense and tea.<br/><br/></li>

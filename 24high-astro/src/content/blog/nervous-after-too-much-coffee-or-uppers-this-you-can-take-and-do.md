<p>Theanine is highly recommended for those who get nervous easily from coffee or caffeine. This is because this substance is able to counteract the nervous effect of coffee. Theanine is found naturally in tea - which is why tea is considered more calming than coffee. Even though, tea secretly contains quite a bit of caffeine. <p>Another sedative worth trying is passionflower. Passion flower is also called passiflora or passion flower, and you can find it in various calming products such like: <a href="/en/product/9109428-relaxmix-1x-50-gram">Relaxmix</a>. Make a tea of it or go for capsules with passion flower.</p>
<p>Passion flower helps increase GABA, which results in a calming effect on the nervous system. Many studies, meanwhile, have shown that passionflower can be effective in reducing stress and anxiety. Passion flower, however, is a lot healthier, as it is less addictive and makes you less dull.</p>

<h2>Exercise and meditate after too much caffeine: get rid of anxiety without drugs</h2>
<p>Even if they are not physical substances; walking, <a href="/en/">balancing body and mind with yoga</a>, meditation and relaxation exercises can definitely help you when you are overexcited from too many uppers. All these interventions are known to calm you down from agitation, panic and depressive symptoms. If you have consumed too much caffeine, then exercise can be a good outlet. First, because it is a good distraction, and second, because it allows you to at least put that caffeine and other uppers to work. That way, with a workout, you use up that excess energy. Pay attention to your heart rate though and don't do a sudden intense gym session. A short jog at a healthy pace can work well to clear your head and get the restlessness out of your body.</p>
<h3>Eat</h3>
<p>Another thing that can greatly help you feel less nervous: eat a good meal. Go for a filling, healthy meal. And don't forget your 'comfort foods'! Think of your favourite biscuits, chocolate or another snack that makes you happy. Be careful with too much sugar, as that can also make you nervous.<br/><br/></p>

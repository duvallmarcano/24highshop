<p>The careful application of microdosing with psilocybin-containing truffles is increasingly being studied for its potential effects on mental clarity, creativity, and emotional well-being. <h2>What is Microdosing with Magic Truffles?</h2>
<p>Magic truffles contain the psychoactive compound <em>psilocybin</em>. In a full dose, this substance can induce a psychedelic experience. With  microdosing , only a very small amount is taken, which does not lead to hallucinations but may result in subtle changes in mood, focus, or creative thinking. It’s important to note that effects can vary from person to person, and scientific research is still ongoing.</p>
<h2>How to Use Microdosing Truffles</h2>
<p>Microdosing requires precise dosing. You can weigh the truffles yourself or choose standardized products such as Microdosing XP, which are pre-measured for convenience and consistency. The recommended usage period is typically one to two months, preferably following a fixed schedule. Take the microdose preferably in the morning on an empty stomach for optimal absorption.</p>
<h3>Examples of Microdosing Schedules</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>1-2 schedule:</strong> Two consecutive days of dosing followed by two rest days.</li>
<li><strong>1-3 schedule:</strong> One day of dosing followed by three rest days, then repeat.</li>
<li><strong>Every-other-day schedule:</strong> Alternating dosing and rest days.</li>
<li><strong>Two-days-a-week schedule:</strong> Two fixed days per week of dosing with at least one rest day in between.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Stamets Stacking: Combining Lion’s Mane with Microdosing</h2>
<p>American mycologist Paul Stamets developed a protocol that combines microdosing with psilocybin, <a href="/en/product/1202417-b-vitamin-complex-together">vitamin B3</a> (niacin), and <a href="/en/product/1203592-lions-mane-hericium-erinaceus-brain-activity">Lion’s Mane capsules</a>. According to Stamets, this combination may support neuroplasticity—the brain’s ability to form new neural connections. Although his findings are mostly anecdotal, the protocol is widely followed by users around the world.</p>
<p>The recommended cycle is: four days of dosing with psilocybin, niacin, and Lion’s Mane, followed by three rest days. Lion’s Mane can be continued during rest days. Maintain this cycle for a maximum of one month, then take a break of two to four weeks.</p>
<h2>Why Combine Lion’s Mane with Microdosing?</h2>
<p>Lion’s Mane is a mushroom frequently mentioned in relation to cognitive support. Users appreciate the combination with truffle microdosing for its possible benefits on memory, creativity, and problem-solving. Research into its exact effects is still ongoing; side effects are rare. If you use medication or have health conditions, consult a medical professional first.</p>

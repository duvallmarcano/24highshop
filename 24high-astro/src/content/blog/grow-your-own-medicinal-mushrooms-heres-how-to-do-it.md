<p>Have you ever thought about growing medicinal mushrooms all by yourself? You hear more and more about it. Growing your own is not always easy, but with a prepared strain or special growing kit, you can make it easier on yourself. From oyster mushrooms to Lion's Mane, if you provide the right conditions, you can grow any mushroom yourself.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>What do you need to grow your own medicinal mushrooms?</h2>
<p>When it comes to mushrooms, it is good to know that what you eat from a mushroom is its fruiting body. The fungus grows mainly underground in the form of a fungal network (the mycelium). With this, it gets its nutrients from the soil. From this mycelium, the mushrooms form, which are fruiting bodies. These will then spread the spores.</p>
<p>If you want to grow your own mushrooms, it is important to ensure the right conditions. The advantage is that they can grow on different types of material, which are also very durable. Examples are straw, compost, coffee grounds, dead wood or manure. Mushrooms are the food of the future because they need very little water, space and energy to grow. And they are also highly <a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4244211/">nutritious</a>:<br/><br/><em>Mushrooms naturally contain a wide range of nutrients. For instance, many species are rich in fibre, B vitamins (such as niacin and riboflavin), minerals such as potassium and selenium, and contain plant proteins. According to scientific literature, certain mushrooms, such as Lion's Mane and Reishi, may contain substances that have been investigated in laboratory studies for possible anti-inflammatory or antioxidant properties [PMC].</em></p>
<p>So how can you start growing medicinal mushrooms yourself? The easiest way is to buy a cultivation kit. This contains the spores already dispersed (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mushroom_spawn">mushroom spawn</a>) and the growth medium. This is where the mushrooms can grow. But you can also get started in the garden or on the balcony. Prepare plant beds or pots for your mushrooms to grow in. Another special option is to use a chopped-off tree trunk as a growing medium. Into this you can insert plugs prepared with mushroom spawn.</p>

<h2>How do magic mushrooms grow, and what are spores? </h2>
<p>There are countless Psychedelic Magic Mushroom species, they grow in the wild in nature and have further developed and spread over millions of years. While cannabis and most plants do this with seeds, magic mushrooms do this with 'spores'. Experienced Mushroom growers also work with these spores to develop new grow kits. Spores are single-celled bodies with which the Magic Mushroom reproduces itself. These spores fall from the mushroom's hat, around the mushroom, creating a new generation of magic mushrooms. These <a href="/en/shop/mushrooms/spores-shrooms">spores</a> transform into mycelium and from it grow new mushrooms.<br/><br/></p>

<h3>Instructions and explanations for growing magic mushrooms with a growkit</h3>
<p>First of all, it is very important that you wash your hands (and forearms) well before taking the steps. I also recommend cleaning the grow kit itself with a clean cloth.</p>
<ul>
<li>I carefully took the lid off and kept it. I will need this again shortly after. Poured lukewarm water into the grow kit until it was full and put the lid back on. I let this one soak for 1/2 hour.<br/><br/></li>
<li>After that, I opened the grow kit and carefully let all the excess water flow out.<br/><br/></li>
<li>I then filled the Grow Bag (the large plastic bag) with 250ml of lukewarm water and carefully placed the grow kit (with the lid off) in the Grow Bag. Make sure that the water in the Grow Bag does not go over the edge of the grow kit/grow kit. This layer of water in the Grow Bag is for high humidity, because mushrooms love this very much.<br/><br/></li>
<li>It is important that you leave the Grow Bag closed (by folding it at the top and closing it with the supplied paper clips) until you see the first Mushrooms growing.<br/><br/></li>
<li>You then place the Grow Bag with the grow kit in it in a place that does not receive direct sunlight, but where there is light. The ideal temperature for this room is 22 to 25 ºC. After 2-3 weeks, magic mushrooms will grow out of the grow kit. I started growing on Thursday 21 May and on 1 June I already saw the first Mushrooms emerge.<br/><br/></li>
<li>When the first Mushrooms appear, it is important to always keep the inside of the Grow Bag moist with a plant spray (1 or 2 sprays at a time). Please note: keep the mushrooms dry yourself, so do not spray on the grow kit! We also open the Grow Bag to spray with the plant sprayer to refresh the air again. Always do the above with your gloves on and your mouth mask on, just like I do. Magic mushrooms are, again, very sensitive to bacteria and fungi!<br/><br/></li>

<h2>What is copaiba?</h2>
<p>Copaiba comes from a species of tree found in South America: the Copaifera. Copaiba is tapped from Copaifera trees by making a hole in the bark. A yellowish-brown goo then comes out of this, from which an essential oil is made. Like the <a href="/en/product/3203467-copaiba-10-ml">Copaiba oil</a> in our webshop. And in that South American wilderness, indigenous people discovered the positive effects of the products to be extracted from it 400 (!) years ago. Such local discoveries in the jungle regularly find their way to the West and to our shop. Think, for example, of <a href="/en/">Mulungu</a>, <a href="/en/">Guayusa</a> and <a href="/en/">Chacruna</a>.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>What are the effects of Copaiba according to science?</h2>
<p>The fact that indigenous people have been fans of a particular natural product for centuries obviously says something good. But before you start using it yourself, it is nice to know exactly what the effects are. And how healthy Copaiba is for you, of course. Fortunately, science offers some answers. Indeed, several researchers have looked into the compounds contained in Copaiba and what effects it can have in a human body.<br/><br/>A <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/327866064_Meta-analysis_on_Copaiba_Oil_Its_Functions_in_Metabolism_and_Its_Properties_as_an_Anti-inflammatory_Agent">2018 overview study</a> collected almost 50 studies and compiled the results. Their conclusion about this remedy: "Promising"! The study states that Copaiba oil is an interesting alternative remedy for chronic inflammation, infections and autoimmune disorders. It may also contribute to better absorption of medication. Copaiba consists of a fairly large proportion of the compound Beta-caryophyllene. In fact, it is the essential oil with the highest known concentration of this. And that is one with potency! Beta-caryophyllene, for instance, counteracts inflammation, is an antioxidant and fights bacteria. Further effects mentioned include faster healing of wounds and reduction of pain.</p>

<p>Mix 2 to 4 drops of oil with your regular skincare product (such as a toner) and spread evenly over your skin. Try it the first time on a small area of skin, such as your forearm. That way, you can be almost certain you won't have an allergic reaction on a more sensitive area like your face.</p>

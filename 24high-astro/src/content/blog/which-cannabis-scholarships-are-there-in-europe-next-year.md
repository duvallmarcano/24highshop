<p>Visiting a cannabis fair is the way to keep up to date with the latest trends in cannabis and its use. At a cannabis fair you will find new products, you can network, get ideas, go to lectures or workshops and enjoy everything that the beautiful cannabis plant has to offer. Due to corona most of the fairs have been canceled this year, but next year it will many interesting things on the program. These fairs are sometimes only for professionals, but there are also fairs that you can visit as a cannabis enthusiast. View the overview below and put the dates in your agenda! Please note that this schedule is subject to change due to events surrounding corona<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Cannabis fairs in Europe</h2>

<h3><br/>CannaTrade International Cannabis Expo</h3>
<p>When: May 28-30, 2021 <br/>Where: Bern, Switzerland <br/><br/>CannaTrade has been held since 2001, making it the longest-running hemp festival in Europe. You will find more than 250 exhibitors here, where you can view and buy a wide variety of cannabis and <a href="/en/shop/cbdshop/cbd-vitamins">CBD</a> products. With an area of ​​12,000 square meters, there is plenty to see. Want to chill? Relax in the Chillout Area. When you get hungry, you can shop around and get a snack at the <a href="/en/shop/headshop/cannabis-edibles">Hemp Food</a> Festival. Make business contacts in the Business Lounge. The <a href="/en/shop/headshop/smoking-accessories">Joint Roll</a> Contest is famous in Switzerland. Do you think you are good at rolling a joint? See if you are the fastest or make the most beautiful! The winner received 100 grams in gold last year! <br/><br/></p>

<h3>The CBD Show</h3>
<p> When:  1,2 and 3 May 2021 <br/> Where:  London <br/><br/>In London next year, the CBD Show will be held again in the Olympia. 100 international merchants and 50 speakers gather here. Everyone can go here, but the first two days are only for people who are involved in CBD business. There are retailers, distributors, wholesalers, as well as pharmacists and e-commerce traders. Furthermore, policy experts, government employees and council members come to this large-scale networking event. CBD is popular, and you can tell by the fact that <a href="/en/">CBD</a> is used in many sectors. CBD is used as a beauty product, in food and drinks, and in medication. There are also speakers in the field of policy and the government. <br/><br/>Is this not enough for you yet? There are more fairs in England:</p>

<h3>Spannabis Barcelona</h3>
<p> When:  23, 24 and 25 April 2021 <br/> Where:  Barcelona, ​​Spain <br/><br/>Next year will be the 18th edition of Spannabis. This fair is known all over Europe, which is no wonder as it is one of the largest cannabis trade fairs. You can enjoy everything that has to do with cannabis for three days. There are 300 exhibitors and lectures are given, you can listen to presentations, network and participate in workshops. Both <a href="/en/">cannabis</a> and CBD are discussed. The lectures and presentations are also engaging. Last year, for example, there was a lot of attention for the legislation in Spain regarding cannabis. The conclusion was that it was time for Spain to take a look at countries with a more lenient government, such as Portugal and Uruguay.</p>

<h3>International Cannabis Business Conference</h3>
<p> When:  Date not yet known <br/> Where:  Berlin, Germany <br/><br/>This conference was scheduled for April this year, but unfortunately could not take place due to corona. It is the largest corporate cannabis event in Europe and has people from about 70 different countries. You can make new contacts here and learn more about cannabis-related topics. Attention is paid to policy, advocacy of cannabis and the possibilities of the industry. The ICBC has been set up on a grand scale. The intention this year was that 2500 entrepreneurs and policymakers from all over the world would come. How that will be next year is not known, but it will certainly be interesting. <br/><br/></p>

<p>Do you regularly feel easily distracted while working or studying? You are not alone. In a world full of notifications and digital distractions, it is increasingly difficult to stay focused for long periods of time. Fortunately, there are natural ways to improve your concentration. Specific supplements can play a valuable role in this, especially when combined with a healthy lifestyle. In this blog, you will discover which supplements are worth taking and how they can support your mental sharpness.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>These 4 supplements can support your brain naturally</h2>
<h3>1. Ginseng - Traditional herb for energy and cognitive performance</h3>
<p><a href="/en/product/3211666-ginseng-powder-panax-ginseng">Panax ginseng</a> has been used for centuries in traditional Chinese medicine. This root is known as an adaptogen, meaning it helps the body cope with stress. In addition, a <a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3659633/">2013 clinical study</a> shows that ginseng can contribute to improved cognitive function and physical performance.</p>
<p><strong>Form</strong>: powder, capsules<br/><strong>Recommended use</strong>: 200-400 mg per day<br/><strong>Notes</strong>: Effect on concentration is individual and still subject to research. Do not combine with blood thinners.</p>
<h3>2. Ashwagandha - For focus and stress reduction</h3>
<p><a href="/en/product/0207834-ashwaganda-11-extract-whithania-somnifera">Ashwagandha</a> ( Withania somnifera) is a powerful herb from Ayurvedic medicine. It is best known for its relaxing effects, but it also affects cognitive functioning. In a <a href="https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/19390211.2017.1284970">2017 study,</a> participants improved their memory and concentration after taking Ashwagandha.</p>
<p><strong>Form</strong>: capsules, tincture<br/><strong>Recommended use</strong>: 300-600 mg per day<br/><strong>Extra tip</strong>: Ashwagandha is ideal for stress-related concentration problems.</p>
<h3>3. Guarana - Long-term energy without the dip</h3>
<p><a href="/en/product/8101793-guarana-indian-elements">Guarana</a> ( Paullinia cupana) is rich in natural caffeine, but with a slower release than coffee. As a result, you experience a more stable energy boost. A <a href="https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/15/2/434">2023 study</a> shows that guarana gives slight improvements in reaction speed and alertness.</p>
<p><strong>Form</strong>: powder, drinks, capsules<br/><strong>Recommended dose</strong>: 50-100 mg caffeine per dose<br/><strong>Note</strong>: Not suitable for those who are sensitive to caffeine or have sleep problems.<br/><br/></p>

<h3>4. Ginkgo biloba - Possible brain blood flow support</h3>
<p><a href="/en/product/8101217-ginkgo-biloba-indian-elements">Ginkgo biloba</a> is often used to boost blood flow in the brain. While some users benefit from improved focus and memory, results are mixed. A study published in JAMA found no significant effect in the elderly.</p>
<p><strong>Form</strong>: capsules, tincture<br/><strong>Recommended use</strong>: 120-240 mg per day, divided into two intakes.<br/><strong>Important</strong>: Consult a doctor if you use blood thinners.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>Frequently asked questions about natural supplements for concentration:</h2>
<h3>What are the best natural supplements for focus?</h3>
<p>The most commonly used natural supplements that may support concentration are ginseng, ashwagandha, guarana and ginkgo biloba. Each of these acts differently on stress, brain blood flow or alertness.</p>
<h3>How fast do concentration supplements work?</h3>
<p>This varies from supplement to supplement and person to person. Some people notice effects within days (as with guarana), while other supplements like ashwagandha only give noticeable results after 2 to 4 weeks.</p>
<h3>Are natural supplements safe?</h3>
<p>In general, natural supplements are safe when used correctly. However, watch out for drug interactions and side effects. Always consult a doctor if you are taking medicines or have health issues.</p>
<h3>What is the difference between ginseng and guarana?</h3>
<p>Ginseng mainly supports mental fatigue and stress, while guarana contains caffeine and focuses on alertness and energy. Guarana works faster; ginseng has a broader, long-term effect.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>Storing magic truffles</h2>
<p>Fresh magic truffles can generally be stored for up to one month. It is best to keep them refrigerated at a temperature between 4 and 6 degrees Celsius. Dried truffles can be stored for up to six months, provided they are kept dry and airtight, preferably vacuum sealed. Store dried sclerotia in a cool, dark and dry place, but do not keep them in the refrigerator.</p>
<h2>Difference between fresh and dried truffles</h2>
<p>Sclerotia are available in two forms: fresh and dried. It is important to understand the difference because this has a major impact on dosage. Magic truffles consist of approximately 50% water. During the drying process, they lose about half of their weight. This means that 10 grams of fresh truffles is roughly equivalent to 5 grams of dried truffles. As a result, 10 grams of dried truffles is approximately twice as potent as 10 grams of fresh truffles. Keep this in mind when selecting the right amount of <a href="/en/shop/mushrooms/magic-truffles">magic truffles</a>.</p>

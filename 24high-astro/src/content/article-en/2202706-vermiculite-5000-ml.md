<h2>What is vermiculite?</h2>
<p>Vermiculite is a natural mineral that expands into light, porous granules when heated. In paddock cultivation, it is mainly used to improve structure and moisture balance in the substrate. The 3rd grade variant has a slightly coarser grain, which provides extra aeration.</p>
<h2>Why use vermiculite in paddock cultivation?</h2>
<p>Many growers choose vermiculite because it plays a supporting role during various growing methods. It does not actively contribute nutrients, but it does help keep conditions stable.</p>
<ul>
<li>Provides an airy and permeable substrate structure</li>
<li>Retains moisture without becoming soggy</li>
<li>Protects the mycelium from drying out</li>
<li>Suitable as a top layer or as part of a substrate mix</li>
</ul>
<h2>How to use vermiculite?</h2>
<p>This vermiculite is often used as a top layer or mixed with other ingredients, such as <em>brown rice flour</em>, in growing methods like the PF Tek. Slightly moistening the material beforehand creates an even moisture distribution.</p>
<p>It is important to note that vermiculite itself contains no nutrition, but serves purely as a supporting and regulating component within the growing process.</p>

<p>100% natural vermiculite (3rd grade) with a coarser grain structure for optimum aeration and moisture regulation.</p>

This well-known Mexican strain is often associated with a philosophical, creative and sociable effect, combined with clearly perceptible but generally not overpowering visuals. Thanks to its relatively simple cultivation process, this grow kit is suitable for both novice and more experienced growers.</p>
<blockquote style="background: #f9f9f9; border-left: 5px solid #55b308; padding: 15px 20px; margin: 30px 0; font-style: italic; border-radius: 0 8px 8px 0;"><strong>The Mondo Mexicana Grow Kit is a 1200 cc grow kit containing active <em>Psilocybe cubensis</em> Mexicana mycelium.</strong></blockquote>

<h2>What is the Mondo Mexicana Grow Kit?</h2>
<p>The Mondo Mexicana Grow Kit contains a prepared substrate with active mycelium of the Mexicana variety. The grow tray has a capacity of 1200 cc and comes with a grow bag and a paperclip. This means you have the most important components for setting up the grow kit straight to hand.</p>
<p>Would you like to better understand what happens between setting up a grow kit and the final harvest? In our guide to <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1649f5;"><a href="../guide/home-growing-and-deeper-exploration/the-growing-process.html" style="color: #1649f5; text-decoration: underline;">the home-growing process</a></span>, you can read more about the different stages that mycelium and magic mushrooms go through.</p>
<p>Mexicana is one of the well-known <em>Psilocybe cubensis strains</em> and is related to other Mexican genetics such as Mazatapec. The mushrooms can develop sturdy white stems with yellow to golden-coloured caps.</p>
<h2>Why choose the Mondo Mexicana Grow Kit?</h2>
<p>The Mexicana is often chosen for its combination of a relatively accessible growing process and an effect that many users describe as philosophical, creative and sociable. This makes this strain suitable for people seeking a classic cubensis experience without immediately opting for one of the most potent strains.</p>
<p>The cultivation process is also relatively straightforward. Provided temperature, humidity, light and fresh air are carefully monitored, multiple flushes can be produced under favourable conditions.</p>
<h2>Is the Mexicana Grow Kit suitable for beginners?</h2>
<p>Yes. The Mondo Mexicana Grow Kit is considered a relatively easy-to-care-for grow kit and may therefore be suitable for novice growers. Experienced growers also choose Mexicana because of its reliable growth and the recognisable character of this strain.</p>
<p>The final experience is not determined by the strain alone. Dosage, personal sensitivity, mental state and environment play an equally important role.</p>
<h2>What effects can you expect from Mexicana magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>Mexicana is a medium-strength <em>Psilocybe cubensis strain</em> often associated with philosophical thoughts, creativity and clear but generally not overwhelming visuals.</p>
<ul>
<li>Philosophical and profound thoughts</li>
<li>Creative thought patterns</li>
<li>Mild to moderate visual effects</li>
<li>More intense colours and patterns</li>
<li>Altered perception of time</li>
<li>Social and talkative experience</li>
<li>Heightened sensory perception</li>
<li>Physical sensations or ‘body high’</li>
</ul>
<p>Not everyone experiences the same effects. The intensity is partly influenced by dosage, mood, environment, experience and personal sensitivity.</p>
<blockquote style="background: #f9f9f9; border-left: 5px solid #55b308; padding: 15px 20px; margin: 30px 0; font-style: italic; border-radius: 0 8px 8px 0;"><strong>Mexicana is known for providing a philosophical, creative and moderately intense psychedelic experience.</strong></blockquote>
<h2>How do you use Mexicana magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>After harvesting, Mexicana magic mushrooms can be used fresh or dried first. Bear in mind that dried magic mushrooms weigh considerably less than fresh ones, so the quantities cannot be directly compared.</p>
<h3>General dosage guideline</h3>
<ul>
<li>Microdosing: approximately 0.2 grams dried or 2 grams fresh.</li>
<li>Mild experience: approximately 1 gram dried or 10 grams fresh.</li>
<li>Moderate experience: approximately 2 grams dried or 20 grams fresh.</li>
<li>More intense experience: approximately 3 grams dried or 30 grams fresh.</li>
</ul>
<p>Start cautiously if you have little experience and allow plenty of time for the effects to kick in. Always use an accurate set of scales and do not increase the amount straight away if you feel little effect after a short time.</p>

<h2>When should you harvest Mexicana magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>The right time to harvest is when the membrane between the cap and the stem begins to loosen. Don’t wait too long after that, as the mushrooms can continue to develop rapidly.</p>
<p>Gently grasp a mushroom by the stem and twist it loose carefully. Do not pull hard on the mushroom, as this may damage the surrounding mycelium and potentially affect the next flush.</p>
<h2>How do you get multiple flushes from the Mondo Mexicana Grow Kit?</h2>

<p>For subsequent flushes, it is particularly important to harvest carefully. Minimise damage to the substrate and mycelium as much as possible, and after each harvest, follow Mondo’s instructions again for preparing the next growth cycle.</p>

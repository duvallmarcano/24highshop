<h2>What is the True Albino Teacher grow bag?</h2>
<p>The True Albino Teacher grow bag contains a nutrient-rich substrate that has already been fully colonized with healthy Psilocybe cubensis mycelium. This means you do not need to add spores, liquid culture or other genetics. Once the grow bag is placed in a stable indoor environment, the mycelium can begin fruiting.</p>
<p>True Albino Teacher, often abbreviated as TAT, is a special albino variant within the Golden Teacher line. This strain is known for its light appearance, subtle growth patterns and higher sensitivity to climate fluctuations.</p>
<h2>Who is True Albino Teacher suitable for?</h2>
<p>True Albino Teacher is mainly chosen by growers who already have experience with magic mushroom grow bags or grow kits. This strain requires more precision than many standard cubensis varieties and is less forgiving when conditions fluctuate.</p>
<ul>
<li>Experienced hobby growers</li>
<li>Growers with a stable indoor environment</li>
<li>Fans of albino genetics</li>
<li>Users who want to compare unique strains</li>
<li>Growers who value quality and appearance over maximum yield</li>
</ul>
<h2>Growth characteristics of True Albino Teacher</h2>
<p>True Albino Teacher has a calmer and more sensitive growth pattern than many well-known cubensis strains. Development is usually slower, requiring patience, stability and careful attention.</p>
<ul>
<li>Slower development than standard cubensis strains</li>
<li>More sensitive to temperature and humidity fluctuations</li>
<li>Selective and less aggressive pinning</li>
<li>Striking pale to white fruiting bodies</li>
<li>Requires more attention than beginner-friendly varieties</li>
</ul>
<p>Compared to Golden Teacher, True Albino Teacher is less forgiving. A stable temperature, good air exchange and clean handling are therefore especially important.</p>
<h2>How do you use the True Albino Teacher grow bag?</h2>
<p>Because the substrate is already fully colonized, you can start the fruiting phase straight away. Make sure the environment is clean and avoid unnecessary contact with the grow bag to reduce the risk of contamination.</p>
<p><strong>Important: Small slit below the air filter</strong></p>
<p>Below the air filter on the grow bag, there is a small slit made by the manufacturer. This opening allows excess CO₂ and pressure to escape if the grow bag becomes inflated during the growing process. This is normal and is not damage. You do not need to do anything, and the opening should not be sealed or taped.</p>

<p>If indirect daylight is difficult to achieve, a grow light can help. If maintaining a constant temperature is challenging, a heat mat can offer support.</p>
<h2>What can you expect during cultivation?</h2>
<p>True Albino Teacher generally grows more slowly than Golden Teacher, Thai or Cambodian. As a result, flushes may need more time to develop properly. Many growers choose this strain precisely because of its unique appearance and the special character of its albino genetics.</p>
<p>Under stable conditions, the grow bag can usually produce several flushes. On average, you can expect two to four harvest rounds, depending on care, hygiene and climate control.</p>
<p>Important factors for successful cultivation include:</p>
<ul>
<li>Consistent temperature</li>
<li>Accurate humidity control</li>
<li>Sufficient fresh air</li>
<li>Clean handling</li>
<li>Proper rehydration between flushes</li>
</ul>

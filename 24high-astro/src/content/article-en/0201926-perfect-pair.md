<h2>What is The Perfect Pair Grow Kit?</h2>
<p>The Perfect Pair consists of two fully colonised grow kits: Amazonian and Treasure Coast. The mycelium is already established. This means you can start growing straight away and do not need to inoculate the kits yourself.</p>
<p>This combination allows you to grow two cubensis strains side by side. This way, you can observe the differences in growth, appearance and flushes for yourself.</p>

<h2>What can you expect from these magic mushroom grow kits?</h2>
<p>Amazonian and Treasure Coast each have their own growth characteristics. Both varieties can develop relatively quickly under stable conditions.</p>
<ul>
<li>Two different <em>Psilocybe cubensis</em> grow kits</li>
<li>Amazonian often produces large and firm mushrooms</li>
<li>Treasure Coast often produces compact flushes with plenty of magic mushrooms</li>
<li>Both grow kits have a volume of 1200 cc</li>
<li>Multiple flushes are possible with proper care</li>
</ul>
<p>Growth time and yield vary from one grow to the next. Temperature, humidity, hygiene and care all play an important role in this.</p>
<h2>What is the difference between Amazonian and Treasure Coast?</h2>
<p><strong>Amazonian</strong> is known as a fast-growing cubensis strain. The mushrooms can grow large and often have thick stems with golden-brown to orange caps. According to the product information, multiple flushes are possible with proper care.</p>
<p><strong>Treasure Coast</strong> also grows quickly, but has a different growth pattern. This strain often produces many mushrooms growing close together. The mushrooms have sturdy stems and caramel-coloured caps with light spots.</p>
<p>With Treasure Coast, a completely light-coloured specimen may sometimes appear amongst a flush. The appearance and exact growth pattern may vary from one grow to another.</p>

<h2>How do you use The Perfect Pair Grow Kits?</h2>
<p>Both grow kits are set up separately. Follow the same 9-step plan for each kit. You can also <span style="color: #1649f5;"><strong><a href="../files/index.html@id=3063.pdf&amp;file=mondo-magic-mushroom-growkit-manual+(2).pdf" style="color: #1649f5;">download the Mondo Grow Kit manual as a PDF</a></strong></span>.</p>

<li>Fill the grow tray with water, place the lid on top and leave the kit to soak for 12 hours.</li>
<li>Then pour out all the excess water from the grow tray.</li>
<li>Remove the lid, rinse it clean and set it aside.</li>
<li>Pour approximately 150 ml of tap water into the grow bag and place the grow kit inside.</li>
<li>Fold the top of the bag over twice. Keep the microfilter clear and seal the bag with the paperclip.</li>

<h2>What are the effects of Amazonian and Treasure Coast magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>Amazonian and Treasure Coast belong to the <em>Psilocybe cubensis family</em> and naturally contain psilocybin. The experience can vary greatly from person to person and from moment to moment.</p>
<p><strong>Treasure Coast</strong> is often associated with a cheerful and visual character. Possible effects include changes in colours, sounds, perception and the perception of time.</p>
<p><strong>Amazonian</strong> is often described as intense and visual. Changes in perception, thoughts, mood and the perception of time may occur.</p>
<p>The intensity depends, amongst other things, on the dose, personal sensitivity, mindset and environment. A specific experience cannot therefore be guaranteed.</p>
<h2>How do you use Amazonian and Treasure Coast magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>The harvested magic mushrooms can be used fresh or dried. Fresh and dried mushrooms vary greatly in weight. You should therefore always weigh the quantity carefully.</p>
<p>The magic mushrooms can be eaten or used to make tea. Take into account your experience, personal sensitivity and the potential intensity of the psychedelic effects.</p>
<h2>How do you prepare?</h2>
<p>Choose a quiet and familiar environment. Make sure you have plenty of time and no other commitments. A sober and trusted person can offer extra support.</p>
<p>Your mindset and surroundings can influence the experience. Therefore, choose a time when you feel comfortable and have plenty of peace and quiet.</p>
<h2>Multiple flushes: what should you bear in mind?</h2>

<li>Ensure sufficient humidity and fresh air</li>
<li>Work cleanly to prevent contamination</li>
<li>Harvest carefully to minimise damage to the mycelium</li>
</ul>

San Pedro means Saint Peter who, in Biblical terms, received the keys to the 'Kingdom of heaven' from Jesus. The Spanish saw the San Pedro cactus as sacred.</p>

<h3>What makes the San Pedro Pachanoi Psychedelic?</h3>
<p>Mescaline is a psychedelic alkaloid found in nature. The Mescaline alkaloid closely resembles the body's neurotransmitter epinephrine (adrenaline). <h3>How do you prepare the Mescaline San Pedro Pachanoi cactus for consumption?</h3>
<p>Of course, you cannot consume the cactus directly on arrival. The San Pedro will have to be processed first. San Pedro can also be processed into powder, by drying it first. <ol>
<li>Work hygienically and put on gloves.</li>
<li>Remove the thorns.Make a full-length cut from top to bottom between the ribs.</li>
<li>Pull the translucent skin off the green tissue from top to bottom and, if necessary, grate the entrained green matter off the peeled off skin.</li>
<li>Now the San Pedro Cactus is bare and ready to cut into slices about 1 cm thick.</li>
<li>Put the 1 cm thick San Pedro pieces in an oven at 50 °C until they are cork dry to the touch. You can of course also choose to let them dry in the sun.</li>
<li>Grind the dried disks to a fine powder (The San pedro can also be consumed now but this way will often lead to stomach cramps).</li>
<li>Put the San Pedro powder in one pan with water and let it boil for a few hours.</li>
<li>Boiling dissolves the active substances in the boiling water.</li>
<li>After this, pour the water into another larger pan (pan 2).</li>
<li>A mash remains in pan 1. Fill pan 1 with water again.</li>
<li>Repeat steps 8, 9, and 10, a total of 4 or 5 times.</li>
<li>Test the mash in pan 1 if it still tastes bitter you can repeat the process from point 8 and 9 again until all the bitter taste has disappeared from the Mescaline mash pan 1.</li>
<li>Then boil the contents of pan 2 so that about 1 litre of water remains.</li>
<li>Sieve the contents of pot 2 before drinking it.</li>

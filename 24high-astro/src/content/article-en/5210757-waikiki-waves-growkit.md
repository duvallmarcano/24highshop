As the grow kit is already fully colonised, you do not need to add any spores and can start the cultivation process straight away.</p>

<h3>Extra tip during cultivation</h3>
<p>When the mycelium or substrate starts to visibly shrink, you can carefully pour a small glass of water down the inside of the plastic container. Make sure the water remains <strong>beneath</strong> the substrate and is about 1 to 2 centimetres below the top of the substrate. This will maintain the moisture level without the substrate becoming too wet.</p>

<h2>What are Psilocybe Golden Teacher truffles?</h2>
<p>Golden Teacher truffles are sclerotia from the Psilocybe family. Sclerotia are underground nutrient reserves of the mushroom and naturally contain psilocybin. After ingestion, psilocybin is converted in the body into psilocin.</p>
<p>Psilocin affects serotonin receptors and is associated with changes in perception, mood and thought patterns. The overall experience varies from person to person and is strongly influenced by dosage, mindset (set) and environment (setting).</p>

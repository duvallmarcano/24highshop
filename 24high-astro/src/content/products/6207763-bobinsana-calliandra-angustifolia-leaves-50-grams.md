<p>The distinctive leaves of Bobinsana have been referenced for generations within the ethnobotany of various indigenous communities. Thanks to its rich cultural background and unique origins, Bobinsana is now a much-loved botanical species amongst collectors and enthusiasts of Amazonian plants.</p>

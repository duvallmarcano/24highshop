Syrian rue is known for its MAOI effects but with fewer negative side effects like Banisteropsis caapi. MAOI is an enzyme that breaks down Monoamines (adrenaline, serotonin, dopamine, etc.). A Mao inhibitor inhibits this breakdown process. This breakdown process promotes a stronger and more intense trip in users of Entheogenic (magic mushrooms, LSA seeds, truffles) substances simply because less of the psychedelic active substance is broken down by the body.</p>

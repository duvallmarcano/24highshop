<p><strong>Blue Cheese Automatic</strong> is a flexible plant for both indoor and outdoor, flowering in a normal setting with high light output. The plant's small size makes it very suitable for balconies, terraces or other environments where discreet growth is desired. The plant will show purple colours if the nights before harvest are cold. However, due to its early harvest dates outdoors, this is rare. Indoor yields are 325 - 375 g/m2, and outdoors 60 - 110 g/m2. The buds are heavy, compact and full of sticky resin.<br/>Blue Cheese's self-flowering feminised plants love sunlight. So plant them between mid-May and early June if you are growing outdoors. Adding 30% coconut to the soil mix will improve aeration and root growth, and allow the plant to develop to its maximum potential. As with all automatic plants, Blue Cheese should be supplied with small amounts of water on a frequent basis and have plenty of good light. Blue Cheese Automatic is a grower-friendly seed that has no special requirements and is not too difficult to cultivate</p>

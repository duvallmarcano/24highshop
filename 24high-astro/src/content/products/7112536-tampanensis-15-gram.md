<h2>What are Tampanensis truffles?</h2>
<p>Tampanensis truffles are <em>psilocybe sclerotia</em>, also called magic truffles. This underground structure forms from the mycelium of certain <em>Psilocybe</em> species. They naturally contain psilocybin, a substance that is converted in the body into psilocin and can influence perception, mood, and thoughts.</p>
<p>The Tampanensis truffle is often considered an accessible variety within the assortment and is known for its clear and calm psychedelic experience.</p>

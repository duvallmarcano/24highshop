<p>The <strong>Levenhuk Rainbow 50L PLUS Educational Microscope </strong>is perfect for anyone who loves to explore the world on a microscopic level - whether you are a teenager with a passion for science, a biology student or just love to experiment. With impressive magnification of up to 1280x and a sturdy metal housing, this microscope is suitable for home, school and lab use.</p>
<h3>Features</h3>
<p>This microscope has three objectives (4x, 10x and 40xs) and comes with a 2x Barlow lens, giving you a magnification range of 64x to 1280x. The 40xs lens features a spring mechanism, so you can view your specimens without worry. The optical components are made of high-quality glass with an anti-reflective coating, ensuring a clear, sharp and high-contrast image.</p>
<p>With both top and bottom (LED) illumination, you can view different types of objects. The adjustable brightness allows you to easily adjust the light to your specimen. Transparent, opaque or something in between? No problem - just use the right light source or combine them.</p>
<p>The design is not only robust, but also comfortable. The metal housing makes the microscope extra durable. The head is tilted 45° and can rotate 360°, ideal for group work or prolonged use. You can connect it to mains power or use it with batteries - handy if you want to take it outside.</p>

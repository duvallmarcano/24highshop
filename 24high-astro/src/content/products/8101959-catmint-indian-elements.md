<li>Calming &amp; relaxing – Ideal for a soothing moment.</li>
<li>Mildly psychedelic effect – At higher doses, it can be hypnotic or mildly hallucinogenic.</li>

<li>Natural composition – Contains Nepetalactone, similar to compounds found in Valerian.</li>

</ul>

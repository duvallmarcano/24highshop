<h3>What you need to grow mushrooms</h3>
<ol>
<li>Grain (nutrition): Mushrooms need a source of nutrients to grow. Grain such as rye or maize is often used, as it is rich in carbohydrates and other nutrients essential for the growth of the mycelium (the root structure of the mushroom).</li>
<li>Substrate (soil): The substrate provides physical support for the mycelium and helps with water retention. <li>Culture (the mycelium or spores): This is the biological basis of the mushroom. The mycelium is the vegetative form of the mushroom and grows through the substrate to form the mushroom. It can be bought as 'liquid culture' or home-grown from spores. (We recommend liquid culture)</li>

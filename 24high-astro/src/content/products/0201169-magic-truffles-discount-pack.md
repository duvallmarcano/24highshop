<h2>What are magic truffles?</h2>
<p>Magic truffles (also known as <em>sclerotia</em>) are a natural underground part of certain <em>Psilocybe</em> species. They grow as compact nutrient reserves within the mycelium and contain psilocybin and psilocin, compounds known for their psychedelic effects.</p>
<p>Many users describe changes in perception, more intense colors, deeper thoughts, and an increased sense of introspection. The exact experience can vary from person to person and depends on factors such as dosage, environment, and individual sensitivity.</p>

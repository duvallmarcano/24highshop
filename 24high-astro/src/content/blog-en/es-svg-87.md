<h2 id="what-is-kratom">What is kratom?</h2>
<p>Kratom (<em>Mitragyna speciosa</em>) is a tropical tree belonging to the coffee family (<em>Rubiaceae</em>). It grows mainly in Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, and other parts of Southeast Asia. Depending on the growing conditions, a mature tree can reach a height of between 4 and more than 25 metres.</p>
<p>For centuries, local communities have used the leaves of this tree. Traditionally, the leaves were chewed or brewed into tea. Today, kratom is available worldwide in the form of powder, capsules, extracts, or crushed leaves.</p>
<p>Kratom has become increasingly popular in recent years, mainly because users report a wide variety of experiences. Some people choose kratom for a sense of energy and focus, while others are looking for a more relaxing experience. The effects someone experiences depend on several factors, including the strain used, personal sensitivity, and the amount consumed.</p>

<h2 id="alkaloids">Which active compounds does kratom contain?</h2>
<p>Scientists have identified more than forty different alkaloids in kratom. The best-known are:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mitragynine</strong></li>
<li><strong>7-Hydroxymitragynine</strong></li>
<li><strong>Speciogynine</strong></li>
<li><strong>Paynantheine</strong></li>
<li><strong>Mitraphylline</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>Among these compounds, mitragynine receives the most scientific attention because it makes up the largest proportion of the plant's total alkaloid content. 7-Hydroxymitragynine occurs naturally in much smaller amounts but is also widely studied because of its interaction with specific receptors in the body.</p>
<p>The exact ratio of these alkaloids varies from one harvest to another. Factors such as climate, soil conditions, leaf maturity, drying methods, and growing region all influence the final composition. This means that two batches of the same kratom variety may still produce different user experiences.</p>
<p>For this reason, experienced users often look beyond the colour of a kratom strain and also consider the supplier, origin, product quality, and batch consistency.</p>

<h2 id="safety">Safe use and potential risks</h2>
<p>As with many natural products, responsible use is important when it comes to kratom. Although many people report positive experiences, kratom is not without risks. The likelihood of unwanted effects generally increases with higher amounts, frequent use, or when combined with other substances.</p>
<p>If you take medication or have a medical condition, always consult a physician before using kratom. Extra caution is also advised if you have a personal or family history of mental health disorders or substance dependence.</p>
<h3>Possible side effects</h3>
<p>Not everyone experiences side effects. When they do occur, they are often dose-dependent. Side effects described in scientific literature and user reports include:</p>
<ul>
<li>Dry mouth.</li>
<li>Nausea.</li>
<li>Dizziness.</li>
<li>Reduced appetite.</li>
<li>Constipation.</li>
<li>Drowsiness.</li>
<li>Sweating.</li>
<li>Increased heart rate.</li>
</ul>
<p>Very high amounts or prolonged intensive use have been associated with more serious health risks, including liver problems, dependence, and withdrawal symptoms. The likelihood of these effects varies from person to person.</p>
<h3>Can kratom be addictive?</h3>
<p>Yes. Scientific research indicates that regular kratom use can lead to tolerance and dependence. The risk increases particularly with daily use over an extended period.</p>
<p>People who suddenly stop after prolonged use may experience withdrawal symptoms, including:</p>
<ul>
<li>restlessness;</li>
<li>sleep disturbances;</li>
<li>muscle aches;</li>
<li>irritability;</li>
<li>fatigue;</li>
<li>mood swings.</li>
</ul>
<p>For this reason, many experienced users choose to alternate periods of use with several rest days.</p>
<h3>Do not combine kratom with other substances without caution</h3>
<p>Combining kratom with alcohol, opioids, sleeping medication, or other psychoactive substances may increase the risk of adverse effects. Certain medications may also interact with the active compounds found in kratom.</p>
<p>For that reason, extra caution is recommended if you are taking medication or have underlying health conditions. When in doubt, always seek professional medical advice.</p>

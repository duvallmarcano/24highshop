<h2>What is microdosing?</h2>
<p>With microdosing you will absolutely not trip. Because you only ingest psilocybin (active ingredient in magic mushrooms) in a small amount, this will not induce hallucinations or a trip feeling. With a microdose of psilocybin in your body you can feel calmer / more relaxed and your presentations can improve in certain areas. For example, consider working in the creative sector where you really have to think out-of-the-box. Several studies have shown that people who take a microdose of psilocybin  for an <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../../../10/why-microdosage-with-magic-truffels.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">improved creativity</a></span>, improved emotional well-being and a better everyday experience functionality.</p>
<p>With microdosing you absolutely do not have the feeling that you have ingested magic mushrooms. You shouldn't trip and space, that's not possible because you only took a very small dose.</p>

<h2>How do you go about microdosing?</h2>
<p>If you want to measure the effects properly, a fixed pattern is recommended. Experts and researchers all recommend roughly the same schedule. Microdosing researcher's scheme of James Fadiman often recurs during the microdosing process. This is a good starting point for a microdosing experiment. Fadiman assumes a 3-day cycle. According to him, it is advisable to follow these 8 to 10 weeks.</p>

<h2>Microdosage schedule according to Dr. James Fadiman</h2>
<p><strong><em>DAY 1: Microdosing day</em></strong></p>
<p>Take a day off, prepare yourself mentally and let's go! Take the microdose of mushrooms in the morning and experience your feeling during the microdosing. It is therefore advisable to take it in the morning, as you can feel energetic. This can strain your sleep. You can do a nice forest walk or make <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../../../105/tripping-outdoor-tips-and-location.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">nice spot</a></span> looking up in nature.</p>
<p><strong><em>DAY 2: Transition day (no microdosing)</em></strong></p>
<p>This day is important because your microdoses work through your brain for two days. It is therefore important today to describe your feeling and your perception compared to yesterday. What are you feeling today? Do you still feel different than usual? Take notes.</p>
<p><strong><em>DAY 3: Normal day (no microdosing)</em></strong></p>
<p>Today you will feel as usual again. What is different from previous days? It is important to keep this in mind. Experience emotions, thoughts and perceptions.</p>

<h2>What are the disadvantages of microdosing?</h2>
<p>There are not many complaints / negative effects if you <span style="color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../../../../mushrooms/189-microdosing.html" style="color: #1806ff;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;">microdose</span></a></span> in the right way. <p><em>Does the fatigue (or any other possible complaint) persist? Then you can interrupt the cycle by taking a break. Listen carefully to your body during this process.<br/><br/></em></p>
<h2>When is microdosing not recommended?</h2>
<p>In some cases, microdosing is not recommended.</p>
<ul>
<li>In combination with other drugs</li>
<li>In combination with alcohol</li>
<li>If you are under 18</li>
<li>During pregnancy/breastfeeding</li>

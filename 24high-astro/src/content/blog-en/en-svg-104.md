<h2>Ratio of psilocybin in fresh truffles and magic mushrooms</h2>
<p>The amount of psilocybin in mushrooms can vary depending on the species, growing conditions, and freshness. A common estimate for Psilocybe cubensis, one of the most common species, is that dried mushrooms contain approximately 0.5 to 1.5% psilocybin. This means that 1 gram of dried Psilocybe cubensis contains on average between 5 and 15 mg of psilocybin. In fresh truffles (Psilocybe sclerotia), the psilocybin content varies, but on average truffles contain about 0.5 to 1 mg of psilocybin per gram of fresh truffles. This means that 1 gram of fresh truffles typically contains between 0.05 and 0.1% psilocybin.So before using these psychedelic drugs, it is good to think about the <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1807ff;"><a href="../../../../166/how-should-you-dose-fresh-and-dried-mushrooms.html" style="color: #1807ff; text-decoration: underline;">right dosage</a></span>.<br/><img src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=1426.jpg&amp;file=dryshrooms.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>

<h3>Effects light trip dosage</h3>
<p>In a light trip, you feel slightly tipsy or very mildly stoned. Colours become brighter and your short-term memory goes a little crazy. Sounds become broader, music sounds nicer and you run little risk of losing grip on reality. A perfect choice for budding psychonauts.</p>
<h3>Effects mild trip dosage</h3>
<p>Colours become deeper and they may change suddenly. The faces of people around you may suddenly turn green or pink, and sometimes you burst into a huge fit of laughter all at once. <h3>Effects intense trip dosage</h3>

<h2>More factors that influence your trip with magic mushrooms or truffles</h2>
<p>The dosage not only determines the effects of your trip, several factors play a role. Some people are more sensitive to psilocybin than others. Your mood and environment also play an important role. A few tips before taking fresh <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1807ff;"><a href="../../../../../mushrooms/16-magic-truffles.html" style="color: #1807ff; text-decoration: underline;">truffles</a></span> or magic mushrooms:</p>
<ul>
<li>Take psychedelics on an empty stomach. Preferably, don't eat anything for four hours before you start.</li>
<li>Choose a quiet environment or one you are familiar with to let the magic mushrooms and truffles really do their job.</li>
<li>Use a low dose the first time and build up slowly.</li>
<li>Dim the lights and turn off your TV and phone for a more intense visual experience during the trip.</li>
<li>Never take multiple doses in one evening. Effects often come in fits and starts, and a double dose can cause overwhelming moments.</li>

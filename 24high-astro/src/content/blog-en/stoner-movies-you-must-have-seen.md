<h3>Pineapple Express</h3>
<p>Let me get it right away start with this one, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BWZt4v6b1hI"><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;">Pineapple Express</span></a>. This is one of the most famous stoner movies and for good reason! Almost every cannabis smoker will know this story. It's about two stoner friends, one of whom accidentally sees a murder. They desperately try to stay one step ahead of the killers, but, as you can probably guess, all sorts of things happen to this hilarious stoner duo. I think this is THE stoner movie par excellence for many.</p>
<h3>Cheech &amp; Chong Movies</h3>
<p>This is undoubtedly the most famous stoner duo in the world: Cheech &amp; Chong. They started in the 70's / 80's with stand-up comedy, studio recordings and soon they also played in movies. Films based on the hippie culture, especially drugs and alternative culture, but above all based on their predilection for cannabis. <span style="color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../headshop/131-grinders-creditcard-grinder.html" style="color: #1806ff;">Cheech &amp; Chong</a></span> made a lot of material and had it made. <h3>Mac &amp; Devin Go to High School</h3>
<p>Who doesn't know them? Snoop Dogg &amp; Wiz Khalifa! They star together in this hilarious movie, Snoop Dogg as Mac Johnson and Wiz Khalifa as Devin Overstreet. They play two teenagers who go to high school. In the beginning, they both have a completely different view of their school life. However, this soon changes when Devin meets Mac, who has been at the same school for 15 years. They become more and more friends and at some point they let a 'miracle' happen together.</p>
<h3>Don't Be a Menace to South Central (While Drinking Your Juice in the Hood)</h3>
<p>Yes, another classic: Don't Be a Menace to South Central While Drinking Your Juice in the Hood. That's a mouthful! This film could have chosen any title. A just crazy comedy movie that you like to watch stoned. <a href="../../headshop/135-smoking-accessories-ashtrays.html"><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;">Ashtray</span></a> returns to where he grew up. After a long time he sees his father and they are friends again. However, something is not quite right. Ashtray is older than his father, and his best friend Loc Dog's grandmother is a trigger-happy old woman. And to make it all the crazier, Loc Dog walks around with a nuclear weapon in self-defense. The movie is a parody of the "hood" movies like Menace II Society and Boyz N The Hood. In short, a very funny and hilarious comedy that is definitely worth watching with your friends.</p>
<h3>How High</h3>
<p>In this film you see the rappers Method man and Redman as actors. Nothing useful Silas (Method Man) from Staten Island grows cannabis plants for his hobby. His best customer and close friend is Ivory who, when high, has a fatal accident. After his cremation, Silas spreads his ashes over the plant bed as fertilizer. Jamal King (Redman) from Jersey, who is chased by his mother not to hang around, but to get his high school diploma and go to college. Jamal wants to go crazy with Silas stuff because of all that nagging. When the two start smoking hard, the ghost of the deceased Ivory appears in the smoke like a kind of ghost from the lamp, who will assist them in obtaining good grades for the final diploma. This is how this duo ends up in Harvard University, where they experience the craziest adventures! They feed the neat Dean Cain <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="../67/how-do-you-make-and-use-spacecake.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">space cake</a></span> and discover that the white Professor Jackson, who specializes in the history of black people, is a masochist. Silas begins a romance with dazzling college student Lauren, while Jamal hooks up with US Vice President's daughter, Jamie. The rappers Method Man and Redman as the protagonists keep the momentum going in this very funny comedy and provide enough clumsy and hilarious situations.</p>
<h3>Puff, Puff, Pass</h3>
<p>Friends Larry and Rico share two great passions: smoking cannabis and watching television. The two stoners live in a grim apartment. Just on the day that a marathon of their favorite movie is broadcast, they are evicted from their home for overdue rent. Instead of trying to get the money for the rent together, the two empty-headed people look for another place where they can hang out in front of the TV. What follows is a comic search through rehab clinics and villas. Eventually they accept a proposal from an acquaintance to deliver a briefcase to a certain Mr. Big. However, the problems quickly pile up.</p>
<h3>Friday</h3>
<p>In this film we see a 'comical' day in the life of two gangsters. Craig (Ice Cube) and Smokey (Chris Tucker) are two friends from Los Angeles who are bored. One <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=umvFBoLOOgo" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">Friday</a> </span>afternoon, Craig wakes up after being fired from work the day before. His best friend Smokey comes by and tells him about his plans and Craig himself will have to look for a new job. However, they are more in search of thrills and adventure. While smoking and drinking they do the craziest things to have fun. In addition, they do not care at all what happens. They smoke a dealer's dope and get into even more trouble. In all that time they smoke a lot of weed and experience the most absurd things, such as being attacked and shot at.</p>
<h3>Scary Movie</h3>
<p>The Scary Movie series is a film series that almost everyone knows for its bizarre humor. The story of Scary Movie (the first in the series) goes like this. On Halloween night, Cindy Campbell and five friends accidentally run over a man. They decide to keep the accident a secret, but a year later the six are chased by a masked serial killer, who wants to play cruel games with them. It starts with the brutal murder of Drew Decker, and it doesn't take long for the killer to go after Cindy and the rest. They do everything they can to survive, but the killer does not give up easily! He has a lot of knowledge in the field of horror films. Flat jokes and gloating are interspersed with quotes from horror classics such as The Blair Witch Project, The Matrix, The Sixth Sense and Scream.</p>
<h3>American Pie</h3>
<p>The American Pie film series are very popular, mainly because of their sexual humor. The story of the first American Pie goes like this. Teenagers Jim, Nadia, Michelle and Chris come to the conclusion that losing their virginity is not easy. They are convinced that this absolutely must be done before going to college. To increase motivation, they make a bet with each other to lose their virginity before their high school prom. That of course leads to a very funny comedy, with very nice and hilarious moments.</p>
<h3>Dazed and Confused</h3>
<p>A nice movie to watch stoned is <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3aQuvPlcB-8" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">Dazed and Confused</a></span>. The story is set in Austin, Texas, 1976. Without a fixed pattern or story, we follow how some students experience their last day of school. There are bizarre initiation rituals, love affairs that begin and end, abundant drinking and marijuana use, and above all, great boredom - even though the students are constantly on the hunt for tickets to the Aerosmith concert in town. The film gives a good impression of the time and is full of ironic observations and striking descriptions. The largely debuting cast features many now-familiar faces (Ben Affleck, Matthew McConaughey, Milla Jovovich), and the soundtrack features seventies hits. So this is definitely a movie to watch for those who want to relive the 70's.</p>
<h3>TED</h3>
<p>This funny comedy TED is really a movie you stoned. John (Mark Wahlberg) is an ordinary Boston man who had a less normal childhood. As a child, he wished his beloved teddy bear Ted would come to life, which eventually actually happened! Years later, the boy of yesteryear is now a grown man and the bear is still his best friend. The problem is that the bear is quite irresponsible and keeps John from really growing up and winning the love of his life. John needs to choose to keep the love of his life or does he opt for friendship with Ted, the gross and very inappropriate teddy bear?</p>
<h3>The Big Lebowski</h3>
<p>Qualitatively this one is of the best films in the list. The story is about Jeff Lebowski, nicknamed The Dude. He is visited by a number of shady types who claim that his girlfriend owes money to their boss. The Dude discovers that they mistake him for a very wealthy namesake and goes to tell him. Then the misery really starts, especially when his bowling buddy Walter gets involved.</p>
<h3>Inherent Vice</h3>
<p>The psychedelic work of the American writer Thomas Pynchon was long known as unreportable, but turns out to be well spent on Pynchon fan and director Anderson. Joaquin Phoenix is ​​on a roll as permanently stoned sleuth Doc Sportello in 1960s Los Angeles and becomes entangled in an inimitable disappearance case. Doc Sportello is a private detective. One day his ex arrives at the door with the message that the billionaire she is in love with is going to be kidnapped. Sportello embarks on the case and before he knows it, he finds himself entangled in a bizarre web of motives and in his quest he encounters surfers, con men, drug addicts, a murderous loan shark, an undercover saxophonist and, finally, a mysterious entity known as the Golden Fang. The plot, the noir elements and the stoner corniness are sometimes reminiscent of The Big Lebowski, but <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wZfs22E7JmI" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">Inherent Vice</a></span> is certainly just as unique!</p>
<h3>True Romance</h3>
<p>Clarence works in a comic book store and spends most of his free time in the cinema watching kung fu movies. One day he meets the prostitute Alabama, with whom he falls head over heels in love. However, she cannot just quit the job and so Clarence must first try to buy her off from her pimp. A tumultuous showdown with her pimp ensues, and Clarence accidentally comes into possession of a suitcase of cocaine. He decides to sell this enormous amount on his own and moves to Los Angeles. The combination of Tony "Top Gun" Scott (director) and Quentin "Reservoir Dogs" Tarantino (script) works out wonderfully well, in this hip, funny and exciting chase film. This film has a sequence of unforgettable scenes. And to top it all off, Brad Pitt also appears in the movie as stoner and pothead! Scott's direction is, as always, smooth and smooth but the big question remains what the film would have looked like if Tarantino, "the legend himself", had directed it.</p>
<h3>Drugs Inc. (Series)</h3>
<p>Last but not least, the Drugs Inc. series This series is about all kinds of drugs and everything related to them. How does the drug trade work? Can it be stopped or should it be regulated? What are the individual and social costs? These are just a few of the many questions asked in this documentary series. In Drugs Inc. we look at the most common cases of drug use, including that of cocaine, heroin, methamphetamine, cannabis and many more. In these documentaries smugglers, dealers, users, police, justice and medical professionals have their say. It explains how the <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../smartshop/36-test-for-drugs.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">drugs are processed</a></span> and brought onto the street, but also what effects these drugs have on health and society. The Drugs Inc. series is a real eye-opener and a hard-hitting documentary about an ever-growing trillion industry.</p></div>

They support the balance of the stress system (HPA axis) and may lower cortisol, restore energy, and improve mental resilience. <em>Ashwagandha</em> is mainly calming, while <em>rhodiola</em> helps combat fatigue and mental exhaustion. Adaptogens offer support but do not replace medical treatment.</p>
<h2>What are adaptogens?</h2>
<p>According to <span style="color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://www.uclahealth.org/news/article/what-are-adaptogens-and-should-you-be-taking-them" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">UCLA</a></span>, adaptogens are plant compounds that help the body cope with stress, provided that they:</p>
<ol>
<li>are not toxic when used normally,</li>
<li>support the entire body in responding to stressors,</li>
<li>help bring the body back into balance after a stress response.</li>
</ol>
<p>In summary: adaptogens may offer support during stress, but they do not solve the underlying cause. They simply help the body handle tension more effectively, as also described by <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://scispace.com/pdf/the-effects-of-adaptogens-on-the-physical-and-psychological-2l0jq322il.pdf" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">Ajala (2017)</a></span>.</p>

<h2>How adaptogens influence stress</h2>
<p>The hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis (HPA axis) plays a central role in the regulation of stress hormones such as cortisol. Adaptogens influence this axis and may contribute to a more stable hormonal response, as shown in <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3991026/" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">research by Panossian and Wikman</a></span>.</p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/8/4564" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">Llopis, San-Miguel, and Serrano</a></span> also found evidence in their systematic review that adaptogens may reduce both physiological stress markers and self-reported stress.</p>
<h2>The effects of ashwagandha</h2>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../../../../210/what-is-ashwagandha-and-how-does-it-work.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">Ashwagandha</a></span> is one of the most widely used adaptogens for stress. In the study by Llopis et al., participants with chronic stress experienced a 16% decrease in cortisol after four weeks and a 22.2% decrease after eight weeks. Perceived stress also dropped significantly.</p>
<p>Other sources, such as <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://nutraceuticalbusinessreview.com/ashwagandha-rhodiola-rosea-combatting-stress" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">Nutraceutical Business Review</a></span>, point to positive effects on anxiety, inner restlessness, and sleep problems.</p>
<h3>Important considerations</h3>
<p>Because ashwagandha has a calming effect, it is usually wise to take it in the evening. Keep in mind that adaptogens are only a supplement. Healthy sleep, nutrition, exercise, and relaxation techniques remain essential, as you can also read in our blogs on <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../../../../399/the-most-well-known-sleep-disruptors-this-will-keep-you-awake-at-night.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">sleep problems</a></span> and <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../../../../387/breathwork-breathing-exercises-as-a-drug.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">breathing exercises</a></span>.<br/><br/></p>
<p><img alt="Dry ashwagandha roots scattered on a wooden surface, in warm earthy tones with clearly visible texture." src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=2777.jpg&amp;file=ashwaganda.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h2>The effects of rhodiola</h2>
<p>Rhodiola rosea is another well-known adaptogen, with a slightly different effect than ashwagandha. It is mainly used for mental and physical fatigue and low mood. Rhodiola is more stimulating and may help improve resilience, as discussed in this <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2024/02/22/Combatting-stress-Efficacy-and-safety-of-Rhodiola-rosea-and-Ashwagandha-reviewed/" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">article</a></span>.</p>
<h3>Important considerations</h3>
<p>Because rhodiola has an activating effect, many people choose to take it in the morning or early afternoon. Always consult a doctor if you use medication or have health conditions.</p>
<h2>When to consider using adaptogens</h2>
<p>You might consider adaptogens if you:</p>
<ul>
<li>experience mental or physical exhaustion,</li>
<li>often deal with stress that affects your daily functioning,</li>
<li>want additional support alongside lifestyle interventions such as <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/meditation" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">meditation</a></span>, exercise, or sleep optimization.</li>
</ul>
<p>Note: adaptogens are <strong>not</strong> a replacement for medical treatment. Always consult a professional if you experience severe symptoms such as depression or anxiety disorders.</p>
<h3>Safety and considerations</h3>
<p>Adaptogen supplements fall under dietary supplements and are less strictly regulated than medicines. Pay attention to product quality and preferably buy from a reliable supplier. Extra caution is necessary with certain conditions, such as thyroid disorders or high blood pressure.</p>

If you have health issues, take medication, or are pregnant or breastfeeding, always consult a doctor, pharmacist, or other qualified healthcare provider before using supplements or herbal preparations.</p>

<h2>What is Guayusa?</h2>
<p>Guayusa<span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1c33c4;"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilex_guayusa" style="color: #1c33c4; text-decoration: underline;">(Ilex guayusa</a></span>) is a plant that is related to holly. This potent little plant is native to the vast Amazon rainforest in South America. The leaves of the Guayusa plant are dried and brewed into a tea-like beverage. While Guayusa has only recently been found in the western world, it has been consumed by indigenous tribes for centuries. In the traditional preparation of guayusa, the leaves are roasted over an open fire and then soaked in hot water for several minutes. This process produces a greenish drink that resembles green tea in taste.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>The effects of Guayusa</h2>
<p>Guayusa is known for its uplifting and stimulating properties. You can compare its effects and active ingredients to those of Yerba maté, which you may already be familiar with. The leaves are especially rich in caffeine, theobromine and theanine. One cup of Guayusa tea contains as much <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1c33c4;"><a href="../213/inconspicuous-drugs-caffeine.html" style="color: #1c33c4; text-decoration: underline;">caffeine</a></span> as a cup of coffee. The caffeine makes it a mild stimulant.</p>
<p>The second substance, theobromine, like caffeine, can boost your concentration and mood. Theobromine is also found in chocolate. This substance also works to lower blood pressure.</p>
<p>A third active ingredient in Guayusa is L-theanine. This amino acid has one great distinguishing feature: it counteracts that bloated feeling that caffeine can sometimes give. The combination of these substances results in what users describe as 'uplifting effects and clear focus'.<br/><br/><img src="../../files/index.html@id=1641.jpg&amp;file=Ilex+guayusa.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"><br/><br/></img></p>

<h2 id="kwaliteitsverlies">How to store magic mushrooms without compromising quality</h2>
<p>Storing magic mushrooms without compromising quality means trying to keep their flavour, texture and active ingredients as stable as possible. Factors such as moisture, temperature, light and air play a major role in this.</p>
<p>By storing magic mushrooms correctly, you minimise the risk of spoilage and slow down the breakdown of active compounds.</p>
<h2 id="na-oogst">Why do magic mushrooms change after harvesting?</h2>
<p>After harvesting, magic mushrooms remain biologically active. Fresh magic mushrooms contain a lot of moisture and can therefore deteriorate quickly. You’ll often notice this because they become limp, change colour or feel less firm to the touch <span style="color: #1649f5;"><a href="it.svg.html#bron3" style="color: #1649f5;">[3]</a></span>.</p>
<p>After a successful harvest, it is important to store your magic mushrooms correctly. Would you like to know how to preserve their flavour, texture and quality as well as possible? Then read our guide on <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1649f5;"><a href="../../../../../guide/self-growing-and-deeper-understanding/why-grow-yourself.html" style="color: #1649f5; text-decoration: underline;">‘Why grow your own?’</a></span>.</p>
<p>This process can begin within just a few days if magic mushrooms are stored incorrectly or kept in conditions that are too warm.</p>
<h2 id="potency">Potency of magic mushrooms: meaning and impact on quality</h2>
<p>Potency refers to the strength or effect of magic mushrooms. This is linked to substances such as psilocybin and psilocin.</p>
<p>These substances are sensitive to external influences. Heat, oxygen and light, in particular, can cause the composition to change gradually <span style="color: #1649f5;"><a href="it.svg.html#bron2" style="color: #1649f5;">[2]</a></span>. As a result, the effects may become less predictable over time.</p>
<p><img alt="dried mushrooms in a preserving jar" height="563" src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=3083.jpg&amp;file=dorge+paddo+in+weckpot.jpg" style="width: 1000px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h2 id="verkeerde-opslag">Consequences of incorrect storage of magic mushrooms</h2>
<p>If magic mushrooms are stored incorrectly, they can spoil more quickly. Their smell, taste and texture may also change.</p>
<p>Excess moisture increases the risk of mould or bacterial growth. Furthermore, active compounds can break down more quickly if magic mushrooms are exposed to heat, air or direct sunlight for prolonged periods.</p>
<h2 id="waarom-belangrijk">Why is proper storage of magic mushrooms important?</h2>
<p>Proper storage has a direct impact on the quality and shelf life of magic mushrooms.</p>
<h3>Preventing spoilage</h3>
<p>Fresh magic mushrooms contain a lot of moisture and are therefore susceptible to mould and bacteria, especially at room temperature.</p>
<h3>Preserving texture and flavour</h3>
<p>Poorly stored magic mushrooms can become limp, wet or rubbery. Storing them in a cool, dry place helps to preserve their texture better.</p>
<h3>Preserving potency</h3>
<p>Light, oxygen and heat accelerate the breakdown of active compounds <span style="color: #1649f5;"><a href="it.svg.html#bron1" style="color: #1649f5;">[1]</a></span>. A stable storage environment helps to minimise this breakdown as much as possible.</p>
<h2 id="bewaren">How can you store magic mushrooms without compromising quality?</h2>
<p>The best storage method depends on how long you want to keep your magic mushrooms. Fresh magic mushrooms require a different approach to dried ones.</p>
<h3>Storing fresh magic mushrooms (short term)</h3>
<p>Fresh magic mushrooms are best stored in the fridge. It is best to use a paper bag or kitchen roll to absorb any excess moisture.</p>
<ul>
<li>Keep fresh magic mushrooms cool</li>
<li>Do not use sealed plastic packaging</li>
<li>Make sure they stay dry</li>
<li>Avoid condensation</li>
</ul>
<p>Stored this way, fresh magic mushrooms will usually keep for between 3 and 7 days.</p>
<h3>Storing dried magic mushrooms (long-term)</h3>
<p>Dried magic mushrooms have a much longer shelf life than fresh ones. Store them in an airtight container, such as a glass jar, in a cool, dark place.</p>
<ul>
<li>Store dried magic mushrooms in a dry, dark place</li>
<li>Use a jar with a tight-fitting lid</li>
<li>Avoid heat and moisture</li>
<li>Open the packaging as little as possible</li>
</ul>
<p>The drier magic mushrooms are when stored, the more stable they usually remain. Well-dried magic mushrooms can keep for months.</p>
<h3>What you should avoid</h3>
<p>The following conditions can quickly reduce the quality of magic mushrooms:</p>
<ul>
<li>Storing them in plastic without ventilation</li>
<li>Exposure to direct sunlight</li>
<li>Storing in warm places</li>
<li>Contact with moisture or condensation</li>
</ul>
<p>These factors increase the risk of spoilage and can cause potency to decline more rapidly.</p>
<p><img alt="Vacuum-packed dried mushrooms have a longer shelf life." src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=3082.jpg&amp;file=gevacumeerde+droge+paddo&amp;%23039%3Bs.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h2 id="let-op">Key points to bear in mind when storing magic mushrooms</h2>
<p>When storing magic mushrooms, these factors are the most important:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Moisture:</strong> too much moisture increases the risk of mould growth.</li>
<li><strong>Temperature:</strong> storing them in a cool place helps to slow down spoilage.</li>
<li><strong>Light:</strong> storing in the dark helps to keep the active compounds stable.</li>
<li><strong>Air:</strong> prolonged exposure to oxygen can accelerate degradation.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="bedorven">How can you tell if magic mushrooms have gone off?</h2>
<p>You can often tell that magic mushrooms are no longer good by noticeable changes in smell and texture.</p>
<ul>
<li>A musty or rotten smell</li>
<li>A slimy or extremely soft texture</li>
<li>Visible patches of mould</li>
<li>Excessive moisture or discolouration</li>
</ul>
<p>If in doubt, it’s best not to use the magic mushrooms.</p>

<h2 id="what-is-psilocybin">What is psilocybin in magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>Magic mushrooms naturally contain psychoactive compounds. The best-known of these is<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psilocybin" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">psilocybin</a></span>. Psilocybin is a so-called prodrug: once inside the body, it is converted into psilocin, the compound primarily responsible for the psychedelic effects.</p>
<p>Psilocin affects receptors involved in mood, perception, and consciousness. As a result, magic mushrooms can cause changes in thoughts, emotions, perception of time, visual perception, and feelings of connectedness.</p>
<p>However, it is difficult to predict exactly how potent a mushroom will be. As with many other natural products, the composition can vary depending on the species, growing conditions, harvest time, and drying method. Even mushrooms from the same species can differ in potency.</p>
<p>With home-grown mushrooms, you therefore never know exactly how much psilocybin they contain. However, research has been conducted on different species. The well-known species<span> </span><em>Psilocybe cubensis</em><span> </span>contains an average of about 1% psilocybin, while<span> </span><em>Psilocybe azurescens</em><span> </span>is known as a more potent species.</p>
<p><img align="middle" alt="Different psilocybin mushroom species and their natural variation in potency" src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=1271.jpg&amp;file=Psilocybe+Family.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h2 id="fresh-vs-dried-magic-mushrooms">What is the difference between fresh and dried magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>The biggest difference between fresh and dried magic mushrooms is their moisture content. Fresh mushrooms consist largely of water. Dried mushrooms are lighter, more compact, and have a much longer shelf life, while containing more active compounds per gram.</p>
<p>For that reason, fresh and dried magic mushrooms cannot be compared on a 1-to-1 basis. As a general rule of thumb, 10 grams of fresh mushrooms is considered roughly equivalent to 1 gram of dried mushrooms. This remains only a general guideline, as the exact potency can vary depending on the species and batch.</p>
<h2 id="dosing-magic-mushrooms">How do you dose fresh and dried magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>If you want to use<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../../../../mushrooms.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">magic mushrooms</a></span>, it is important to carefully consider your dosage beforehand. Especially if you have little or no experience, it is advisable to start with a low dose. A low dose gives you the opportunity to discover how your body and mind respond.</p>
<p>For beginners, around 1 gram of dried magic mushrooms or 10 grams of fresh magic mushrooms is often mentioned as a cautious starting dose. Only once you know how you respond and feel comfortable with the experience should you consider trying a higher dose on another occasion.</p>
<p>For beginners, it can be difficult to estimate in advance what effect a certain amount will have. If you first want to better understand how these experiences can develop, read<span> </span><span style="color: #1649f5;"><a href="../../../../../guide/consciousness-and-experience/what-to-expect.html" style="color: #1649f5;"><u>what to expect</u></a></span>.</p>
<p>The right dosage depends on, among other things:</p>
<ul>
<li>your experience with psychedelics;</li>
<li>your personal sensitivity;</li>
<li>your mood at the time;</li>
<li>the environment in which you use them;</li>
<li>the mushroom species;</li>
<li>whether the mushrooms are fresh or dried;</li>
<li>what you hope to achieve with the experience.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="effects-per-dose">What effects can you expect at different dosages?</h2>
<p>To determine the right dose, you need to take the potency of the mushrooms into account. A low dose of approximately 1 gram of dried magic mushrooms or 10 grams of fresh magic mushrooms usually produces mild effects. This is not a microdose, as you may still notice visual, emotional, or physical effects.</p>
<p>With a medium dose, for example around 2 grams of dried or 20 grams of fresh magic mushrooms, the effects can become more noticeable. Users often describe feelings of euphoria, altered perception, increased creativity, and thought patterns that take unexpected directions.</p>
<p>With a higher dose, for example around 3.5 grams of dried or 35 grams of fresh magic mushrooms, the experience can become much more intense. You may experience strong visuals, your sense of time may change, and the experience can be emotionally or mentally profound. These kinds of doses are mainly suitable for experienced users who know what they are doing.</p>
<h2 id="set-and-setting">Why are set and setting so important?</h2>
<p>Besides dosage, set and setting are crucial factors that determine the quality of your experience. "Set" refers to your internal state: how you feel, what you expect, and whether you are mentally calm. "Setting" refers to your surroundings: the location, the people around you, and the atmosphere in which you use magic mushrooms.</p>
<p>It is important to make sure you are in a safe<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../../../89/5-things-you-can-do-wrong-when-tripping.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">environment</a></span><span> </span>where you feel comfortable. Magic mushrooms can amplify emotions. If you are not feeling well mentally, this may negatively influence the experience.</p>
<p>Especially for beginners, it is advisable to have a sober<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../../../81/the-best-tips-for-trip-sitters.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">trip sitter</a></span><span> </span>nearby. This person does not use any substances themselves and can calmly offer support if the experience becomes too intense.</p>

<h2 id="scale">Why is an accurate scale important?</h2>
<p>One of the most common mistakes when using magic mushrooms is estimating the dosage. Because the potency of mushrooms can vary and small differences in weight can affect the experience, using an accurate scale is highly recommended.</p>
<p>If you want to enjoy magic mushrooms and other mind-altering substances safely, using a good, accurate<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../../../140/how-to-choose-the-best-scale.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">scale</a></span><span> </span>is a wise choice. It allows you to weigh your dose accurately and helps prevent accidentally taking more or less than intended.</p>
<p>Especially with dried magic mushrooms, even a small difference in weight can have a significant impact on the intensity of the experience.</p>
<h2 id="use-and-storage">How do you use and store magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>As mentioned earlier, you can choose between fresh and dried magic mushrooms. Fresh mushrooms are easy to use when you grow them yourself using a grow kit. After harvesting, they can be used immediately.</p>
<p>The disadvantage is that fresh magic mushrooms have a limited shelf life. In the refrigerator, they usually remain fresh for only a few days up to a maximum of two weeks. That is why many people choose to<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../../../82/the-best-way-to-dry-your-magic-mushrooms.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">dry</a></span><span> </span>their magic mushrooms.</p>
<p>Dried magic mushrooms take up less space, are easier to dose, and can remain usable for years when stored properly. If you would like to learn more about storage, you can also read how long a<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../../../128/how-long-can-i-store-a-grow-kit-fresh-or-dried-magic-mushrooms.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">grow kit, fresh, or dried magic mushrooms can be stored</a></span>.</p>
<h2 id="safety">How can you use magic mushrooms as safely as possible?</h2>
<p>Responsible use starts with good preparation. Because magic mushrooms can alter perception and emotions, it is wise to think about your dosage, surroundings, and expectations in advance.</p>
<p>Since the potency of magic mushrooms can vary by species and even by harvest, many people are unsure how to get started responsibly. If you want to understand how to gradually increase your dosage, read<span> </span><span style="color: #1649f5;"><a href="https://www.24high.com/en/safe-use-knowledge-and-context/dosage-and-careful-building" style="color: #1649f5;"><u>building up your dosage carefully</u></a></span>.</p>
<ul>
<li>Start with a low dose if you have little experience.</li>
<li>Use an accurate scale.</li>
<li>Choose a safe and familiar environment.</li>
<li>Do not take an additional dose while the first effects are still coming on.</li>
<li>Consider having a sober trip sitter during your first experience.</li>
<li>Choose a mushroom species that matches your level of experience.</li>
</ul>
<p>If you are new to magic mushrooms, it may be wise to first find out which<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../../../134/which-magic-mushroom-species-are-suitable-for-a-first-experience.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">magic mushroom species are suitable for beginners</a></span>.</p>

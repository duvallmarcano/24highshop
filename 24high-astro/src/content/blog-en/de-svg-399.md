Being high causes mental euphoria and creativity, usually through THC-rich cannabis. Being stoned, on the other hand, causes physical languor and relaxation, often through cannabis with more CBD. So the difference is in the type of drug and its effect on body and mind.</p>
<h2>Tripping: What psychedelics do to your brain and consciousness</h2>
<p>Tripping refers to a profound change in consciousness, usually caused by psychedelic drugs such as <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../../../../398/the-history-of-lsd-from-laboratory-to-controversy.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">LSD</a></span>, <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../../../../../mushrooms/24-growkits-mushroom.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">magic mushroom</a></span><span style="color: #0907f5;"></span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"></span><span> or </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../../../../../smartshop/26-mescaline-cacti.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">mescaline</a></span>. A trip often affects your senses, emotions and self-image in profound ways.</p>
<h3>How do you recognise a psychedelic trip?</h3>
<ul>
<li>Visual and auditory hallucinations</li>
<li>Altered perception of time and space</li>
<li>Intense emotional experiences</li>
<li>Spiritual or philosophical insights</li>
<li>Feeling of ego loss (dissolution)</li>
</ul>
<p>Research shows that substances such as LSD interact with the 5-HT2A receptor in the serotonin system, leading to these changes in perception and consciousness.</p>

<h2>Being high: Euphoria and mental stimulation by THC</h2>
<p>Being high is an experience that typically occurs when using cannabis with a high THC content. The effect is often mentally stimulating, with feelings of euphoria, increased creativity and altered sensory perception.</p>
<h3>What happens in your head when you are high?</h3>
<ul>
<li>Bouts of laughter or euphoria</li>
<li>Increased sensitivity to sound, light and taste</li>
<li>Altered perception of time and maintenance of consciousness and cognition</li>
</ul>
<p>THC activates the endocannabinoid system in the brain, especially in areas involved in reward, memory and motor skills. According to<a href="https://www.cdc.gov/cannabis/health-effects/brain-health.html">CDC</a>, this temporarily<span> affects </span>your mood and perception.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Being stoned: The physical relaxation of cannabis</h2>
<p>Being stoned is often associated with a heavier physical state. This effect usually occurs when using indica-dominant cannabis or cannabis with a higher CBD ratio. It causes deep relaxation and often fatigue.</p>
<h3>This is what it feels like to be stoned - physical effects of cannabis</h3>
<p>Being stoned is often described as a state of deep physical relaxation, which usually occurs after using indica-dominant cannabis strains or cannabis with a higher CBD content. Users often experience a languid or drowsy feeling, with both thinking and movement feeling slower. Coordination may be reduced, leading to slowed movements and the feeling of heavy limbs, as if the body is less easy to control. In addition, drowsiness is common, especially when cannabis strains with calming terpenes such as myrcene are used. Many people find that their motivation decreases, which can make it difficult to stay active or perform everyday tasks.</p>
<p>While these effects are seen as pleasant by some, they are not for everyone. In situations where alertness is important - such as driving, studying or working with machines - the use of cannabis is not recommended. The extent to which you experience these effects depends on your body weight, tolerance, environment and the type of cannabis you use.</p>

<h2>Safe tripping: Tips for a responsible experience</h2>
<p>Especially when using psychedelics, or tripping, proper preparation is essential. The environment in which you are tripping plays a big role in this. Make sure you are in a safe and familiar setting, preferably at home or in a place where you feel completely at ease. Avoid combining psychedelics with other substances such as alcohol or stimulant drugs; this can cause unpredictable and sometimes dangerous interactions. Planning to trip for the first time? Then it is wise to have a sober and reliable 'trip sitter' present, someone who will stay with you, guide you and can help if the experience becomes overwhelming. It is also important to take mental vulnerability seriously: people with a history of anxiety disorders or psychosis are strongly advised against using psychedelics, as the chances of negative experiences or triggering symptoms are significantly higher.</p>
<h2>Smart choice: Understand the differences and tailor your experience</h2>
<p>Knowing the difference between tripping, being high and being stoned will help you make more conscious choices when using psychoactive substances. Whereas a high is brief and mentally stimulating, a trip offers a profound, sensory and emotional journey. Being stoned, on the other hand, brings mostly physical relaxation and languor. By understanding these nuances, you can reduce risks and better tailor your experience to your intention.</p>

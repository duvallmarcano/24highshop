<h2>Why grow cannabis indoors?</h2>
<p>It might be weird to grow cannabis indoors, but it can still work well. Why? Simply because it is easier for you as a grower. After all, you can control the light, nutrition and water yourself, which is not possible if you plant outdoor seeds in your garden. It is also a good alternative if you live in a cold climate or if your garden gets little sunlight. Moreover, it is not visible to others that you are growing weed if you do it in your home.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Tips for choosing the best indoor cannabis strains</h2>
<p>Choosing the best weed varieties to grow indoors depends on your personal preferences and available space. Consider, for example, the height of the plant, flowering time and desired effects. Autoflower varieties are often a good choice for beginners thanks to their simplicity and fast cycle. Yield per square metre can also be an important factor in selecting the best cannabis strain for your growing space.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>Can you grow all cannabis strains indoors?</h2>
<p>Not every strain is suitable for indoor cultivation. Some grow too fast towards the light source, making them too big. Fortunately, there are strains developed specifically for indoor growing that stay compact.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>The best weed varieties for indoor growing</h2>
<p>These are the best weed varieties for growing indoors:</p>
<h3>1. Candy Kush Express Fast Flowering</h3>
<p>Candy Kush Express Fast Flowering is a hybrid (40% Sativa, 60% Indica) with distinct effects of both types. It contains around 18% THC and little CBD. It flowers quickly (about eight weeks) and grows to a maximum height of 100 cm.</p>
<p><em>Note: The listed THC/CBD values and effects are indicative and not medically validated. Consult a professional for reliable advice.</em></p>
<h3>2. Fat Banana Automatic</h3>
<p>Fat Banana Automatic is an autoflower that is fast-growing and strong. The cycle from seed to harvest takes around ten weeks, with a yield of 450 g/m². The plant grows to around 100 cm tall.</p>
<h3>3. Bubblegum XL</h3>
<p>Bubblegum XL grows slightly taller (around 140 cm) and gives a high yield of around 500 g/m². The variety is known for its sweet taste and smell.</p>
<h3>4. Northern Light Automatic</h3>
<p>Northern Light Automatic is a popular classic. The Automatic variety has a fast flowering time and a high yield (around 550 g/m²). Plants grow to an average height of 120 cm.</p>
<h3>5. Lowryder Auto</h3>
<p>When space is at a premium, Lowryder Auto is ideal. It stays compact (around 40 cm tall) and is very fast, with a flowering time of around 56 days. Yield is around 35g/m².<br/><br/><img alt="Frequently Asked Questions About Best Cannabis Strains" src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=2658.png&amp;file=8+jul+2025,+16_30_33.png" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h2>Frequently asked questions about the best indoor varieties</h2>
<div>
<div>
<h3>How much light do indoor cannabis plants need?</h3>
<div>
<p>Most growers use a light schedule of 18 hours of light and 6 hours of darkness during the growing phase. For the flowering phase, switch (for photoperiod varieties) to 12 hours of light and 12 hours of darkness. Autoflower varieties can often just stay on 18/6.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<h3>How long does it take to grow weed indoors?</h3>
<div>
<p>On average, indoor growing takes between 8 and 12 weeks, depending on the strain. Autoflowers are usually faster (8-10 weeks), while photoperiod varieties can take slightly longer.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div>
<h3>Should I flush my plants before harvest?</h3>
<p>Yes, flushing your plants (just watering without feeding) in the last 1-2 weeks helps to remove excess nutrients from the soil and the plant, improving the flavour and purity of your harvest.</p>

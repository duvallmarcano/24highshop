It is made by infusing cannabis in butter, which releases the psychoactive components such as THC and CBD and absorbs them into the fat. This process results in a butter that differs from regular butter in both taste and effect. cannabisbutter is often used in various recipes to make edible cannabis products or <span style="color: #0907f5;"><a href="../../headshop/113-cannabis-edibles.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">cannabis food</a></span> that can be used both recreationally and medicinally.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>How to make your own cannabisbutter?</h2>
<p>Making cannabisbutter is a fairly simple process that requires some time and care. Here is a step-by-step guide to making your own cannabisbutter:</p>
<h3>Supplies:</h3>
<ul>
<li>1 cup unsalted butter</li>
<li>1 cup of water</li>
<li>7-10 grams of cannabis (dried and finely chopped)</li>
<li>Fine sieve or cheesecloth</li>
<li>Jar for storage</li>
</ul>
<h3>Steps:</h3>
<ol>
<li>
<p><strong>Decarboxylating the Cannabis</strong>:</p>
<ul>
<li>Preheat your oven to 115°C.</li>
<li>Spread the finely ground cannabis evenly on a baking tray covered with baking paper.</li>
<li>Bake the cannabis for about 30-40 minutes, stirring occasionally. This process, known as decarboxylation, activates the THC in the cannabis.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Melt the Butter</strong>:</p>
<ul>
<li>Melt the butter along with the water in a saucepan over low heat. The water helps regulate the temperature and prevents the butter from burning.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Adding the Cannabis</strong>:</p>
<ul>
<li>Add the decarboxylated cannabis to the melted butter.</li>
<li>Let the mixture simmer on low heat for 2-3 hours. Stir occasionally to prevent the cannabis from sticking to the bottom of the pan.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Sieve the Mixture</strong>:</p>
<ul>
<li>Pour the mixture through a fine sieve or cheesecloth into a bowl to remove the solid parts of the cannabis.</li>
<li>Press on the solids with a spoon to squeeze out as much butter as possible.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Cool and Store</strong>:</p>
<ul>
<li>Let the cannabisbutter cool to room temperature before placing it in an airtight container in the fridge.</li>
<li>You can also freeze the cannabisbutter for longer storage.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>

<h2>How do you store cannabisbutter?</h2>
<p>Storing butter correctly is essential to maintain its freshness and potency. Here are some tips for storing your cannabisbutter:</p>
<p>Refrigerator:</p>
<ul>
<li>Store the cannabisbutter in an airtight container in the fridge. This helps to keep the butter fresh and avoid absorbing odours from other foods.</li>
<li>Weed butter can stay fresh in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.</li>
</ul>
<p>Freezer:</p>
<ul>
<li>For longer storage, you can keep cannabisbutter in the freezer. Use a freezer-proof container or divide the butter into portions and wrap them in plastic wrap.</li>
<li>Frozen cannabisbutter can last up to 6 months or more without losing quality.</li>
</ul>
<p>Protection from light:</p>
<ul>
<li>Store the butter in a dark container or wrap the container in aluminium foil to minimise light exposure. Light can reduce the potency of the THC and other cannabinoids.<br/><br/></li>
</ul>

<h2>Cannabisbutter recipes</h2>
<p>Now that you've made your own cannabisbutter and stored it correctly, it's time to enjoy the fruits of your labour! Here are some simple and delicious cannabis <span style="color: #0907f5;"><a href="../286/cooking-with-weed-5-tasty-and-easy-recipes-with-cannabis.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">butter/cannabis recipes</a></span> to get you started:</p>
<h3>Cannabisbutter Cookies</h3>
<p><strong>Ingredients:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>1 cup of cannabisbutter</li>
<li>1 cup of sugar</li>
<li>1 cup brown sugar</li>
<li>2 eggs</li>
<li>1 teaspoon vanilla extract</li>
<li>3 cups flour</li>
<li>1 teaspoon baking powder</li>
<li>1/2 teaspoon salt</li>
<li>2 cups chocolate chips</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Instructions:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Preheat the oven to 175°C.</li>
<li>Mix the butter, sugar and brown sugar until creamy.</li>
<li>Add the eggs and vanilla and mix well.</li>
<li>Mix the flour, baking powder and salt in a separate bowl.</li>
<li>Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and stir until a smooth dough forms.</li>
<li>Stir in the chocolate flakes.</li>
<li>Spoon the dough onto a baking tray and bake for 10-12 minutes.</li>
</ol>
<h3>Cane Butter Brownies<br/><br/><img src="../../files/index.html@id=2138.jpg&amp;file=wietbrownie.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></h3>
<p><strong>Ingredients:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>1/2 cup of cannabisbutter</li>
<li>1 cup sugar</li>
<li>2 eggs</li>
<li>1 teaspoon vanilla extract</li>
<li>1/3 cup cocoa powder</li>
<li>1/2 cup flour</li>
<li>1/4 teaspoon salt</li>
<li>1/4 teaspoon baking powder</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Instructions:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Preheat oven to 175°C.</li>
<li>Melt the butter and mix with sugar, eggs and vanilla.</li>
<li>Add the cocoa powder, flour, salt and baking powder and mix well.</li>
<li>Pour the mixture into a greased baking tin.</li>
<li>Bake for 20-25 minutes until the brownies are firm.</li>
</ol>

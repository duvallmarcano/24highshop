<h2>What is Royal Rapé?</h2>
<p>Royal Rapé is a traditional plant-based snuff derived from the Amazon region. It is carefully formulated by indigenous tribes, often during ceremonial processes involving sacred plants and tobacco (such as <em>Nicotiana rustica</em>). Rapé is known for its spiritual application within rituals that focus on intention and respect. Royal Rapé is usually used in a calm, mindful setting and is intended as a tool for personal reflection or meditation.</p>
<p><strong>Please note that</strong> this product is not medicine and has no therapeutic purpose. Always use it with respect and awareness. If in doubt, consult a doctor or specialist.<br/><br/></p>

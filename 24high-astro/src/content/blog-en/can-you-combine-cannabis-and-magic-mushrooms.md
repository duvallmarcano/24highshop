Using several types of drugs at the same time (polydrug use) is not uncommon. You can do this for several reasons, but usually it is to induce a more powerful high.</p>
<p>Magic mushrooms can be combined with cannabis, but also with, for example, mescaline and MDMA. Some substances can, when taken together with others, provide special and pleasant effects. <h2>Why should you combine cannabis and magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>The mixing of magic mushrooms and weed is done for several reasons:</p>
<ul>
<li>First of all, most people want to experience a more potent high.</li>
<li>It can be done to alleviate some negative effects of one drug with another. Certain positive effects can also be enhanced.</li>
<li>Finally, you can take a second psychedelic drug to ensure that the first drug continues to work for longer.</li>
</ul>
<p>With magic mushrooms and weed, they are both natural substances. <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0000ff;"><a href="https:/en/mushrooms" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">Magic mushrooms</a></span> contain the mind-altering substance psilocybin and cannabis contains THC (tetrahydrocannabinol). These substances affect the brain and cause a high. But many people find that the effects complement each other beautifully and synergistically enhance each other.</p>

<h2>How do psilocybin and cannabidoids influence each other?</h2>
<h3>The influence of cannabis</h3>
<p>Cannabis contains <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0000ff;"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahydrocannabinol" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">THC</a></span> (psychedelic substance), but also many other cannabidoids, such as CBD, CBC and CBG. Some of these cannabidoids can dampen the effect of THC. When using cannabis, you may experience a euphoric high, which can also be sedating at the same time. How intense your trip feels depends on the cannabidoid ratio of the strain. Some negative side effects of cannabis are its effect on motor skills and reflexes. You can also get a dry mouth and your memory works less well.</p>
<h3>The effects of magic mushrooms</h3>
<p>The active ingredient in magic mushrooms is psilocybin. Tripping on magic mushrooms can be quite intense: you can get visual hallucinations, experience strong emotions, you can experience the passage of time differently and get spiritual visions. Magic mushrooms have a stimulating effect on your body and mind.</p>
<p>The use of magic mushrooms can cause you to suffer from fears or delusions. You may become nauseous, feel lethargic, or become disoriented. Other possible side effects include restlessness, shivering, sweating or getting a higher body temperature.</p>
<p><img align="middle" src="../../files/index.html@id=605.png&amp;file=cannabisysetasmagucas,cannabisenpaddo,shrooms.png" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<div> </div>
<h2>Combine magic mushrooms and cannabis</h2>
<p>Combining magic mushrooms with cannabis can have benefits. The psychedelic substances provide a synergistic effect. A more intense high is created, which gives you more mental stimulation. But the weed has a calming effect and smoothes out the trip.</p>
<p>You can use this effect however you want: take weed at the beginning of the trip, so that you stay calmer and are not immediately drawn into violent hallucinations. If you take it right at the end, you will return to earth in peace. Taking weed when the trip is at its peak can provide more powerful visual effects and emotions. By consciously using cannabis, you can influence the phases of the trip as it suits you.</p>
<p>Magic mushrooms and weed are both able to stimulate the production of dopamine (the happiness hormone). On the one hand you can notice this by the fact that you feel motivated, but it can also lead to repetitive thoughts. If you take both substances at the same time, these effects can become more intense.</p>
<h3>Choose the right cannabis strain</h3>
<p>If you want to use magic mushrooms and cannabis together, you can take the strain into account which you choose. A <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0000ff;"><a href="https:/en/seedshop/122-high-thc-strain" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">strain </a></span>that contains a lot of THC and les CBD gives more energy and can have a positive effect on your mood. <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0000ff;"><a href="../../seedshop/120-cbd-seeds.html" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">Indica strains</a></span> contain more CBD and thus you can benefit from the calming effect of this cannabinoid. This can help to reduce possible anxiety and nausea.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Combining magic mushrooms with…</h2>
<p>There are other combinations that can be made with magic mushrooms that provide a nice high. They combine especially well with mescaline and MDMA.</p>
<p><img align="middle" src="../../files/index.html@id=604.png&amp;file=sanpedrocactus,mescaline,mescalina.png" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h3>Magic mushrooms and mescaline</h3>
<p>Not only magic mushrooms produce a psychedelic substance, also some <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0000ff;"><a href="https://www.verywellmind.com/what-is-mescaline-4155320" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration: underline;">cacti</a></span> do. The substance is called mescaline and can be found in, for example, the Peyote and San Pedro cactus. If you want to combine the substance, it is best to take mescaline first and then the mushrooms. You then get a slow onset trip that lasts for a long time. Take the magic mushrooms when you feel that you are getting a nice trip and after half an hour you can feel the strength increase.</p>
<h3>Magic mushrooms with MDMA</h3>
<p>MDMA is the active ingredient in XTC (3 .4 methylenedioxymethamphetamine). It belongs to the group of amphetamines and is known for the reliable high it gives you. Magic mushrooms are more unpredictable in that regard. By combining the two resources, you get the best of the two. The MDMA has a stabilizing effect on the trip. You get an intense trip with powerful visuals and a euphoric feeling.</p>
<p>If you want to combine magic mushrooms with another psychedelic, be aware that the results are almost impossible to predict. Whether you have a good or bad trip depends on many factors, such as the two resources you use, your own mood and the environment. Always be careful and make sure you are not alone when experimenting with mind-altering drugs.</p>

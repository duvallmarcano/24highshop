<h2 id="wat-is-microdosing">What exactly is microdosing with truffles?</h2>
<p>Magic truffles are sclerotia of mushrooms that contain psilocybin. The body converts this substance into psilocin (<span style="color: #1649f5;"><a href="it.svg.html#bron2" style="color: #1649f5;">2</a></span>). Microdosing means taking a very low dose, usually about 5–10% of a recreational dose. This means you will not hallucinate, which normally does occur at higher doses. A typical microdose of fresh truffles is usually around 0.5 to 1 gram, although it depends on your sensitivity and the type. Important: a true microdose is sub-perceptual. If you are clearly “under the influence,” the dose is probably too high.</p>
<h2 id="waarom-microdosing">Why do people choose microdosing?</h2>
<p>Microdosing with truffles is becoming increasingly popular, partly for the following reasons:</p>
<ol>
<li>Focus and productivity: some users experience improved concentration, less procrastination, and a smoother flow of thoughts.</li>
<li>Emotional balance: users sometimes report less rumination, more emotional openness, and a more stable mood.</li>
<li>Creativity: microdosing is often associated with more flexible thinking and the ability to make new connections.</li>
<li>Personal development: through subtle changes in self-reflection, microdosing can contribute to insight into behavioral patterns.</li>
</ol>
<p>It is important to mention that scientific research on microdosing currently shows mixed results (<span style="color: #1649f5;"><a href="it.svg.html#bron3" style="color: #1649f5;">3</a></span>). Some studies show slight improvements in mood and cognitive flexibility, while others indicate that expectation effects, or placebo, play an important role.</p>

<h2 id="dosering-schema">How to microdose with truffles: dosage and schedule</h2>
<p>If you want to try microdosing with truffles, we recommend the following steps:</p>
<h3 id="begin-laag">1. Start low</h3>
<p>It is important to start with a low dose. Because you may not yet have experience with the effects of <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1649f5;"><a href="../../../../../mushrooms/16-magic-truffles.html" style="color: #1649f5; text-decoration: underline;">magic truffles</a></span>, it is often recommended to begin with approximately 0.5 grams of fresh truffles. Increase the dosage only if necessary and do so gradually. Preferably use an accurate scale so you can measure the amount as precisely as possible.</p>
<h3 id="volg-schema">2. Follow a schedule</h3>
<p>In addition, it is advisable to follow a schedule when microdosing with truffles. For example, take a microdose and then take two days off. This helps prevent tolerance.</p>
<p>A well-known schedule within microdosing is the Fadiman protocol. With this method, you take a microdose on day 1, followed by two days without intake. This makes it easier to distinguish between effects and rest days.</p>
<div style="display: flex; gap: 30px; flex-wrap: wrap; margin-top: 20px;">
<div style="flex: 1; min-width: 260px; background: #f5f7ff; padding: 20px; border-radius: 12px;">
<h4 style="margin-top: 0;">Commonly chosen schedule</h4>
<ul>
<li>Take a microdose on day 1</li>
<li>Then include two rest days</li>
<li>Observe how you feel on dose and rest days</li>
<li>Prevent tolerance from building too quickly</li>
<li>Maintain a consistent rhythm to better evaluate effects</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div style="flex: 1; min-width: 260px; background: #fff8f2; padding: 20px; border-radius: 12px;">
<h4 style="margin-top: 0;">Fadiman protocol explained</h4>
<ul>
<li>Day 1: you take a low microdose</li>
<li>Day 2: no dose, but pay attention to after-effects</li>
<li>Day 3: again no dose, allowing your body to rest</li>
<li>Day 4: you start again with a microdose</li>
<li>This schedule is often used to microdose consciously and in a structured way</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>
<h3 id="logboek">3. Keep a logbook</h3>
<p>Record your mood, focus, energy, sleep, and any side effects in a logbook. This allows you to more objectively evaluate whether microdosing has an effect. Be as honest as possible in your notes.</p>
<h2 id="criteria">Important criteria: what should you pay attention to?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Expectations:</strong> microdosing is not a miracle cure. The effects are subtle and vary per person.</li>
<li><strong>Set and setting:</strong> even at low doses, mindset and environment play a role, so ensure a good set and setting.</li>
<li><strong>Mental health:</strong> people with a history of psychosis, bipolar disorder, or severe anxiety disorders are advised not to experiment without medical supervision.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Step-by-step plan for cleaning your bong</h2>
<p>Cleaning your <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../../../../../headshop/6-bongs-glass.html" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">bong</a></span> is a good idea. Below is a handy step-by-step plan for this.<br/><br/></p>
<h3>Step 1: Empty and rinse your bong</h3>
<p>First of all, it is important to empty your bong completely. Pour all the dirty water into the sink and then rinse the bong thoroughly with warm water. This will remove loose dirt and actually prepare your bong for the big clean.<br/><br/></p>
<h3>Step 2: Disassemble your bong</h3>
<p>To clean your bong properly, it is wise to take it completely apart. To do this, you remove the individual parts. We recommend you keep the smallest parts in a plastic bag, in which you can also clean them if necessary. This way, you ensure that all components are thoroughly cleaned.</p>
<p><img alt="bong cleaning tips" src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=2558.jpg&amp;file=bong.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h3>Step 3: Create your cleaning solution</h3>
<p>When cleaning your bong, experience experts say you do well to create your own cleaning agent. Have no fear, this is not difficult. For example, mix a lot of isopropyl alcohol with baking soda, or baking soda. This cleaning solution has an abrasive effect, removing resin buildup. Would you rather use a natural cleaner? Then mix lemon juice with hot water. This remedy often does not work as well, according to users.<br/><br/></p>
<h3>Step 4: Shake the bong</h3>
<p>The next step is to pour your cleaning agent into the bong. Make sure the mixture reaches all the spots with resin. Then cover the openings of the bong, for example with a cloth or your hands. Next, it is time to give your bong a good shake for a few minutes. This will probably remove all the deposits.<br/><br/></p>
<h3>Step 5: Clean the small parts</h3>
<p>Now it's time to focus on the small components. You can use the same cleaning agent for this. Once you have put the small components in a plastic bag, pour the mixture into it. Then seal the bag and shake it slightly. This may not clean all the dirty spots. Scrub these spots with a cotton swab or bottle brush, for example.<br/><br/></p>
<h3>Step 6: Rinse the bong thoroughly</h3>
<p>Now that you have basically cleaned all parts of your bong, it is a must to rinse them all again. This way, you will remove all residues of the cleaning agent and also its smells.<br/><br/><img alt="bong water pipe clean" src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=2562.jpg&amp;file=cleaning+bong.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h3>Step 7: Wash down with washing-up liquid</h3>
<p>If you want to clean your bong even more thoroughly, you may want to wash the parts again. To do this, use warm water and a little washing-up liquid. This will remove residual grease and odours, but users say this is not always necessary.<br/><br/></p>
<h3>Step 8: Reassemble the bong</h3>
<p>You can dry all parts of the bong yourself with a tea towel or kitchen paper, but you can also let them air dry. Then reassemble the bong, which you will probably have done in no time. Cleaning your bong is not difficult at all, according to most users, and it doesn't take long in principle. So do this chore regularly to make your smoking experience as healthy and delicious as possible!<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Health risks of a dirty bong</h2>
<p>A dirty bong can lead to fungal and bacterial growth, posing the following risks:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Respiratory infections:</strong> Inhalation of fungi can cause bronchitis and pneumonia <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://www.cdc.gov/mold-health/about/" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">(source: CDC</a></span>).</li>
<li><strong>Gastrointestinal problems:</strong> Bacteria such as E. coli can cause nausea and diarrhoea <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/search/all/?term=E.+coli+" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">(source: NIH</a></span>).</li>
<li><strong>Allergic reactions:</strong> Mould spores can aggravate allergies and asthma <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/mold-allergy/symptoms-causes/syc-20351519" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">(source: Mayo Clinic</a></span>).</li>
</ol>
<p>Regular cleaning prevents these risks and ensures a better smoking experience.<br/><br/></p>

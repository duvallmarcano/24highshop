<h2>The benefits of cannabis food and drinks</h2>
<p>Why choose cannabis edibles, such as a <a href="../../article/0208185-dutch-hash-brownie.html"> <span style="color: #1806ff;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;">brownie</span> </span></a> or <span style="color: #000000;">cannabis milk chocolate</span>? Eating or drinking hemp-based products has many advantages.</p>
<h3>1. Essential amino acids</h3>
<p>The hemp plant is rich in all kinds of valuable nutrients, some of which are very important for staying healthy. Hemp seeds contain 9 essential amino acids. The body can make a number of amino acids (these are the building blocks of proteins) itself, but there are 9 that cannot. So you have to get it from your diet. If you eat something with hemp seeds, you will immediately receive all 9 essential amino acids. And it is special that hemp contains these amino acids, because normally you will only find them in animal products. So it is also a good idea to eat cannabis edibles if you are a vegetarian.</p>
<h3>2. Easily digestible proteins</h3>
<p>The proteins contained in cannabis are easy for the body to absorb. Some protein-rich products, such as nuts and grains, contain phytic acid. This acid makes it difficult for your body to absorb the proteins. Hemp does not contain this acid. Of all edible plants (some three million), the proteins from hemp are the easiest for the human body to digest.</p>
<h3>3. Good source of essential fatty acids.</h3>
<p>Hemp and hemp oil are rich in essential fatty acids. These fatty acids are also essential (just like the previously mentioned amino acids), because they are indispensable for our health and our body cannot produce them on its own. Do not create yourself. Hemp contains omega-3, 6 and 9 fatty acids and then also in the perfect proportions. These fatty acids are important for the health of the cardiovascular system, to keep blood pressure in balance, and also for the health of the nerves and brain.</p>
<h3>4. Contains CBD</h3>
<p>The hemp plant contains cannabinoids, one of which is CBD (<span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;"><a href="../98/what-other-cannabinoids-are-there-in-full-spectrum-cbd-oil.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">cannabidiol</a></span>). Just like THC, CBD has an influence on the endocannabinoid system. This system regulates a large number of processes in your body, such as your sleep-wake rhythm, your appetite, the hormone balance and more. Sometimes this system gets upset. CBD can ensure that you get back into balance. So you can take cannabis edibles with CBD for various complaints, such as headaches, inflammation, anxiety, stress or, for example, PMS.</p>

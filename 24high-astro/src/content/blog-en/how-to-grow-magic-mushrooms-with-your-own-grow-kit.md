<h3><strong>What is a grow kit?</strong></h3>
<p>A grow kit is also called a <span style="color: #000000;">growkit </span> and contains everything you need to grow your own magic mushrooms successfully. Which one you buy naturally depends on what type of mushroom you want to grow. Hence grow kit differs in handling and content, but usually contains a <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #2301fc;"><a href="../../mushrooms/24-growkits-mushroom.html" style="color: #2301fc; text-decoration: underline;">mushroom growkit</a></span> in any case:</p>
<ul>
<li>Plastic cultivation tray with ready-to-use substrate (nutrient medium)</li>
<li>Injected with mushroom spores</li>
<li>Manual</li>
</ul>

<h3><strong>Hygiene is very important</strong></h3>
<p>When growing mushrooms it is essential that you work very hygienically. Always work with clean hands or even with gloves, sneeze, and cough into your elbow and wear a mask. If you don't do this, you could endanger your mushroom harvest.</p>
<p>The magic mushrooms are extremely susceptible to competition in the grow kit. When you sneeze, cough or even exhale, very small fungal spores can end up in the tank with very fertile substrate. The fungi feel comfortable under these perfect conditions and there is a good chance that these unwanted fungi will emerge before your magic mushrooms.</p>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #2301fc;"><a href="../109/these-fungi-cause-fungal-infection-on-your-mushrooms-growing-kit.html" style="color: #2301fc; text-decoration: underline;">An infestation</a></span> with a “wrong mold” is fortunately easy to spot. The <span style="color: #000000;">mycelium</span>, the underground hyphae are white. When you discover a different color in the substrate, you know that your grow kit is contaminated. Before you start growing, we recommend that you use the <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #2301fc;"><a href="../../mushrooms/77-grow-supplies.html" style="color: #2301fc; text-decoration: underline;">Magic Mushroom Starterkit</a></span> to purchase. <h3><strong>Start growing magic mushrooms</strong></h3>
<p>Growing with a grow kit is very easy, but for a successful harvest it is very important to follow the manual carefully. The cultivation itself will certainly not take you hours, but above all patience and the right timing. The tutorial of the <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #2301fc;"><a href="../../mushrooms/202-all-in-one-growkits.html" style="color: #2301fc; text-decoration: underline;">Magic Mushroom Golden Teacher</a></span> for example looks like this :</p>
<ul>
<li>Remove the grow kit, plastic bag and paperclip from the box. Remove the lid from the grow kit (keep lid well). Rinse the lid under running water. You will need it again later.</li>
<li>Place the grow kit without a lid in the supplied plastic bag. Slide the opening of the bag under the bottom with the micro perforation upwards. The opening of the bag is now largely closed. You don't have to worry about air circulation.</li>
</ul>
<p>Now you have to wait until you see small balls grow. Is that the case? Then you have to put the bag upright, so don't water and open it a little. This way the water can evaporate. The first magic mushrooms will be visible and will pop out of the <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #2301fc;"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mycelium" style="color: #2301fc; text-decoration: underline;">mycelium</a></span> after about 1 week. For a successful harvest it is very important to harvest before the first spores fall.</p>

<h2>What is maca and where does it come from?</h2>
<p>The maca root (Lepidium meyenii) is native to the Andes. It has been found in Peru and surrounding areas since the 14th century. Here it was used as a medicinal plant, but also in food. In fact, maca is one of the few medicinal plants that has a genuinely delicious flavour: a little nutty. It does resemble hazelnut, with a little imagination. Maca root is also used today in <span style="color: #1621d2;"><a href="../../../../331/traditional-chinese-medicine-what-is-it-and-what-are-the-best-remedies.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">traditional Eastern medicine</a></span>.</p>
<p>Maca usually refers to the supplement made from the maca root. This supplement is available as powder and capsules, but it is also incorporated into multivitamins and other healthy products.<br/><br/><br/><img src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=2054.jpg&amp;file=maca.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>

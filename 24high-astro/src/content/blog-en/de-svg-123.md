<h2>Relaxation exercise for sleeping #1: Meditation</h2>
<div>By practicing a short meditation or <a href="../../../../59/how-the-herbs-from-nature-help-you-relax.html"><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;">herbs</span></a> you can let your body completely relax. This relaxation in your body and mind allows you to surrender to sleep that you need so desperately. You can create rest through this meditation. This relaxation exercise will help you to fall asleep. This meditation to fall asleep works as follows:</div>
<ul>
<li>Lie down in bed on your back, stretch your legs and lay your arms along your body.</li>
<li>Close your eyes and start the relaxation exercise.</li>
<li>Start with three deep breaths.</li>
<li>Breathe for 3 seconds, hold your breath for 1 second, and then breathe for 5 seconds.</li>
<li>After that, you'll adjust the rhythm of your breathing.</li>
<li>Now breathe 3 seconds in and 5 seconds off, without holding your breath.</li>
<li>Keep concentrating on your breathing.</li>
<li>Count the number of breaths.</li>
<li>Once you are at the tenth breathing you start again at zero.</li>
<li>Make sure you don't focus on counting, it's not important that you perform the counting exactly correctly.</li>
</ul>
<p>After a few minutes, you will feel that your body is relaxing. As if a calmness comes over you. Let all the thoughts you have loose, just feel the calmness in your body. Feel how relaxing and relaxed your body is now. Keep lying down and when the sleep comes, you allow it. You are now in a relaxed, quiet state that makes you ready to fall asleep. <br/><br/></p>

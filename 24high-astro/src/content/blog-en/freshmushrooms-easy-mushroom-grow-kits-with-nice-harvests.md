<h2>Why go for a kit from Fresh Mushrooms?</h2>
<p>Fresh Mushrooms psilocybe grow kits are simple mushroom grow kits. They are especially so <em>easy</em> since they do not require watering until after the first harvest. This means that upon delivery, the grow kit can be taken out of the box and set up without adding water. Once you have the kit in your home, remove it from the packaging and place it in the grow bag provided. Then it's just a matter of waiting a while until you are treated to a rich harvest of good mushrooms.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>Tips for the Fresh Mushrooms growkits</h2>
<p>When growing magic mushrooms like those from Fresh Mushrooms, there are a number of things you can take into account if you want to generate an optimal harvest. Read our tips from preparation to trips here.</p>
<h3>Work sterile</h3>
<p>If you want to make sure your mushroom harvest is successful, work in a super clean environment. Wash your hands thoroughly and wear gloves before working with the cultivation kit, keep pets, food and bacterial sources away as much as possible. Why is this important? Mushrooms are very susceptible to contamination by unwanted invisible guests. This is because they grow on a so-called substrate, which the mushrooms feed on. That substrate is very susceptible to infection, partly because mushrooms have to grow in a fairly warm environment. And of course, that substrate is not just a tasty meal for your mushrooms. Want to know the best way to work sterile? Read all about it here: <span style="color: #1621d2;"><a href="../167/how-do-you-work-sterile-when-growing-mushrooms.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">How do you work sterile when growing mushrooms? </a></span></p>
<h3>The first harvest</h3>
<p>Setting up the grow kit is super easy: remove the lid of the grow kit, place it in the grow bag, fold the bag around the kit and wait for the mushrooms to grow. No water is needed for the first harvest. This makes it even easier, especially for beginners, to start that first mushroom growing journey.</p>
<h3>After harvest: drying and storing your Fresh Mushrooms</h3>
<p>As you read earlier, a Fresh Mushrooms grow kit will give you a hefty portion of mushrooms. Probably more than you can finish in one go. Now, mushrooms are quite perishable; they remain, but only edible for a short time. Fortunately, you can solve this by drying your mushrooms. Properly dried mushrooms can be kept for up to a year. So a sustainable source of psychedelic trips and microdoses!

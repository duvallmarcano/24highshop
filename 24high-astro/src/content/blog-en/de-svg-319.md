<h2>Traditional uses of herbs for skin and health</h2>
<p>Herbs have long been <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #2107fe;"><a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK92761/" style="color: #2107fe; text-decoration: underline;">used</a></span> to improve skin - all over the world. Often prescribed as oral medicines, but also as skin treatments.</p>
<h3>Ashwagandha</h3>
<p><span style="color: #2107fe;"><a href="../../../../210/what-is-ashwagandha-and-how-does-it-work.html" style="color: #2107fe; text-decoration: underline;">Ashwagandha</a></span> is a well-known supplement, but you can also release it on your skin. When you take it, you improve your immune system and reduce stress. But you can also apply it to your skin. This is because it works anti-inflammatory, rejuvenating and fights bacteria. Do you suffer from acne, scars, dry or red skin? Ashwagandha is your friend! Make a mixture of this herb and a moisturiser or oil and treat your face to the healing powers of this tuber.</p>

Because your outer protective layer also appears to have cannabinoid receptors, which cannabis-based oil can bind to. <p>Tea tree oil can do it all: heal wounds, disinfect, fight bacteria, fungi and dandruff, reduce rashes and much more.</p>

<p>This fragrant plant works against fungi, inflammation and bacteria. As a result, you can fight superficial infections and pimples. It also leaves your pores open, so less junk gets in. <h3>Oregano</h3>
<p>Oil made from oregano clears away harmful radicals and metals by containing antioxidants. With oregano oil, you can soothe irritated skin, heal wounds and combat ageing. It also works for pimples. <h3>Lemon</h3>
<p>Lemon is a great fruit. Very low in calories and sugar, a delicious flavouring and also packed with healthy substances. If you make a skin oil with this citrus fruit, you can count on even more <span style="color: #2107fe;"><a href="https://jklst.org/index.php/home/article/view/218" style="color: #2107fe; text-decoration: underline;">bonuses</a></span>.

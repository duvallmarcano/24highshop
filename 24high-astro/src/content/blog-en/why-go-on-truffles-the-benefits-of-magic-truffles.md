<h2>First advantage of magic truffles: legal trip buds</h2>
<p>A first major advantage of magic truffles is the main reason why users prefer them over magic mushrooms. Truffles are legal in the Netherlands, while magic mushrooms are not these days. And that is actually quite special, because truffles literally grow up together with those illegal mushrooms. But underground; they are a so-called sclerotium of magic mushrooms. And sclerotium is another difficult word for mushroom package. As long as truffles stay nice and underground, they are heartily legal.</p>
<p>However, the sale of fresh mushrooms has been banned since 2008. Yet both magic mushrooms and truffles contain the same psychoactive ingredients: <span style="color: #1621d2;"><a href="../176/what-are-psilocybin-truffles.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">psilocybin</a></span>.</p>
<p>If you are someone who likes to stick to the law and enjoy tripping just as much, then of course the legality of truffles is a huge advantage. No need to worry about that during your inner journeys! By the way, growing mushrooms yourself is legal. Just like growing truffles, of course.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Benefit #2: Sharper brain by microdosing with truffles</h2>
<p><img src="../../files/index.html@id=1839.jpg&amp;file=brain.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<p>In mega-productive Silicon Valley, they have long since discovered it: with small doses of psilocybin, you boost your brain power. By using a fraction of a regular trip-portion, the brain appears to be stimulated in certain places. Among other things, users experience more focus after <span style="color: #1621d2;"><a href="../10/why-microdosage-with-magic-truffels.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">microdosing with magic truffles</a></span>, making it easier to focus on one task, or several at once. They also regularly report a feeling of <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><span><a href="../227/how-to-get-into-the-flow-with-microdosing.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">flow</a></span></span>: being completely absorbed in what you are doing and forgetting the time and world around you for a moment.</p>
<p>So how do you microdose? You can choose to buy a portion of truffles and split it into 10-20 microdoses, but you can also buy ready-made microdosing kits. Of these, all you have to do is take a pre-dosed portion each day you take it, and you're done. A third option is a microdosing stack. This is a pre-dosed amount of truffles plus additional ingredients that are good for your brain. An example is the <span style="color: #1621d2;"><a href="../265/wise-rootz-the-new-microdosing-with-truffles-cocoa-and-medicinal-mushrooms.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">Wize Rootz microdosing stack</a></span> with extra additives like cocoa and medicinal mushrooms in addition to truffles.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Benefit #3: life-changing</h2>
<p><img src="../../files/index.html@id=1841.jpg&amp;file=explore.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<p>The main reason people use truffles is the trip that follows a medium to large dose. What can you expect like this? First: changes in your consciousness, in the broadest sense of the word. Time, space, senses, colours, smells: everything can seem (very) different. Also count on visual effects, from classic visuals in certain trippy patterns and at high doses quite spiritual experiences. You often feel more connected to the world around you, like nature or the people you're with. Another nice benefit of truffles: you will often feel extra happy to even euphoric.</p>
<p>Many first-time users tripping on truffles or similar substances describe the experience as life-changing. You can definitely call this a benefit of magic truffles, if you are open to this.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Benefit #4: Truffles possibly effective for mental complaints</h2>
<p><img alt="ptss" src="../../files/index.html@id=1840.jpg&amp;file=20241+(1).jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>

Microdosing psilocybin, for example, has been shown to help people with <span style="color: #1621d2;"><a href="../192/does-microdosing-psilocybin-magic-mushrooms-or-truffles-help-with-ptsd.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">PTSD</a></span> get ahead, but those suffering from anxiety can also benefit.<br/><br/></p>

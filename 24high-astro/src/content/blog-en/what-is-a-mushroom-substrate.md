<h2>What is a mushroom substrate?</h2>
<p>If you are going to grow mushrooms without a grow kit, you will have to make and add a substrate yourself. Normally, a mushroom gets its nutrition from nature, but since you want to grow your own mushrooms in this case, it needs to be able to get the nutrition from somewhere else. And that's what the substrate is for: it provides energy and nutrition to the mycelium. This is the network of <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #2c06f9;"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypha" style="color: #2c06f9; text-decoration: underline;">fungal threads</a></span> of the mushroom you want to grow. From the mycelium, fruiting bodies eventually grow above ground. So a healthy mycelium is very important if you are going to grow your own mushrooms!<br/><br/><img src="../../files/index.html@id=1444.jpg&amp;file=myceliummushrooms.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"><br/><br/></img></p>
<h2>Growing with mushroom substrates</h2>
<p>Before use, you should always prepare the substrates. This includes adding moisture and other nutrients. You also need to pasteurise and sterilise it so that unwanted fungi, bacteria and other substances leave your substrate. This can cause your harvest to be a disaster, and of course you don't want that! You can sterilise your substrate under high pressure in a pressure cooker at a temperature of 120 °C or higher. For pasteurisation, you heat the substrate at 65-85°C for several hours.</p>
<h3>Substrates you can use</h3>
<p>If you want to grow mushrooms yourself, you will have to experiment a lot in the beginning. You can use different substrates to find out which gives the best results. There is no ideal substrate, but we have a list of substrates that give good results:</p>
<ul>
<li>Rice flour and vermiculite</li>
<li>Coconut fibre and vermiculite</li>
<li>Coffee grounds</li>
<li>Wood block</li>
<li>Live trees</li>
<li>Manure</li>
<li>Soya hulls</li>
<li>Sawdust or wood chips</li>
<li>Straw</li>
</ul>
<p>You get the best substrate for growing mushrooms with coconut fibre and vermiculite. Straw and manure can also give good results. If you mix coconut and vermiculite 1:1, you will have enough nutrients to grow your mushrooms well. This doesn't mean that the other options don't work well; they too produce good results.</p>

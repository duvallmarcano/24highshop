<h2>What is Blue Lotus?</h2>
<p>Blue Lotus originally grows in the Nile River and was already used by the ancient Egyptians during religious ceremonies and festivities because of its relaxing effects. Today, Blue Lotus is often used as a natural alternative to reduce stress and improve mood.</p>
<h3>Blue Lotus in Ancient Egypt</h3>
<p>During ceremonial gatherings, the flowers were often soaked in wine or made into tea. This preparation was then shared among participants to promote a sense of calm, harmony and spiritual connection. It is said that even pharaohs used Blue Lotus to enhance meditation and clarity during important decisions.</p>
<p>Blue Lotus was also often depicted in Egyptian art, murals and in hieroglyphics, as a symbol of rebirth, purity and spiritual awakening.</p>

<h2>Effects of Blue Lotus</h2>
<p>The effects of Blue Lotus are subtle, calming and sometimes slightly euphoric. Users report a sense of relaxation, elevated mood and sometimes enhanced dream activity. This makes the herb ideal for meditative purposes and relaxation.</p>

<h2>Safety and dosage</h2>
<p>Blue Lotus is generally safe when used responsibly. The recommended dosage is between 2 and 5 grams per use. High doses may cause mild nausea or drowsiness. Always consult your doctor before using Blue Lotus, especially if you are on medication or pregnant.</p>
<h2>Is Blue Lotus legal in the Netherlands?</h2>
<p>Blue Lotus is legal in the Netherlands. It does not fall under the Opium Act and can therefore be freely used and traded. Nevertheless, we always advise responsibility and caution when using it.</p>

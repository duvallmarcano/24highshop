<h2>What is the difference between herbs and aromatherapy?</h2>
<p>First of all, it is important to know the difference between herbs and aromatherapy:</p>
<ul>
<li>When you<span> use </span><span style="color: #0907f5;"><a href="../../healthshop.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">herbs</a></span> in meditation, you take them, for instance in the form of tea or supplements. You can additionally vapourise the plants or burn dried herbs, which is possible with <span style="color: #0907f5;"><a href="../../article/4203867-white-sage-smudge-xl.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">sage</a></span>, for example.</li>
<li>Meditating with <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../407/aromatherapy-do-essential-oils-really-work.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">aromatherapy</a></span><span> works </span>slightly differently, as here the essential oils of plants are diffused with a diffuser. You can also possibly inhale the essential oils directly.</li>
</ul>
<p>So both methods use plants, but the method of administration and the effect on the body and mind differ.<br/><br/></p>
<p><img alt="herbs and aromatherapy" src="../../files/index.html@id=2600.jpg&amp;file=therapy+herbal.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h2>How herbs can help with meditation</h2>
<p>Herbs can have many different effects. For instance, they can calm your nervous system, but can also activate it. We list below a few <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../429/enhance-your-meditations-with-these-herbs.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">herbs that many people use to meditate</a></span><span> and </span>what they do.</p>
<ol>
<li style="list-style-type: none;">
<ol>
<li><strong>Valerian: </strong><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../../article/8101452-valerian-indian-elements.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">Valerian</a></span> is described by many users as calming. Scientific research, such as this study <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17145239/" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">(Source</a></span>), also suggests valerian can increase the availability of gamma-aminobutyric acid in the brain, which promotes relaxation.</li>
<li><strong>Ashwagandha: </strong><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://www.24high.com/en/article/3211126-whole-root-ashwagandha-together" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">Ashwagandha</a></span> is considered an adaptogenic herb. Users often experience it as supportive for stress. A scientific study <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31517876/" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">(Source</a></span>) shows that it can potentially contribute to an increased sense of calm during meditation, although the effects vary from person to person.</li>
<li><strong>Passion flower: </strong>Passion flower is often used for its possible calming effects. According to one study <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11679026/" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">(Source</a></span>), it may have similar effects to oxazepam, but such findings are still limited and require more research.</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ol>

<h2>How aromatherapy can help with meditation</h2>
<p>With aromatherapy, the scents come into direct contact with the limbic system in the brain via your nose, which is the part that processes memories and emotions. As a result, scents can quickly affect your mood. Researchers recommend using the essential oils below during aromatherapy meditations:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Sandalwood oil: </strong>Sandalwood has a warm scent and is traditionally used in spiritual practices. Some people experience deeper meditation as a result. Research <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6171454/" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">(Source</a></span>) suggests a relaxing effect, but this experience is subjective and depends on personal preference. This<span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"> <a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6171454/" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">research</a></span><span> suggests </span>that sandalwood oil can contribute to greater relaxation and deeper concentration.</li>
<li><strong>Lavender oil:</strong><span> Lavender oil </span>is usually the most commonly used for increased relaxation. This scent is known for its anxiety-reducing properties. Therefore, this <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3612440/" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">research</a></span><span> shows </span>that lavender oil can help significantly reduce stress and anxiety.</li>
<li><strong>Bergamot oil:</strong><span> Bergamot oil </span>has a relaxing effect and also provides a fresh feeling. This scent is usually perfect for people who feel agitated while meditating. This is partly because bergamot oil can lower cortisol<span> levels </span>, according to this <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/pharmacology/articles/10.3389/fphar.2015.00036/full" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">research</a></span>.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Which works better for meditations?</h2>
<p>Whether you meditate better with herbs or aromatherapy depends largely on your personal preferences and needs. If you struggle to quiet your mind, aromatherapy is often better. This is also the case if you are easily tense. Herbs, on the other hand, may be better if you suffer from long-term stress or insomnia. It may be even better to combine both options. For this, start your session by diffusing a calming scent and support your daily well-being with herbs. This way, your meditative experience may become deeper and more stable.</p>

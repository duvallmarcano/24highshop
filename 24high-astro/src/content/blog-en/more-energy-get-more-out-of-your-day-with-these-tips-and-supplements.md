<h2>Practical tips for more energy</h2>
<p><img src="../../files/index.html@id=1770.jpg&amp;file=energy-exercising.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<p>Before you reach for supplements, there are some things you should do to work on your energy levels. This is because these will have long-term effects, which is of course what you would like most.</p>
<h3>1: Exercise</h3>
<p>Most people don't get enough exercise. And that's why you may suffer from fatigue. With exercise, you stimulate circulation and make it easier for your body to keep your energy levels high and stable. For example, you suffer less from energy dips after eating. Our government's exercise guidelines are a good guide:</p>
<ul>
<li>At least 150 minutes per week of moderate-intensity exercise. Think walking and cycling.</li>
<li>Work on your muscles and motor skills twice a week or more, for example with strength training.</li>
<li>Avoid prolonged periods of inactivity such as working sitting down for hours at a time or spending whole weekends in bed.</li>
<li>The more exercise, the better. While respecting your body, of course.</li>
</ul>
<h3>2: Sleep</h3>
<p>Having enough energy during the day starts with a good night's sleep. If you sleep poorly, see how you can improve with these tips:</p>
<ul>
<li>Go to bed on time at set times and establish a certain routine. This way, your body and brain know that it is time to sleep at a certain time.</li>
<li>Avoid stimuli and incentives late in the day and evening, such as intense conversations, bright lights, strenuous activities, heavy meals, gaming and Netflix.</li>
<li>Focus on 7-9 hours of sleep per night.</li>
<li>Provide a calm and clean sleep environment. Dimmed lights, fresh sheets and keeping busy pets and snoring partners out of bed can make a world of difference.</li>
<li>Fall asleep easier and make longer nights with supplements such as <span style="color: #2819df;"><a href="../216/which-herbs-promote-sleep.html" style="color: #2819df; text-decoration: underline;">sleep-promoting herbs</a></span>. <span style="color: #2819df;"><a href="../../article/8101452-valerian-indian-elements.html" style="color: #2819df; text-decoration: underline;">Valerian</a></span>, <span style="color: #2819df;"><a href="../../article/8101431-passion-flower-5x-extract.html" style="color: #2819df; text-decoration: underline;">Passion Flower</a></span> and melatonin can help.</li>
</ul>
<h3>3: Nutrition</h3>
<p>Nutrition is the energy source that keeps us going. Eat enough, healthy and varied food. A healthy diet consists of sufficient protein, at least 250 grams of vegetables and 2 pieces of fruit, whole grain cereals, legumes and healthy fats. Avoid highly processed foods, excessive consumption of sugar and large amounts of alcohol. These have been proven to have a negative impact on your energy. Also pay attention to how your body reacts to certain foods and meals. Keep a diary of what you eat, what time and how much and how tired you are before and after eating.<br/><br/>Besides your diet, you can look into supplements that will improve your health and brain function in the long run. Read this article to make the right choice: <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #2819df;"><span><a href="../256/the-best-supplements-to-support-your-health.html" style="color: #2819df; text-decoration: underline;">The best supplements to support your health</a></span></span>.</p>
<h3>4: Rest</h3>
<p>You may actually need more moments of rest during the day, to recharge for the rest of your to-do's. If you strain yourself too much and for too long, your energy simply runs out. Stress can also make you tired. Take a critical look at your schedule and commitments. Are you doing enough things that give you energy, besides all the must-do's? And are you taking enough breaks? A good way to take a break could be a short walk. That way you clear your head for a while and work on your fitness as well.<br/><br/></p>

It has been used for centuries because of its benefits for circulation, mental alertness and memory. More energy and focus is the result.</p>
<h3>Caffeine</h3>
<p>Who doesn't get through the day without coffee? <span style="color: #2819df;"><a href="../213/inconspicuous-drugs-caffeine.html" style="color: #2819df; text-decoration: underline;">Caffeine</a></span> is what makes this black goodie a great energy booster. Coffee is also healthy: several cups a day have proven positive effects on your health. Tea also contains caffeine, among several other healthy compounds. This makes tea a healthy choice for more futility in the short and long term. Green tea also has additional health benefits, so feel free to drink one or two cups of that every day.</p>

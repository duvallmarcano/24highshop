<h2><strong>Liquid cultures? What are they?</strong></h2>
<p>Considering growing magic mushrooms with <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #2301fc;"><a href="../../mushrooms/33-spores-shrooms.html" style="color: #2301fc; text-decoration: underline;">spores</a></span>? Then you have probably heard of liquid cultures, or ‘liquid culture’ or ‘LC’. This involves a closed, sterile environment in which mycelium grows in a mixture of water and sugar. There are many different ways to obtain magic mushrooms. You can buy them on the internet, look for them in nature, or grow them with the liquid culture method. Liquid culture is a sterilized, nutritious solution for  <span style="color: #000000;">growing </span>magic mushrooms. Usually this is a mixture of water and different types of sugars. This mixture can develop mycelial growth if inoculated with spores. This can be done with <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #2301fc;"><a href="../../mushrooms/205-spore-syringes.html" style="color: #2301fc; text-decoration: underline;">syringes</a></span> or vials.<u> </u></p>
<p>Normally, mycelial growth occurs when spores are inoculated onto the <span style="color: #000000;">substrate.</span> When the germination process is complete, the mycelium develops the <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #2301fc;"><a href="../76/what-types-of-substrate-are-there-for-growing-magic-mushrooms.html" style="color: #2301fc; text-decoration: underline;">substrate,</a></span> and then it colonizes. The difference with the liquid culture method is that the development of the mycelium takes place in the liquid culture pot or a bottle. This has several advantages.<br/><br/></p>
<p><img alt="spore syringe" src="../../files/index.html@id=715.png&amp;file=spore syringe+mushroom's.png" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"><br/><br/></img></p>
<h3><strong>What are the benefits of liquid cultures?</strong></h3>
<p>Nice that liquid culture, but what are the advantages of this method? We looked it up for you. The two most mentioned advantages are the fast result and the unlimited mycelium growth. Read more about it below:</p>
<ul>
<li>You have much faster results</li>
</ul>
<p>The grower no longer has to wait for the spores to germinate. This means that the incubation time has been reduced by at least one week. This of course saves a lot of time.</p>
<ul>
<li>There is unlimited mycelial growth</li>
</ul>
<p>1 cc of spore solution is sufficient to create an unlimited amount of liquid culture medium. Once the mycelium starts to develop, it continues to develop.</p>

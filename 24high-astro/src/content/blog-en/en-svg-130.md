<h2>What does transcendence mean?</h2>
<p><span style="color: #2c06f9;"><a href="https://www.sloww.co/transcendence-maslow/" style="color: #2c06f9; text-decoration: underline;">Transcendence</a></span> literally means 'transcending' or 'climbing'. It is about transcending our 'little self' so that we can make more meaning out of life. Rising above everyday reality so that we can experience a higher reality - or actually great mystery. An experience that many consider meaningful in their lives.<br/><br/><br/><img src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=1538.jpg&amp;file=budhisme.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"><br/><br/></img></p>
<h2>What is transcendence?</h2>
<p>With transcendence, we try with so-called Transcendental Meditation techniques, to transcend thought and to naturally discover our inner self. The techniques are based on ancient knowledge and it has now been proven that this is the most efficient and natural way to discover your inner self.</p>
<p>It is a very special experience to experience once. It connects us to reality: that everything is connected and interdependent. An experience where your 'little self' takes a step back and individual successes are no longer the norm, but values like unity and connectedness.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>How to transcend?</h2>
<p>You can achieve the deep peace and connectedness that transcendence brings in several ways. There are several techniques, but one of the best-known ways is meditating with a mantra. This is a form of depth meditation where you don't have to do much yourself. You are guided by the mantra, and all you have to do is let go of control to discover your inner self.<br/><br/></p>

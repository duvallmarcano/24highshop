<p class="text-center"><img alt="BEST SUPPLEMENTS AND HERBS FOR RUNNERS" height="333" src="../../../images/image.php@n=468-1759756908.jpg&amp;f=news&amp;w=500&amp;h=500" style="width: 500px; max-width: 100%; max-height: 500px; height: auto;" width="500"/></p><div style="padding: 0 10px;"><p>Running is great for body and mind, but it also demands a lot from your muscles, energy and recovery capacity. Whether you are training for a 5 km or a marathon, the right supplements and herbs can give you that extra bit of support. Below are the best supplements and herbs for runners that have been scientifically researched and work well in practice.</p>
<h2>1. Creatine: for strength, recovery and explosiveness</h2>
<p><strong>Effect:</strong><span> Increases </span>phosphocreatine stores in your muscles, improving ATP production during intensive exercise. This can lead to increased strength, faster recovery and possibly muscle growth.</p>
<p><strong>Dosage:</strong><span> 3 </span>- 5 grams per day, including on rest days.</p>
<p><strong>Source: </strong><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14636102/" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition</a></span></p>
<h2>2. Caffeine: for more focus and less fatigue</h2>
<p><strong>Effect:</strong><span> Increases </span>alertness, reduces fatigue and can improve running performance.</p>
<p><strong>Dosage:</strong><span> 3-6 </span>mg per kilogram of body weight, taken 30-60 minutes before exercise.</p>
<p><strong>Source: </strong><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26526788/" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">European Journal of Sport Science</a></span></p>
<h2>3. Beet juice: for improved blood flow and endurance</h2>
<p><strong>Effect:</strong><span> Rich </span>in nitrates that are converted into nitric oxide. This promotes blood flow and oxygen uptake - important for endurance athletes.</p>
<p><strong>Use:</strong><span> 200 </span>- 500 ml beetroot juice, 2 - 3 hours before training.</p>
<p><strong>Source: </strong><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20345979/" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">Journal of Applied Physiology</a></span></p>
<p><img alt="beetroot juice for more endurance" height="563" src="../../files/index.html@id=2745.jpg&amp;file=bietensap.jpg" style="width: 1000px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h2>4. Rhodiola Rosea: against fatigue and stress during exercise</h2>
<p><strong>Effect:</strong><span> Adaptogenic </span>herb that<span> can </span>reduce <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../../article/4211242-stress-aid-complex-together.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">fatigue</a></span> and support endurance: useful during intense or stressful training periods.</p>
<p><strong>Source: </strong><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21635876/" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">Phytomedicine</a></span></p>
<h2>5. Omega-3 fatty acids: for reduced muscle soreness and inflammation</h2>
<p><strong>Effect:</strong><span> EPA </span>and DHA have anti-inflammatory effects and can reduce muscle pain.</p>
<p><strong>Use:</strong><span> 1000 </span>- 2000 mg combined EPA/DHA per day.</p>
<p><strong>Source: </strong><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://www.health.harvard.edu/staying-healthy/the-truth-about-fish-oil" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">Harvard Health Publishing</a></span></p>
<h2>6. Ashwagandha: natural booster for strength and stamina</h2>
<p><strong>Effect:</strong><span> Adaptogen </span>that <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../449/how-can-you-improve-your-stamina-5-tips.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">can improve</a></span> strength, recovery and <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../449/how-can-you-improve-your-stamina-5-tips.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">endurance</a></span>.</p>
<p><strong>Source: </strong><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26609282/" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition</a></span></p>
<h2>7. L-carnitine: supports fat burning and endurance performance</h2>
<p><strong>Effect:</strong><span> Supports </span>the transport of fatty acids to the mitochondria, where they are used as energy - good for endurance runners.</p>
<p><strong>Source: </strong><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11897894/" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">American Journal of Clinical Nutrition</a></span></p>
<p><img alt="L-carnitine supports fat burning" height="563" src="../../files/index.html@id=2746.png&amp;file=l-carnitine.png" style="width: 1000px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h2>8. Magnesium: prevents cramps and supports muscle recovery</h2>
<p><strong>Effect:</strong><span> Helps </span>with muscle contraction and relaxation. Deficiency can lead to cramps or fatigue.</p>
<p><strong>Use:</strong><span> 200 </span>- 400 mg daily, preferably in the evening.</p>
<p><strong>Source: </strong><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24049963/" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">Magnesium Research</a></span></p>
<h2>9. Zinc: for recovery, resistance and energy production</h2>
<p><strong>Effect:</strong><span> Supports </span>the immune system and recovery processes. Deficiency may reduce performance and resistance.</p>
<p><strong>Source: </strong><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18806122/" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">Sports Medicine</a></span></p>
<h2>10. Cranberry extract: natural antioxidant for muscle protection</h2>
<p><strong>Effect:</strong><span> Contains </span>antioxidants that help reduce muscle fatigue. Research shows that intake before training can improve performance.</p>
<p><strong>Source: </strong><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34724532/" rel="noopener" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">Nutrients</a></span></p>
<h2>Practical experience of runners: what really works?</h2>
<p>Many runners find that caffeine is particularly effective on short intervals, while beetroot juice and omega-3 work better on long endurance runs. Always test one supplement at a time to see how your body reacts.</p>
<p><img alt="running and supplements, what works?" height="563" src="../../files/index.html@id=2744.jpg&amp;file=kruiden+en+hardlopen.jpg" style="width: 1000px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h2>Recommendations for runners: smart, safe and effective</h2>
<p>Supplements can support performance and recovery, but never replace basics like nutrition, rest and hydration. Consider:</p>
<ul>
<li>Choose a maximum of two supplements at a time and observe the effect.</li>
<li>Always combine with balanced nutrition and adequate sleep.</li>
<li>Choose reliable brands with verified quality.</li>
<li>Consult with doctor or dietician when using medication or health conditions.</li>
</ul>

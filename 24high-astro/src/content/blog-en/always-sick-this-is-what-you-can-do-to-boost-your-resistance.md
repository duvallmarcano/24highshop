<h2>This is what you can eat to get sick less often</h2>
<p>One of the most important ways to boost your resistance and keep it at a nice level is *trump* a healthy and balanced diet. And shockingly, this is where things often go wrong, as most people do not eat enough healthy things. For instance, we eat too few vegetables, fruit and fibre en masse. So be the first to take a critical look at your diet. Tip: eat healthier easily with the Nutrition Centre's app. In it, you keep track of what you eat and immediately see where you can do better. A second tip: eat as much unprocessed food as possible. So fresh products and as few packaged things as possible with all kinds of additives. These are usually (but not always!) less good for your immune system.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Sleep = less often ill</h2>
<p>Those who sleep poorly or little have a much lower resistance. During sleep, your body repairs itself and important immune cells are produced. Make your sleep a real priority. Try to get at least 7-8 hours of sleep every night and make sure your sleep environment is clean and restful. Avoid using screens and other stimuli before bedtime and make sure your bedroom is dark, cool and quiet. And remember: good sleep starts during the day. Your activities, diet, <span style="color: #1621d2;"><a href="../216/which-herbs-promote-sleep.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">supplements</a></span> and caffeine intake all affect the quality of your sleep.<br/><br/><img src="../../files/index.html@id=1991.jpg&amp;file=always+sick.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h2>Sicker more often due to stress</h2>
<p>Don't underestimate stress! Prolonged exposure to stress can weaken your immune system, making you more susceptible to disease. As difficult as it may sometimes be, it is really important to reduce stress as much as possible. Try relaxation techniques such as <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><a href="../285/meditating-better-this-is-what-you-can-do-to-get-more-out-of-your-meditation.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">meditation</a></span>, yoga or deep breathing exercises. Take regular breaks throughout the day and make sure you have enough relaxation and fun in your life. Also take a moment to actively look for things that stress you out in daily life, such as people, your physical environment or in your work. And above all, apply our <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><span><a href="../252/unwind-the-best-tips.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">calming tips</a></span></span>.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Walking and exercise for better immunity</h2>
<p>Regular exercise will strengthen your immune system and improve your resistance. Really try to get at least 30 minutes a day of moderate to intense exercise. This can be walking, cycling, swimming or any other activity you enjoy. Walking is a good move anyway, literally and figuratively. This will clear your head, stabilise your sugar levels and make you overall fitter. Just make sure you listen to your body and don't over-exercise, as this can actually weaken your immune system.<br/><br/></p>

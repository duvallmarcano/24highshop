<h2>WHAT ARE FEMINIZED WEED SEEDS:</h2>
<p>Feminized cannabis seeds are designed to produce only female plants. Normally, a weed seed can grow into a male or female plant; the entire sex determination process has to do with the X and Y chromosomes. “Feminization” is a process of conditioning female plants to obtain male pollen necessary for seed production. A plant with two X chromosomes will develop into a female plant 99.99% of the time (<span style="text-decoration: underline;"><a href="../../../../../seedshop/121-feminized-seeds.html"><span style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">Feminized cannabis seed</span></a></span>), while regular cannabis seeds with an X and Y chromosome sometimes tend to produce more male than female plants. <br/><br/>At Royal Queen Seeds, they use methods that have been used in horticulture for years to ensure that 99.99% of their Feminized seeds will produce only female plants.</p>
<p><span style="font-weight: bold;">The feminized</span> <strong><strong>weed seeds give you 99.99% certainty that your seed will flower into a female cannabis plant. The female cannabis plant is the plant on which buds (Cannabis) grow and which can be used for smoking, eating or making oil.<br/><br/></strong></strong></p>

<h2>WHAT ARE AUTOFLOWER WEED SEEDS:</h2>
<p>Autoflower strains are Indica or Sativa plants that are crossed with the Ruderalis strain. This cannabis strain is found in areas where the summer is very short. The ruderalis will flower automatically (autoflower) within a very short period of time. (Normally within 2 to 3 weeks). The ruderalis weed plant is mainly found in Northern Europe, Russia and neighbouring countries such as China and Mongolia. This plant naturally contains much less <span style="color: #1806ff;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><a href="../../../../143/what-are-the-substances-delta-8-thc-and-delta-9-thc.html" style="color: #1806ff; text-decoration: underline;">THC</a></span> </span>than the Indica and Sativa strains. By crossing this Ruderalis with an Indica or Sativa, you get a fast flowering variety with a high THC production and good bud formation.</p>

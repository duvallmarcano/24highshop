<h2>What are hallucinogens?</h2>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #2c06f9;"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallucinogen" style="color: #2c06f9; text-decoration: underline;">Hallucinogens</a></span> are substances in a particular substance that cause hallucinations. The word is derived from the Latin word hallucinere, meaning <em>"wandering in the mind"</em>. Drugs with hallucinogenic effects are also called psychedelic or mind-altering drugs. These include drugs such as LSD, LSA, mescaline, psilocybin, Dimethyltryptamine, ketamine and ibogaine. But there are also several herbs that have hallucinogenic effects.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>What are psychedelic herbs?</h2>
<p><span style="color: #2c06f9;"><a href="../../../../../smartshop/29-hallucinating-herbs.html" style="color: #2c06f9; text-decoration: underline;">Psychedelic herbs</a></span> are herbs that make you trip, basically nothing but hallucinate. The herbs put you in a higher state of consciousness and make you experience the world differently from what you are used to. You see colours more clearly, objects distort or move, and you go on a kind of spiritual journey. This can lead to new discoveries about yourself and your needs.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Effects of psychedelic herbs</h2>
<p>Exactly how you experience the journey depends on which kind you buy. In fact, there are a lot of different psychedelic herbs, with different possible effects. Some have mild effects - they mainly have a calming effect and cause mild euphoria. But there are also herbs that can give you the trip of a lifetime. So ask yourself in advance what you want to use hallucinogens for and find a suitable strain to go with it.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>How to use hallucinogenic herbs?</h2>
<p>Nowadays, hallucinogenic herbs are very popular. Not to use during a festival or a fun night out, but rather in a quiet environment to gain personal insights. Insights that can ensure you get more out of life. A fresh start in a new world, where you know exactly what you want and how to achieve it. You can use the herbs in different ways:</p>
<ul>
<li>You can make tea from them</li>
<li>You can eat the seeds by simply chewing them</li>
</ul>
<p>Always be well-prepared before using a trip drug. Make sure your surroundings are tidy and familiar. Arrange a trip sitter to keep an eye on you and have a <span style="color: #2c06f9;"><a href="../../../../../smartshop/40-top-10-smartshop.html" style="color: #2c06f9; text-decoration: underline;">trip stopper</a></span> ready.<br/><br/></p>

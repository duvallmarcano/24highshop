<h2 id="why-difficult">Why is quitting smoking so difficult?</h2>
<p>Smoking tobacco is a common habit, but it is also one of the most difficult addictions to overcome. Smoking<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><a href="https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/tobacco" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">kills</a></span><span> </span>8 million people every year, including 1.3 million non-smokers who die from secondhand smoke. Ultimately, half of all smokers die as a result of smoking. Yet a large proportion of the world's population still smokes.</p>
<p>Nicotine activates the brain's reward system and temporarily releases dopamine. This creates a feeling of relaxation and reward while also causing strong dependence. As a result, many people are unable to quit permanently without support.</p>
<h2 id="tools">Which tools increase the chances of quitting successfully?</h2>
<p>Research on smoking cessation clearly shows that the chances of success increase when support tools are used. These range from medication and therapy to nicotine replacement products such as patches, chewing gum, lozenges, and sprays.</p>
<p>Nicotine replacement products are known to reduce cigarette cravings. Even more effective appears to be the<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><a href="https://evidence.nihr.ac.uk/alert/using-both-nicotine-patches-and-gum-together-improves-the-chances-of-quitting-smoking/" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">combination</a></span><span> </span>of a nicotine patch together with a faster-acting nicotine product, such as nicotine gum. A study involving more than 40,000 smokers showed that this combination can significantly increase the likelihood of successfully quitting smoking.</p>
<p><img alt="quitting smoking and overcoming nicotine addiction" src="../../files/index.html@id=1912.jpg&amp;file=stopsmoking.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h2 id="ketamine">Can ketamine help with quitting smoking?</h2>
<p>Researchers are currently investigating the effectiveness of ketamine as an aid for smoking cessation. Previous studies have already shown that ketamine may have therapeutic applications for depression and anxiety disorders. This has also sparked interest in its potential role in addiction treatment.</p>
<p>How does it work? It appears to be related to the brain's reward system. Smoking triggers the release of dopamine, making nicotine use feel rewarding. Ketamine may influence the<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><a href="https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2018.00277/full" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">receptors</a></span><span> </span>involved in this process, which may help reduce cravings.</p>
<p>Researchers are also studying similar effects in other addictions, including alcohol, cocaine, cannabis, and opioid addiction.</p>
<p>When psychedelic experiences are mentioned as a potential aid, many people wonder what such an inner experience can actually bring about. If you would first like to understand this better, read<span> </span><span style="color: #1649f5;"><a href="../../guide/consciousness-and-experience/personal-experience-and-context.html" style="color: #1649f5;"><u>personal experience and context</u></a></span>.</p>
<h2 id="psilocybin">Can psilocybin help with quitting smoking?</h2>
<p>Like ketamine, psilocybin is also known for its potential therapeutic applications. Psilocybin is the active compound found in<span> </span><span style="color: #1649f5;"><a href="../../mushrooms.html" style="color: #1649f5; text-decoration: underline;">magic mushrooms</a></span><span> </span>and magic truffles. At higher doses it produces psychedelic effects, while some people choose to microdose with much smaller amounts.</p>
<p>Research suggests that psilocybin may contribute to behavioural change and help break deeply ingrained patterns.<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27441452/" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">Studies</a></span><span> </span>have shown promising results in programmes where psilocybin is combined with professional guidance for people who want to quit smoking.</p>
<p>Although further research is still needed, these findings are among the most interesting developments in addiction treatment in recent years.</p>
<h2 id="natural-tools">Which natural remedies may provide support?</h2>
<h3>Yoga and meditation</h3>
<p>Did you know that yoga, meditation, and relaxation exercises may help reduce nicotine cravings? For many people, stress is a major trigger for smoking. By practising meditation or yoga regularly, some people learn to cope better with stress and withdrawal symptoms.</p>
<p>Many people find that changing habits only becomes possible once both body and mind regain balance. If you would like to understand how natural support may contribute to this process, read<span> </span><span style="color: #1649f5;"><a href="../../guide/natural-support-and-balance/energy-and-relaxation.html" style="color: #1649f5;"><u>energy and relaxation</u></a></span>.</p>
<p><img alt="yoga and meditation as support for quitting smoking" src="../../files/index.html@id=1913.jpg&amp;file=yoga-meditate.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h3>Tobacco substitutes such as damiana</h3>
<p>Not every tobacco smoker is heavily addicted to nicotine. Some people mainly use tobacco while smoking a joint or in social situations. In such cases, a natural tobacco substitute such as<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><a href="../../article/8101867-damiana-indian-elements.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">damiana</a></span><span> </span>may be an alternative.</p>
<p>Damiana contains no nicotine and is not physically addictive like tobacco. However, it is still wise to be moderate with anything that is smoked, as smoke can place strain on the lungs.</p>
<p>Would you like to use cannabis without smoking it? Then edibles may be an alternative. <p>Some users also choose<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><a href="../../smartshop/222-rape.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">rapé</a></span>, a traditional herbal blend that has been used for centuries by indigenous tribes in South America.</p>
<h3>Passionflower</h3>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30707857/" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">Several</a></span><span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22873194/" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">studies</a></span><span> </span>on passionflower, also known as<span> </span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><a href="../../article/8101630-passion-flower-indian-elements.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">Passiflora incarnata</a></span>, suggest that this plant may help support people dealing with stress, anxiety, and sleep problems.</p>
<p>Because many people experience restlessness and sleeping difficulties while quitting smoking, passionflower is sometimes used as part of a broader smoking cessation strategy. In addition, the plant is well known for its relaxing properties.</p>

<p>In this article, we explain what can go wrong, why these problems occur and how you can solve or prevent them.</p>
<h2>What are the most common reasons a mushroom grow kit does not grow?</h2>
<p>If a <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1a20e8;"><a href="../../mushrooms/24-growkits-mushroom.html" style="color: #1a20e8; text-decoration: underline;">mushroom grow kit</a></span> does not grow, you will usually notice one of the following problems:</p>
<ol>
<li>No pin formation: small buds (pins) do not appear on the surface of the mycelium.</li>
<li>Pins stop growing: pins appear but stop developing and dry out.</li>
<li>Discoloration or mould: green, black or slimy spots can indicate contamination.</li>
<li>Dried substrate: the block becomes hard on top or starts to shrink.</li>
<li>Too much moisture: the surface becomes wet or soggy and may smell musty.</li>
</ol>
<p>These are symptoms, not causes. The underlying causes are usually related to climate conditions, moisture balance, hygiene or timing.</p>
<p><img alt="Infographic explaining problems with a mushroom grow kit, including lack of pin formation, drying pins, mould, discoloration, dehydration and excess moisture. The main causes are temperature, humidity, hygiene and timing." src="../../files/index.html@id=2917.jpg&amp;file=problems+growkit.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h2>Why can a mushroom grow kit fail to grow?</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Temperature problems</strong><br/>Most grow kits perform best between 20 and 25°C<span style="color: #1a20e8;"><sup><a href="why-your-magic-mushroom-grow-kit-is-not-growing-causes-solutions.html#bron1" style="color: #1a20e8; text-decoration: underline;">.1</a></sup></span>. Below 18°C, growth slows down significantly. Above 27°C, the mycelium becomes stressed and the risk of contamination increases.</li>
<li><strong>Incorrect humidity</strong><br/>Mycelium needs high humidity to grow properly. Air that is too dry can cause pins to dry out before they develop. Too much moisture can lead to bacteria or mould.</li>
<li><strong>Lack of fresh air</strong><br/>Keep the bag tightly closed until you see the first pins, then start airing your kit daily. Always read the manual carefully before you start growing. Some grow kits do not need to be aired.</li>
<li><strong>Insufficient light</strong><br/>Grow kits do not need strong light, but indirect daylight helps stimulate growth. Complete darkness can slow down pin formation.</li>
<li><strong>Incorrect soaking</strong><br/>Many grow kits need soaking before the first flush. Too little soaking can leave the substrate too dry, while soaking for too long can damage or suffocate it.</li>
<li><strong>Contamination</strong><br/>Bacteria or mould compete with the mycelium. Even a small infection can stop growth completely.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How to solve and prevent these problems</h2>
<h3>1. Check the temperature</h3>
<p>Measure the room temperature with a thermometer. If it is below 20°C, move the grow kit to a warmer place. If the temperature is too high, place it in a cooler room away from direct sunlight.</p>
<h3>2. Adjust humidity levels</h3>
<p>If the substrate looks dry, keep the kit away from heaters and dry airflow. Lightly mist the inside of the grow bag and make sure the bag is closed properly. If the surface looks very wet, briefly ventilate the bag. Use less water and carefully remove excess moisture if needed.</p>
<h3>3. Improve fresh air exchange</h3>
<p>Open the grow bag briefly once or twice a day to refresh the air. This can help stimulate mushroom growth. Always check the instructions to confirm what is recommended for your specific grow kit.</p>
<h3>4. Adjust lighting conditions</h3>
<p>Place the grow kit in a spot with indirect daylight. Avoid both direct sunlight and complete darkness. Normal daylight in a room is usually ideal.</p>
<h3>5. Check for contamination</h3>
<p>If you see green powder, black spots or smell a sour odour, the grow kit may be contaminated<span style="color: #1a20e8;"><sup><a href="why-your-magic-mushroom-grow-kit-is-not-growing-causes-solutions.html#bron2" style="color: #1a20e8; text-decoration: underline;">.2</a></sup></span>. In many cases, contaminated kits cannot recover. Remove them promptly to prevent contamination from spreading.</p>

<h2>What is a mantra?</h2>
<p>The word mantra comes from Sanskrit. Here, man stands for spirit, while 'tra' means an instrument or vehicle. So literally, mantra means an instrument for the mind. Mantra is usually a word or sound repeatedly<span> meditated </span>upon. Most people do this out loud, gently or just silently in the mind. In this way, they want to reduce distractions or increase their concentration. According to experts,<span> these </span><span> meditative </span>sayings<span> are </span>over 3,000 years old and were originally created by the Vedas from the Indian tradition.</p>

<h2>Does a mantra work?</h2>
<p>You would think that mantras are barely researched by scientists, but nothing could be further from the truth. In fact, this topic is extremely researched and, as a result, we can say a thing or two about how functional the best mantras are. According to this <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35329068/" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">2022 study by Alvarez-Perez et al,</a></span> meditating using mantras<span> can </span>help reduce anxiety, depression and post-traumatic stress. Accordingly, the study saw a small to moderate improvement in participants' mental health. The same result came from this <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1876382018304591" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">2018 scientific study by Julie Lynch et al.</a></span><br/><br/>This <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29650130/" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">2017 study by Fatemeh Torkamani et al</a></span> also<span> suggests </span>that mantra meditation in group sessions may have possible effects on salivary immunoglobulin A and mood in general. Moreover, mantras could help reduce knee pain and dysfunction in people with osteoarthritis, according to the study by <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6136530/" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">Kim E. Innes et al</a></span>. So all in all, the best mantras can definitely work, especially if you use them for stress reduction. Just know that the positive effects are modest though, keep this in mind. So don't expect a mantra to be a panacea.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>The best mantras</h2>
<p>Most<span> meditative </span>sayings are associated with Hindu or Buddhist traditions. Below are some good examples of these, including their explanations and potential benefits.</p>
<p></p>
<h3>1. To</h3>
<p>It is perhaps the best-known mantra of all. Users think of it as the universal sound, focusing on their <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0907f5;"><a href="../387/breathwork-breathing-exercises-as-a-drug.html" style="color: #0907f5; text-decoration: underline;">breathing</a></span><span> to </span>calm their nervous system. Do this by repeating Om during your inhale and exhale. Optionally, you can also resonate it softly in the throat.</p>
<p></p>
<h3>2. Om Mani Padme Hum</h3>
<p>Om Mani Padme Hum stands for O jewel in the lotus and is a Buddhist mantra. All six syllables are said to correspond to qualities such as wisdom and compassion. Om Mani Padme Hum, according to those who believe in mantra, is said to promote compassion while cleansing you of negativity. You can recite this mantra both silently and out loud. Most people do this in combination with mala beads.</p>
<p></p>
<h3>3. Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya</h3>
<p>This mantra is used to worship Vishnu and/or Krishna. Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya is therefore a mantra of devotion and surrender, it is said to promote devotion and peace. The Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya mantra consists of 12-syllables, which you repeat during Japa meditation or in satsang.Although these mantras were originally the most popular, there are now also modern variants that are widely used. I am enough is a good example, as is I am calm and in control. Experts recommend using several mantras and you will eventually find out which mantra is best for you.</p>
<p><img alt="Om Mani Padme Hum" src="../../files/index.html@id=2643.png&amp;file=mantra.png" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<h3>4. Mahamrityunjaya</h3>
<p>Mahamrityunjaya represents the great liberator of death and is user-focused on health, protection and life dynamics. People believe this mantra strengthens physical and emotional resilience. Mahamrityunjaya is usually repeated in times of illness, stress or high emotional needs.</p>
<p></p>
<h3>5. Gayatri 5.</h3>
<p>The Gayatri mantra is a call to divine solar energy and is said to bring enlightenment and understanding. It is traditionally used in morning prayers and is said to be spiritually powerful. Most people repeat this mantra silently in the morning. Gayatri can also be used during study sessions.</p>

This is not only because of the influence they can have on you, but also because of their growth habit. Especially if you want to grow <a href="../../mushrooms.html"><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;">magic mushrooms</span></a>, it is useful to know a little more about it. In mushroom cultivation, you often come across the word mycelium. That is the network of hyphae from which the magic mushrooms originate. You can read more about that in this blog. <br/><br/></p>
<h2>What is a mushroom mycelium network, and how does it grow?</h2>
<p>Anyone who wants to <a href="../../mushrooms/24-growkits-mushroom.html"><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;">grow mushrooms</span></a> themselves will also have to deal with the mycelium. In the plastic container you will find a breeding ground and that contains the mycelium. This is the network of fungal threads (also called hyphae) of the conscious mushroom you want to grow. This can arise from a single spore. In the wild, mycelia can form underground or in a nutrient medium such as a rotten tree trunk. The mycelium will normally continue to spread through long threads. The fruiting bodies grow above the ground from the mycelium. These are the mushrooms (or in your grow kit the magic mushroom, such as <a href="https:/en/article/0208819-100-mycelium-magic-mushroom-growkit-golden-teacher"> <span style="color: #1806ff;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Golden Teacher</span></span></a> or <span style="color: #000000;">Mexican </span>mushroom). For easy visualization, you can think of the mycelium as the roots of a plant and the mushroom as the fruit. <br/><br/><img align="middle" alt="mycelium" src="../../files/index.html@id=334.png&amp;%3Bfile=MyceliumPsilocybe.png" style="width: 1000px; max-width: 100%;"><br/><br/></img></p>
<h2>The task of a mushroom mycelium</h2>
<p>The fungus reproduces in an asexual way through the mycelium. It also ensures the absorption of nutrients, so that the fungus can grow further. Fungi break down organic material, after which these small particles return to the soil. The mycelium also works together with all kinds of plants and trees, in a symbiosis. It filters the soil, so fungi help keep it healthy. Fungi themselves are also a source of food for insects and invertebrates. Fungi therefore have a very important function in nature.<br/><br/>Also in your grow kit at home, the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mycelium"><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;">mycelium</span></a> will absorb nutrients from the nutrient medium (the substrate). This allows your mushrooms to grow. Did you know that fungi can grow enormously? There is a very large fungus in Oregon in the United States. This grows under the Blue Mountains. Its mycelium occupies an area of ​​10 square kilometres. This makes this the largest organism on earth! <br/><br/></p>
<h2>Growth of the mycelium</h2>
<p>Only one <a href="../131/how-do-you-make-a-magic-mushroom-spore-syringe.html"><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;">spore</span></a> has to fall into the soil and from there the mycelium can grow. It forms threads that branch out more and more. It is striking that it grows from a center. When the food in the center is gone, the mycelium there will disappear, causing the mushrooms to form a circle. That is the well-known witch circle.<br/><br/><img alt="contamination growkit" src="../../files/index.html@id=1033.png&amp;file=neurospora,aspergillus,trichoderma+(1).png" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"><br/><br/></img></p>
<h2>How do you recognize a contamination with fungi or bacteria on your mushroom grow kit?</h2>
<p>You may be wondering what the difference is between the mycelium and the fungus. What you don't want is mold that can <a href="../109/these-fungi-cause-fungal-infection-on-your-mushrooms-growing-kit.html"><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1806ff;">contaminate</span></a> your grow kit. You can recognize an infection with fungus (or bacteria) by:</p>
<ul>
<li>Discoloration: Gray, black, white or green spots are bad news. Blue spots can have several causes.</li>
<li>Strange smells</li>
<li>The mycelium becomes smooth and spongy</li>
</ul>
<p>If you suffer from an infection in the mycelium, it can really ruin your mushroom culture. That is why it is also important to prevent this by working hygienically. As you could read earlier, a fungus can form from a single spore. Therefore, try to sterilize the environment and your tools well before you start working with your <a href="../76/what-types-of-substrate-are-there-for-growing-magic-mushrooms.html"> <span style="color: #1806ff;"><span style="text-decoration: underline;">growkit</span></span></a>. Also make sure you are clean yourself by taking a shower. Wear <span style="color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../mushrooms/77-grow-supplies.html" style="color: #1806ff;"> <span style="text-decoration: underline;">gloves</span></a></span>, clean clothes and you could even wear a <span style="color: #1806ff;"><a href="../../article/9107668-disposable-face-mask.html" style="color: #1806ff;"> <span style="text-decoration: underline;">mouth mask</span></a></span>.</p></div>

<h2>What is the difference between magic mushrooms and truffles?</h2>
<p>Although magic mushrooms (psilocybin mushrooms) and truffles (sclerotia) are often mentioned in the same breath, they are technically different parts of the same fungus. Mushrooms are the above-ground fruiting bodies of the fungus, while truffles grow underground as thickened, tuberous storage structures. The active ingredient is the same in both: psilocybin. As a result, they have similar mind-altering effects, such as intense visual experiences, changes in perception and deep introspection. However, there are slight differences in strength and intensity per strain. Truffles are generally slightly milder than magic mushrooms, which makes them popular with beginners. An important difference is also in the legislation: in the Netherlands, magic mushrooms (when dried or processed) are banned, while fresh truffles can be sold legally.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>The appeal of growing your own mushrooms compared to ready-made truffles</h2>
<p>If you are curious about <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #490bfa;"><a href="../../../../../mushrooms/16-magic-truffles.html" rel="noopener" style="color: #490bfa; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">truffles</a></span>, you can find ready-made kits of different strains in a webshop. These can be used immediately. So why go for a mushroom grow kit? You have to grow these carefully first, which takes a lot of time and attention. Of course, you may not feel like doing that at all. But if you keep reading, we'll explain why you might go for a grow kit anyway. Because these bring several advantages.</p>
<h3>Lower costs</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p>If you regularly use magic mushrooms or truffles, you will find that this is quite an expensive hobby. A grow kit does cost something when you buy it, but remember: from a single grow kit you can harvest several times and one flush already produces a good yield. That's why a grow kit can last longer and is a cost-effective option in the long run. Keep in mind that a growing process is not always successful, and external factors such as temperature and hygiene affect the yield.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3>You are in control</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p>A big advantage of using a grow kit compared to ready-made truffles is that you are in control of the growing process. You choose the strain and determine the growing conditions. That means you can be sure they are of good quality. With ready-made truffles, you don't know exactly how they were produced. But this does not mean that home-grown mushrooms are always better. There is the risk of contamination (e.g. by fungi or bacteria). This risk exists especially if you don't work hygienically.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3>Enjoy the magic of growing magic mushrooms</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Mushrooms are of course magical in themselves. But if you also grow them yourself, you are much more involved in the creation of these magical creations of nature. It starts from preparing the growing kit, spreading the mycelium to watching the fruiting bodies appear: growing mushrooms is a beautiful and moving process. It certainly adds something to the whole experience, which you would miss if you use ready-made products.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Possibly this also contributes to the opinion of many enthusiastic mushroom growers, that home-grown mushrooms actually produce more intense trips than ready-made ones, because you have developed a deeper connection with what you have grown. Of course, there is no scientific evidence for this.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3>Completely legal</h3>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Selling dried mushrooms is not legal in our country. However, growing your own mushrooms is (and fresh truffles are also legal). So you can grow your own psychedelic garden safely and without worrying about legality. Want to know more about the legality of magic mushrooms and truffles in the Netherlands? The central government provides a clear overview of the legislation.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>If you don't live in the Netherlands, make sure you are well-informed about the local laws and regulations in your country before buying a mushroom growing kit.<br/><br/><img alt="GROW YOUR OWN MUSHROOMS OR TRUFFLES" src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=2526.jpg&amp;file=truffels.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"><br/><br/></img></p>
<h2>Starting to grow mushrooms with a grow kit</h2>
<p>So if you want to get started with a <span style="color: #490bfa;"><a href="../../../../../mushrooms/24-growkits-mushroom.html" rel="noopener" style="color: #490bfa; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">mushroom grow kit</a></span>, getting started is easy and requires no special knowledge. Take a look around in our mushroom shop. Here you will find grow kits from different brands, but the principle is the same in all of them. The kit consists of a substrate colonised by the mycelium of a Psilocybe mushroom. Getting started is easy, just follow the instructions and the magic can begin.</p>
<p>First, of course, you choose the appropriate grow kit. There are several strains available, each with its own effects. Read carefully in the description what the effects are and especially how strong the mushroom is. If you're a beginner, choose a mushroom with mild effects. Once you have received the grow kit, make sure your surroundings are clean: Mushrooms are susceptible to contamination. Work hygienically and wash your hands before touching the grow kit. Find the right place to put the grow kit, so the mushrooms can grow properly. Pay special attention to heat, cold and direct sunlight. On <span style="color: #490bfa;"><a href="https://www.erowid.org/plants/mushrooms/mushrooms_mmgg.shtml" rel="noopener" style="color: #490bfa; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">Erowid.org</a></span> you can find a handy overview of the growing process and what to look out for. The next step? Be patient. Mushrooms need time to grow. Follow the fascinating growing process and it won't be long before it's time to harvest. They are ready when the membrane under the cap just begins to loosen.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>The safety of magic mushrooms and truffles</h2>
<p>Mushrooms are mushrooms that contain a mind-altering substance (psilocybin). Always proceed with caution when using psychedelics. Stick to the recommended amount stated on the package. Although this is a natural substance, safe and responsible use is still important. Mushrooms can cause severe effects such as altered perception, euphoria and anxiety. According to <span style="color: #490bfa;"><a href="https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/308850" rel="noopener" style="color: #490bfa; text-decoration: underline;" target="_blank">Medical News Today</a></span>, psychedelics can lead to negative experiences such as paranoia and anxiety. People with mental disorders are advised against using psychedelics. In addition, always make sure you are in a safe environment and ask a friend to be a trip sitter:</p>

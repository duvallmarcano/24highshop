<h2>What are medicinal mushrooms?</h2>
<p>Mushrooms have been around for a long time on this earth and their medicinal use goes back about 5000 to 7000 years, as far as we know. <a href="../../../../../mushrooms/213-medicinal-mushrooms.html"><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0000ff;">Medicinal mushrooms</span></a> are full of nutrients and have therefore been picked and eaten a lot from time immemorial. Medicinal mushrooms have a very positive effect on your immune system, are cell-renewing and can be used in chronic conditions. Think of autoimmune diseases, cancer, skin disorders or allergies.</p>
<h3>Scientific research</h3>
<p>The medicinal effect of mushrooms has been used on earth for quite some time. But real scientific research into the medicinal effect has only been under discussion for 60 years. The research and studies mainly focus on the composition and properties of the mushrooms. For example, not only the fruiting body is looked at, but also the ‘roots’ of the mushrooms.</p>

<h2>How do medicinal mushrooms work?</h2>
<p>Medicinal mushrooms have a wide range of healthy nutrients. We used to administer these mushrooms according to the ‘hot water principle’, but now we have been able to use the most relevant parts for a good medicinal effect for some time now. For example, we now make <a href="../../../../../article/4209769-microdose-synergy-60-capsules.html"><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0000ff;">capsules</span></a>, tablets, powder or liquid forms from the relevant parts of the mushrooms. In the past, the extracts were pulled from the mushrooms with hot water and drunk.</p>
<p>You can take these capsules, tablets, powder or liquid forms of the medicinal mushrooms. The effects of this are different per mushroom, but in general it causes:</p>
<ul>
<li>The presence of essential sugars and polysaccharides.</li>
<li>High-quality and essential amino acids that our body does not make itself. We need a total of 22 amino acids, of which our body produces 13 itself and the remaining 9 do not.</li>
<li>Vitamins and minerals.</li>
<li>Large amounts of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antioxidant"><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0000ff;">antioxidants</span></a> to prevent oxidation.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Medicinal mushrooms vs. magic mushrooms</h2>
<p>There is always a lot of confusion around the well-known magic mushrooms and truffles, and the medicinal mushrooms. Let's clarify this once and for all: these two mushrooms are really different and certainly not the same. Mushrooms cause you to change your consciousness or hallucinate. Medicinal mushrooms have <strong>no</strong> psychoactive effect. It is a dietary supplement with a positive and balancing effect on your health.However, there is an exception, namely microdosing with psychedelic mushrooms or truffles.

<h2 id="what-is-chaga">What is the Chaga mushroom?</h2>
<p>The Chaga mushroom is officially known as <em>Inonotus obliquus</em> and mainly grows on birch trees in cold climates. It is also known as the birch conk and is often referred to as the <strong>Mushroom of Immortality</strong>. On the outside, Chaga looks like a dark, almost charred fungus, while the inside has a warm orange colour.</p>
<p>Chaga is one of the most popular <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1649f5;"><a href="../../../../../mushrooms/213-medicinal-mushrooms.html" style="color: #1649f5; text-decoration: underline;">medicinal mushrooms</a></span>, alongside Lion's Mane, Reishi, Cordyceps, Turkey Tail and Maitake.</p>
<p>The mushroom has been used for a long time in traditional practices and is now mainly sold as powder, extract or capsules.</p>
<p>Chaga is often described as a powerful medicinal mushroom, but many people wonder where these natural products actually fit within the broader world of natural substances. If you'd like to understand this better, read <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1649f5;"><a href="../../../../../guide/natural-psychedelics-experience-types/what-are-natural-psychedelics.html" style="color: #1649f5; text-decoration: underline;">what are natural psychedelics</a></span>.</p>

<p>Because Chaga has a very hard texture, the mushroom is usually processed into powder, tea, extract or capsules. This makes it much easier to incorporate into your daily routine.</p>
<h3>Popular ways to use Chaga</h3>

<li>Take Chaga capsules as a dietary supplement.</li>
<li>Mix Chaga powder into hot beverages.</li>

<h2 id="side-effects">Possible side effects and considerations</h2>
<p>Although Chaga is a natural product, this does not automatically mean it is suitable for everyone. Some people choose to be extra cautious when using supplements or extracts.</p>
<p>Many people automatically associate natural products with safety, while interactions with medication may require additional attention. If you would like to know when using supplements may or may not be advisable, read <span style="text-decoration: underline;"><span style="color: #1649f5; text-decoration: underline;"><a href="../../../../../guide/safe-use-knowledge-and-context/when-to-use-or-not.html" style="color: #1649f5; text-decoration: underline;">when to use them and when not to</a></span></span>.</p>
<h3>When should you be extra careful?</h3>
<ul>
<li>If you use blood thinners.</li>
<li>If you have an autoimmune disorder.</li>
<li>During pregnancy or while breastfeeding.</li>
<li>If you have existing kidney problems.</li>
</ul>
<p>Chaga contains relatively high levels of oxalates. For this reason, some people choose to limit their intake or consult a healthcare professional before use if they have existing medical conditions.</p>

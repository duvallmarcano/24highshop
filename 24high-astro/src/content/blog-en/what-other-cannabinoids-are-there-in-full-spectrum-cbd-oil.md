<h2>What is known about the entourage effect?</h2>
<p>Each cannabinoid in full spectrum CBD oil has its own specific characteristics. But if you take all these substances together, they influence each other's effect and properties. In this way, they can change or reinforce each other's effect. This is called the entourage effect. This does not only apply to cannabinoids, but also to the other substances in CBD products, such as terpenes and flavonoids.</p>
<p>However, it is not yet 100% proven that this effect exists and how it works. Not much large-scale research has been done yet. In 2018, the results of multiple <span style="color: #0000ff;"><a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6143706/" style="color: #0000ff; text-decoration-line: underline;">studies</a> </span>analyzed. It seemed to emerge from this that pure CBD extracts were not as effective as full spectrum products. Patients who suffered from epileptic seizures benefited more from full spectrum CBD oil. At 71%, the amount of seizures decreased. For the people who received pure CBD, this was only 46%.<br/><br/></p>

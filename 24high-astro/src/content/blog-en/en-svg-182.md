<h2>Your outdoor cannabis plant: from sowing to growth, flowering and harvest</h2>
<p><img src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=1709.jpg&amp;file=marijuana-plant-.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<p>An outdoor cannabis plant goes through several stages in its life: from seed to germinated seedling, growth, flowering and harvest time. First of all, when your outdoor cannabis will flower naturally depends on when you put it in the ground. So be sure to start at the right time! Read here when exactly that is: <span style="color: #2819df;"><a href="../../../../163/what-is-the-best-time-to-grow-weed-outdoors.html" style="color: #2819df; text-decoration: underline;">What is the best time to grow outdoor weed?</a></span></p>
<h3>Seed and germination</h3>
<p>Like any plant, the life of your outdoor cannabis grow starts with a <span style="color: #2819df;"><a href="../../../../../seedshop/125-top-10-outdoor-seeds.html" style="color: #2819df; text-decoration: underline;">seed</a></span>. This will germinate, a process that takes a few days. After <span style="color: #2819df;"><a href="../../../../7/how-to-germinate-your-cannabis-seeds.html" style="color: #2819df; text-decoration: underline;">germination</a></span>, you can set the seedlings aside to let them grow into independent plants.</p>
<h3>Growth phase</h3>
<p>The growth phase is an important stage for your outdoor plant. Whereas the seed will actually manage just fine without too much support, you'll want to help it a bit more as it grows. If you don't, you'll find that your outdoor cannabis plant won't do as well in the flowering and harvesting stages.</p>
<h3>Flowering phase</h3>
<p>The flowering stage of the outdoor cannabis plant is when flowers appear. This does not happen randomly, but at the point in the year when the plant catches less daylight. This is a fixed moment in the annual calendar, so very convenient to predict. After all, every year the days get shorter on a certain date. For the cannabis plant, the growth phase turns into the flowering phase when the number of hours of light reaches 12 hours a day. A week later, you can expect those flowers on the plant. White strands appear on the plant and multiply rapidly.</p>
<h3>Harvesting</h3>
<p>Even though the flowers appear quickly, the flowering of your outdoor cannabis plant will take around 2 to 3.5 months. How long your plant will flower depends on the seeds you bought. A rule of thumb is that indica weed flowers shortly, while sativa takes a long time. So harvesting is generally done in late summer and (late) autumn.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>So when will my outdoor cannabis plant flower?</h2>
<p><img src="../../../../../files/index.html@id=1710.jpg&amp;file=marijuana-buds.jpg" style="width: 1100px; max-width: 100%;"/></p>
<p>To answer the question of the article; when exactly does your outdoor cannabis plant flower? Cannabis plants in our country normally start flowering after summer has officially started: after 21 June.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Does an outdoor plant need nutrition in the flowering phase?</h2>
<p>Of course, you can let nature do its work and leave your cannabis plants to their own devices after sowing. But you'll understand that this will not lead to a perfect harvest. That is why we advise you to provide your cannabis plants with support while growing and blooming. As far as possible, do your best to give them a solid development with water, nutrition, light and the right temperature and humidity. Just as you would with your other outdoor plants.

<h2>What are Magic Truffles?</h2>
<p>Magic truffles, also known as "sclerotia", are the underground substructures of certain mushrooms, such as the Psilocybe cubensis, that contain psilocybin, a psychoactive compound similar to psilocin. These truffles grow underground and serve as energy reserves for the mushroom during less favourable conditions. Magic sclerotia offer an intriguing glimpse into the possibilities of the human mind and the potential of psychedelics for personal growth and spirit.</p>
<h3>The Mechanisms of Action of Truffles and Mushrooms</h3>
<p>The psychoactive effects of magic truffles are mainly caused by the presence of psilocybin, which is converted to psilocin in the body after consumption.

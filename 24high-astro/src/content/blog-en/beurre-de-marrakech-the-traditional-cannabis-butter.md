<h2>What is Beurre de Marrakech?</h2>
<p>Beurre de Marrakech is a form of cannabis butter popular in Moroccan cuisine. It combines the rich flavour of butter with the psychoactive effects of cannabis. This cannabis butter is often used as a base for various edibles, such as biscuits, brownies, <span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #1621d2;"><span><a href="../67/how-do-you-make-and-use-spacecake.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">space cake</a></span></span> and other pastries, but can also be used simply as a spread. Here are some key points about which parts of the cannabis plant are used:</p>
<h3>Used Parts of the Cannabis Plant:</h3>
<ol>
<li><strong>Cannabis flowers (Buds)</strong>: For the highest quality and strongest effects, the female cannabis flowers, also known as "buds", are usually used. These contain the most cannabinoids, such as THC and CBD.</li>
<li><strong>Small leaves (Sugar leaves)</strong>: The small leaves surrounding the flowers, which are often covered in trichomes, can also be used. These are often called "sugar leaves" because of their high concentration of resin.</li>
<li><strong>Waste material</strong>: Although less potent, larger leaves, male plants and stems can also be used. However, this will result in a less potent butter with a more bitter taste<br/><br/></li>
</ol>

<h2>How do you make Beurre de Marrakech?</h2>
<p>Making beurre de Marrakech is a simple process that requires a few basic steps. Here's an overview:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Gather ingredients</strong>: You will need butter, hash or chopped <span style="color: #1621d2;"><a href="../251/what-is-cannabis.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">cannabis</a></span> and water.</li>
<li><strong>Heat butter and water</strong>: Melt the butter in a pan together with water. The water helps regulate the temperature and prevents the butter from burning.</li>
<li><strong>Adding cannabis</strong>: Add the crumbled hashish or finely chopped cannabis to the melted butter. Stir well to mix everything well.</li>
<li><strong>Simmer</strong>: Let the mixture simmer on low heat for several hours (usually 2-3 hours). This process allows the THC and other cannabinoids to dissolve in the fats of the butter.</li>
<li><strong>Cool and strain</strong>: After simmering, let the mixture cool and strain it to remove the vegetable residues. What remains is a powerful, green beurre de Marrakech.</li>
</ol>
<h3>Using Beurre de Marrakech</h3>
<p>Beurre de Marrakech can be used in several ways:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pastries and edibles</strong>: Use the butter as a base for making biscuits, brownies and other pastries.</li>
<li><strong>Spread</strong>: Spread the beurre de Marrakech on bread, crackers or use it in culinary dishes to give an extra kick.</li>
<li><strong>Dosage</strong>: It is important to be careful when dosing beurre de Marrakech, especially if you are new to <span style="color: #1621d2;"><a href="../../headshop/113-cannabis-edibles.html" style="color: #1621d2; text-decoration: underline;">cannabis edibles</a></span>. Start with a small amount and wait at least an hour to feel the effects before consuming more.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Precautions</h3>
<p>When using beurre de Marrakech, it is essential to observe some precautions:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Storage</strong>: Store beurre de Marrakech in the fridge or freezer to preserve its potency and freshness.</li>
<li><strong>Legal Status</strong>: Check the laws regarding the use of cannabis in your region, as this varies by country and state.<br/><br/><br/></li>
</ul>

<h2>Where does Cordyceps come from?</h2>
<p>Cordyceps 'mushrooms' are a species of naturally occurring parasitic fungi that have been used in traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) for centuries. In fact, the entire family consists of hundreds of species. They grow mostly in tropical forests. Thus, it easily found its way to China and its rich history of medicinal traditions. Cordyceps is also called mushroom because of the shape it takes: namely, it resembles a small <span style="color: #0814e3;"><a href="../../../../../mushrooms.html" style="color: #0814e3; text-decoration: underline;">magic mushroom</a></span><span style="text-decoration: underline; color: #0814e3;">.</span> So the Cordyceps is a fungus, which nests in another organism, infecting its host This could be a larva, a caterpillar, a moth... Sounds creepy perhaps, but you don't have to worry about being taken over by this fungus. You are simply unfit for that.<br/><br/></p>

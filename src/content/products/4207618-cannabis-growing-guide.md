<p><strong>Cannabis Growing Guide</strong> by J.B. Haze is an essential guide for novice cannabis growers. Haze begins with an important explanation of genetics and its importance for both the practical grower and the hobbyist. He emphasises the joy of growing alongside technical knowledge. This passion continues in sections on the ideal growing environment and the history of crossbreeding cannabis strains, with Haze discussing the different traits growers may want to develop in their own varieties.</p>
<p>Next, Haze guides the reader through the process of selecting seeds and explains the differences between Haze and Kush plants, as well as what makes a "good seed" as a starting point for cultivation. He acknowledges that not everyone lives in an ideal environment for growing cannabis and offers tips for selecting seeds that will give the best results depending on the environment. He also discusses the types of seeds available, with their advantages and disadvantages, and gives recommendations for seed banks.</p>
<p>The focus then shifts to soil, which Haze says is crucial for optimal yields. He describes how to prepare pots and soil, address the dangers of chemicals in water and balance soil pH. These basics make the book ideal for beginners. Haze elaborates on the germination process, temperature and humidity control, and introduces an alternative method for watering the seeds, which has a high success rate and is easy for beginners.</p>
<p>As the plants grow, Haze discusses proper potting methods and the best lighting. He also explains how to protect plants from pests and what to do if they do appear. Moreover, he warns about the risks of sharing your growing location.</p>
<p>An important part of the book is the explanation of plant cloning, which allows growers to preserve their ideal plant characteristics. Haze also explains the difference between a bud and a flower, how to match the medicinal effects of a bud and the best time to harvest.</p>
<p>In the chapter on regeneration, Haze explains how this method works to increase yields and offers a specific timeline for results. He shares his experience with auto-drying harvests and gives tips on how to handle plants minimally to avoid damage.</p>
<p>With more than 150 impressive photos by the author and clear instructions, "Cannabis Growing Guide" is the perfect book for any beginner who wants to learn how to grow the best cannabis outdoors. The book is full of expert advice and the author's charming personality, making it the ultimate guide for beginners.</p>

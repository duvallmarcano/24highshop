<p><strong>*The Psilocybin Handbook for Women*</strong> is an informative guide for anyone who wants to learn more about psilocybin, microdosing and the history of psychedelics. Although the book is suitable for a wide audience, the focus is on topics that may be relevant to people assigned female at birth.</p>
<p>Author <strong>Jennifer Chesak</strong> discusses the background to psilocybin, its cultural history, contemporary scientific research and practical concepts such as microdosing, set &amp; setting and trip sitting. The information is explained in an accessible way, with a focus on questions specifically relating to hormones, the menstrual cycle and other women-centred topics.</p>

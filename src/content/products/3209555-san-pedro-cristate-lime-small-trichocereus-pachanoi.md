<h3>Cultivation and growth information San Pedro Cristata</h3>
<p>The San Pedro Cristata Lime (Echinopsis pachanoi cristata) is a special variety of the well-known San Pedro cactus. Watering this plant correctly is crucial for healthy growth and to prevent root rot. Here are some guidelines:</p>
<h3><strong>1. Frequency of watering</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Spring &amp; Summer:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Water about <strong>once every 2-3 weeks</strong>, depending on temperature and humidity.</li>
<li>Let the soil <strong>dry out</strong> completely between waterings.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Autumn &amp; Winter:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Reduce watering drastically, about <strong>once a month or less</strong>.</li>
<li>In the colder months, the cactus has a dormant period, during which it needs almost no water.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>2. How to water?</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Use <strong>rainwater or filtered water</strong> (tap water sometimes contains too much lime and minerals).</li>
<li>Pour the water <strong>directly onto the soil</strong>, avoiding contact with the trunk and the growth chamber (cristate form), to prevent rot.</li>
<li>Make sure the water <strong>can drain</strong> well from the pot (no stagnant water in the saucer).</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>3. Best method</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>"Soak &amp; Dry" method:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Water until it runs out of the drainage holes.</li>
<li>Then let the soil <strong>dry completely</strong> before watering again.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>4. Additional tips</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Use a well-drained <strong>cactus soil</strong> (mixture of sand, perlite and potting soil).</li>
<li>When in doubt, <strong>water too little rather than too much</strong> - over-watering is cacti's worst enemy.</li>
<li>In extremely hot summers, a light spray in the early morning can help prevent dehydration.</li>
</ul>

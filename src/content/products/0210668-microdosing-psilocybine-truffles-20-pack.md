<h2>How does microdosing with psilocybin work?</h2>
<p>Psilocybin is converted in the body into psilocin. This substance influences serotonin receptors, which are involved in mood, perception, and thought processes. Because microdosing involves a very low amount, a classic psychedelic experience usually does not occur.</p>
<p>Many users describe the effects as subtle. Think of a slight change in focus, creativity, or mental clarity. It is important to know that experiences may differ per person and depend on factors such as mindset, environment, and personal sensitivity.</p>

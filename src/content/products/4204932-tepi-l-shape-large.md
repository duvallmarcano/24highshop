<p>A<strong>"Tepi large L</strong>" is a blowgun traditionally used by indigenous tribes in South America, mainly in the Amazon. It is used to blow herbal preparations, including shamanic snuff such as rapé, directly into a participant's nostrils. The aim is to provide a quick and intense experience of the snuff. Rapé is a sacred shamanic snuff powder often made from various medicinal plants and tobacco.</p>
<h3>Use</h3>
<p>Take a small amount of rapé and place it at the end of the tepi. The receiver covers one nostril and the dispenser uses the tepi to blow the rapé into the open nostril.</p>

<h3>Caution!</h3>
<p>Rapé can be dangerous to health when combined with certain medicines, alcohol or with MAO inhibitors. Are you on medication? Then always consult your doctor first and read the leaflet of your medication to avoid health risks. Also, do not use Rapé if you are pregnant or breastfeeding. Do not participate in traffic when using it. So do not drive a car or drive other vehicles.</p>

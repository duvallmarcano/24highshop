<p><strong>Green Sensation</strong> is a multi-purpose, powerful fertilizer that can be used with all growing styles and is specifically designed not only to maximize the quality of the harvest, but also to promote overall plant health. This product is unique for its four-in-one action, supporting each key stage of plant growth and development. The four main functions of Green Sensation are explained in detail below:</p>
<ol>
<li>
<p><strong>Flowering:</strong> It promotes a more vigorous and productive flowering phase, resulting in a richer flowering process.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Soil:</strong> It improves the quality and health of the soil or substrate used. Healthy soil is essential for strong root development and overall plant vitality.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Yield:</strong> By improving flowering and soil health, Green Sensation increases plant yield.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Flavour:</strong> It also has a positive impact on the flavour of the finished products.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3>USAGE AND DOSAGE</h3>
<p>Shake well before use. Add 1 ml of Green Sensation per 1 litre of water (1:1000). Then add base nutrient until the desired EC value is reached. Use this nutrient solution with every watering in the last four to six weeks of the flowering phase.</p>

<h2>What are Mexicana truffles?</h2>
<p>Mexicana truffles are <em>psilocybe sclerotia</em>, also known as magic truffles. This underground structure develops from the mycelium of certain <em>Psilocybe</em> species. They naturally contain psilocybin, a substance that is converted in the body into psilocin and may influence perception, mood, and thoughts.</p>
<p>The Mexicana truffle is known as one of the most accessible varieties. Because of this, they are often chosen by people who are curious about psychedelic truffles and want to explore a first experience.</p>
<h2>What can you expect from Mexicana?</h2>
<p>The effects of magic truffles differ from person to person and are influenced by factors such as dosage, mood, and environment (set &amp; setting). Many users describe the following with Mexicana:</p>
<ul>
<li>Light visual patterns and more intense colors</li>
<li>A relaxed and positive atmosphere</li>
<li>Greater openness in thoughts and conversations</li>
<li>A subtle introspective experience</li>
</ul>
<p>Due to its mild character, this truffle is often chosen by beginners or by experienced users who are looking for a lighter psychedelic experience.</p>
<h2>How do you use Mexicana truffles?</h2>
<p>Truffles can be eaten raw or prepared as tea. When eating them raw, it is important to chew them well so the active compounds are absorbed more effectively.</p>
<ul>
<li>A package usually contains <strong>15 grams</strong></li>
<li>Preferably use on an empty stomach</li>
<li>Chew the truffles well before swallowing</li>
<li>Ensure a calm and familiar environment</li>
<li>Do not combine with alcohol or other substances</li>
</ul>
<p>You can also make truffle tea by cutting the truffles into small pieces and letting them steep in warm water (not boiling) for about 15 to 20 minutes.</p>
<h2>What is a dose?</h2>
<p>The right dosage varies per person. Many users consider <strong>10 to 15 grams</strong> a full experience. Beginners often choose a lower starting dose first to discover how their body reacts.</p>

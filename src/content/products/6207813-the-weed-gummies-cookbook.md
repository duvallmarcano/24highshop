<p><strong>The Weed Gummies Cookbook</strong> is a practical and colourfully designed cookbook for anyone who wants to make their own gummies, sweets and other sweet edibles. The book contains clear step-by-step instructions and shows you how to make various types of gummies, wine gums, soft toffees, hard sweets and other treats.</p>
<p>Thanks to its clear explanations, colour photographs and practical tips, the book is suitable for both beginners and experienced home cooks. As well as the recipes, it covers ingredients, preparation techniques, storage and labelling of homemade edibles.</p>

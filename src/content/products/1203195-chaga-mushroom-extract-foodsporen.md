<p><strong>Chaga (<em>Inonotus obliquus</em>)</strong> is a mushroom that grows on birch trees in cold climates, including parts of Northern Europe, Russia, Siberia, Korea and North America. Chaga has long been used in various traditional herbal practices, and in recent years the mushroom has been the subject of scientific research due to the presence of various natural bioactive compounds.</p>
<p>Foodsporen Chaga Extract is produced using a triple extraction method based on water and alcohol. This extraction method has been developed to preserve a broad spectrum of natural compounds from the mushroom.</p>
<p>Although Chaga is being extensively researched, there is currently insufficient scientific evidence to establish any health benefits or medical effects in humans. This food supplement is not intended to prevent, treat or cure any diseases.</p>

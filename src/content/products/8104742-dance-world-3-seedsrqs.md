<strong>Dance World</strong> is a low-growing, sativa-dominant cannabis plant that grows about 60-90cm tall. Her flowering period takes around 8 weeks in the process. This is comparable to the growth of an indica. In terms of effects, however, the strain is not comparable to indicas.
Like many of our medicinal cannabis strains, it has a high concentration of CBD, almost as much as the THC it contains. And as the name suggests, Dance World gives an uplifting, stimulating high. It is similar to the Jamaican dancehall music its parents are named after. These CBD-rich cannabis strains are used to relieve various complaints. Think inflammation, pain, anxiety, muscle spasms and many more.

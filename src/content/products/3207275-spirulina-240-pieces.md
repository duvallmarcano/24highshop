But what exactly is spirulina, and what makes it so special? Spirulina is a type of blue-green algae that grows naturally in freshwater bodies such as lakes, rivers and ponds. It has been around for billions of years and was used by ancient cultures as a potent food source. Spirulina is unusual among algae because it has a distinct spiral structure, hence the name 'Spirulina'.</p>

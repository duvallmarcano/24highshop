<p><strong>White Runtz (Vision Seeds)</strong> is a feminised strain known for its compact growth and rich resin production. You can count on sweet, candy-like flavours with creamy and slightly gassy aromas. <h3>Features</h3>

With a flowering time of around 8 to 9 weeks, this strain is attractive to growers looking to combine quality and efficiency.</p>

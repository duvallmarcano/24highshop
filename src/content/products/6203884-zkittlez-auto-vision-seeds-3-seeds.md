<p><strong>Zkittlez Auto (Vision Seeds)</strong> is an autoflowering feminised strain with a distinctly sweet and candy-like flavour profile. <h3>Features</h3>
<p>Zkittlez Auto was developed from the genetics of Zkittlez Z3, Grapefruit and Ruderalis, making for a stable autoflower with a predominantly indica character. The plant grows compactly and delivers resinous buds within a relatively short flowering time of around 8 to 9 weeks. Thanks to its automatic flowering, this strain is suitable for different growing environments and experience levels.</p>

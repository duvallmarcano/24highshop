<p><strong>PH Min</strong> is a pH regulator that allows you to lower the pH value of the feed water to the ideal range of 5.5 - 6.5. Only a nutrient solution with the right pH value ensures that the plant absorbs the nutrients in the right proportion. This in turn has a stimulating effect on the metabolism, so that the plant grows faster.</p>
<h3><strong>Dosage and use</strong></h3>
<p>Shake well before use. Add PH Min in steps of 0.5 ml per liter of water (1:2000) and continuously measure the pH value.</p>
<h3><strong>What are the benefits of Ph Min</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Revitalizing effect.</li>
<li>Can be used in the growth and flowering phase.</li>
<li>Suitable for all watering systems.</li>
</ul>

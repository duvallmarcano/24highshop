<p><strong><em>Panax ginseng</em> is </strong>a plant that grows naturally in East Asia and has been used for centuries in various traditional herbal practices. In Korea in particular, ginseng has a long history, and the root is used both in food products and in traditional remedies.</p>
<p>Ginseng naturally contains various bioactive compounds, including ginsenosides. These compounds are the subject of scientific research due to their potential biological properties. At present, however, there is insufficient scientific evidence to establish any health benefits or medical effects of ginseng in humans. This product is not intended to prevent, treat or cure any diseases.</p>

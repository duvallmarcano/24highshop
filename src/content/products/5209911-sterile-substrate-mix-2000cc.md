<h2>What is this substrate mix?</h2>
<p>This substrate mix is a growing medium for mushroom cultivation and consists of coconut coir and vermiculite. The combination provides an airy structure, good moisture retention, and a stable environment in which mycelium can optimally expand.</p>
<p>Because the substrate is sterilized in advance, the risk of contamination is significantly reduced. This makes the mix particularly suitable for both beginner and experienced growers.</p>
<h2>How does the substrate work?</h2>
<p>After adding colonized grain spawn, the mycelium grows throughout the substrate. The coconut coir retains moisture, while vermiculite provides airiness and structure. Together, they create a climate in which the mycelium can spread quickly and evenly.</p>
<p>The built-in microfilter (5.0 μm) ensures controlled gas exchange while blocking unwanted external influences.</p>

<h2>What are Dutch Dragons truffles?</h2>
<p>Dutch Dragons truffles are psilocybe sclerotia, also known as magic truffles. This underground structure forms from the mycelium of certain Psilocybe species. <p>Within the assortment, Dutch Dragons are often considered a stronger variety, known for providing a more visual and deeper psychedelic experience.</p>

<h2>Why choose a metal grinder?</h2>
<p>A metal grinder is sturdy, durable and easy to use. The sharp teeth grip the herbs as you turn the grinder, breaking them down into a uniform consistency. This means you don’t have to separate the herbs by hand.</p>
<p>The metal design also makes the grinder easy to keep clean and suitable for long-term use.</p>

<h2>Easy to use</h2>
<p>Place a small amount of dry herbs between the teeth and close the grinder. Then turn the top and bottom halves a few times in opposite directions. The finely ground material falls automatically into the collection chamber.</p>
<p>For best results, it is advisable not to overfill the grinder.</p>

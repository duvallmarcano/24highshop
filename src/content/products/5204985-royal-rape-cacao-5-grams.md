<h2>How do you use rapé?</h2>
<p>Traditionally, rapé is administered using a special blowpipe. This can be done in two ways:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Kuripe</strong> – instrument for self-administration</li>
<li><strong>Tepi</strong> – instrument for administration by another person</li>
</ul>
<p>Preferably use in a calm and safe environment, with respect for the traditional context. Many users choose a moment of silence or reflection.</p>

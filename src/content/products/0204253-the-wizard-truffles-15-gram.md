<h2>What are The Wizard truffles?</h2>
<p>The Wizard truffles are sclerotia from the Psilocybe family. Sclerotia are underground nutrient reserves of the mushroom and naturally contain psilocybin. After ingestion, psilocybin is converted in the body into psilocin.</p>
<p>Psilocin affects serotonin receptors and is associated with changes in perception, mood, and thought patterns. The experience differs from person to person and is strongly influenced by dosage, mindset (set), and environment (setting).</p>
<h2>What can you expect from The Wizard?</h2>
<p>Many users describe The Wizard as visually rich and mentally deepening, with a solid yet generally manageable intensity. The effects usually begin within 30 to 60 minutes and can last on average 4 to 6 hours.</p>
<ul>
<li>Pronounced visual patterns and mild distortions</li>
<li>Changes in perception of time and space</li>
<li>Deeper introspective thoughts</li>
<li>Enhanced sensory perception</li>
<li>More intense color perception</li>
<li>Creative and abstract thinking processes</li>
<li>Emotional depth</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>It is important to note that the experience may vary.</strong> For users without experience with stronger truffles, the intensity may be perceived as powerful.</p>

<h2>What is a dose?</h2>
<p>With truffles such as The Wizard, dosage plays an important role. What is experienced as suitable differs per person and depends on experience, sensitivity, and setting.</p>

<p>Using more does not automatically lead to a better experience. A mindful dosage and proper preparation generally contribute more to a valuable session than the quantity alone.</p>

<h2>What is brown rice flour?</h2>
<p>Brown rice flour is made by finely grinding whole, unhusked rice grains. As the bran is retained, the flour naturally contains more nutrients than white rice flour. In the context of grow kits, it is particularly valued as a stable and consistent source of nutrients.</p>
<h2>Why is this rice flour used in grow kits?</h2>
<p>When preparing a growing medium, balance is key. This rice flour mixes evenly with vermiculite and water, creating a light, airy structure. Many growers therefore choose brown rice flour as the basis for their grow kit.</p>

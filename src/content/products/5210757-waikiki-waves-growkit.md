<h2>How does a magic mushroom grow kit work?</h2>
<p>A magic mushroom grow kit contains fully developed mycelium that has already grown through the sterile substrate. As soon as the right conditions are in place – such as sufficient humidity, a temperature between 20 and 25°C and indirect daylight – the mycelium will naturally form mushrooms. As the grow kit is already fully colonised, you do not need to add any spores and can start the cultivation process straight away.</p>

<p>Upon receipt, you can start growing straight away or store the grow kit temporarily in the fridge at <strong>2 to 4°C</strong>. Then follow the included manual or download the detailed PDF instructions: <strong><a href="/en/">INSTRUCTIONS FOR SETTING UP YOUR GROW KIT</a></strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Store unused grow kits in a cool place (2–4°C).</li>
<li>Place the kit in the supplied grow bag.</li>
<li>Ensure a constant temperature between <strong>20 and 25°C</strong>.</li>
<li>Place the grow kit in a bright spot out of direct sunlight.</li>

<li>Always work as cleanly as possible to prevent contamination.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Extra tip during cultivation</h3>
<p>When the mycelium or substrate starts to visibly shrink, you can carefully pour a small glass of water down the inside of the plastic container. Make sure the water remains <strong>beneath</strong> the substrate and is about 1 to 2 centimetres below the top of the substrate. This will maintain the moisture level without the substrate becoming too wet.</p>

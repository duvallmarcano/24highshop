<p><strong>Quick One</strong> is a fast-growing autoflowering variety. She was among the first autoflowering varieties. At the time of her introduction, she was one of the fastest-growing varieties in the world.Quick One is descended from Canadian genetics and based on the original autoflowering variety Lowryder.Quick One is obtained from Canadian genetics and based on the original autoflowering strain Lowryder. She was bred by crossing Williams Wonder, Northern Lights and ruderalis. The ruderalis gave the plant its autoflowering characteristics. That means she will flower automatically without changing the light schedule. This genetics is at the cradle of almost all autoflowering strains available today.</p>

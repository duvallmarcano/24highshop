<p><strong>Gary Payton (Vision Seeds)</strong> is a feminised hybrid known for its rich aroma and balanced character. <h3>Features</h3>
<p>Gary Payton combines the genetics of Y-Griega and Snowman to create a stable hybrid with a slightly sativa-dominant profile.

<h2>How do you use Mexicana magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>After harvesting, Mexicana magic mushrooms can be used fresh or dried first. Bear in mind that dried magic mushrooms weigh considerably less than fresh ones, so the quantities cannot be directly compared.</p>

<h2>When should you harvest Mexicana magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>The right time to harvest is when the membrane between the cap and the stem begins to loosen. Don’t wait too long after that, as the mushrooms can continue to develop rapidly.</p>
<p>Gently grasp a mushroom by the stem and twist it loose carefully. Do not pull hard on the mushroom, as this may damage the surrounding mycelium and potentially affect the next flush.</p>

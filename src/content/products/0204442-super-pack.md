<h2>What are magic truffles?</h2>
<p>Magic truffles (also called sclerotia) are the underground part of certain <em>Psilocybe fungi</em>. In this compact structure, the mycelium stores nutrients. Truffles naturally contain the substances psilocybin and psilocin, which are responsible for the psychedelic effects.</p>
<p>Many users describe changes in perception, more intense colours, a deepened thought process and a stronger focus on inner thoughts. Exactly how the experience goes can vary from person to person and is influenced by factors such as dosage, environment and personal sensitivity.</p>

<h2>What are Psilocybe Hollandia truffles?</h2>
<p>Hollandia truffles are sclerotia from the Psilocybe family. Sclerotia are underground nutrient reserves of the fungus. They naturally contain psilocybin, which is converted into psilocin in the body after ingestion.</p>
<p>Psilocin influences serotonin receptors and is associated with changes in perception, mood, and thought patterns. How this is experienced differs per person and depends on dosage, mindset (set), and environment (setting).</p>
<h2>What can you expect from Hollandia?</h2>
<p>Hollandia is typically experienced as strong to very strong. Many experienced users describe the effects as intense, visually pronounced, and mentally deepening.</p>
<ul>
<li>Strong open- and closed-eye visuals</li>
<li>Enhanced colors and patterns</li>
<li>Changes in time perception</li>
<li>Deep introspection and self-reflection</li>
<li>Euphoric or energetic moments (depending on setting)</li>
</ul>
<p>Effects usually begin within 30 to 60 minutes and last on average 4 to 7 hours. Intensity varies per person.</p>

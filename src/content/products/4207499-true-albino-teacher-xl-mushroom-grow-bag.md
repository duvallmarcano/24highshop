<h2>Growth characteristics of True Albino Teacher</h2>
<p>True Albino Teacher has a calmer and more sensitive growth pattern than many well-known cubensis strains. Development is usually slower, requiring patience, stability and careful attention.</p>
<ul>
<li>Slower development than standard cubensis strains</li>
<li>More sensitive to temperature and humidity fluctuations</li>
<li>Selective and less aggressive pinning</li>
<li>Striking pale to white fruiting bodies</li>
<li>Requires more attention than beginner-friendly varieties</li>
</ul>
<p>Compared to Golden Teacher, True Albino Teacher is less forgiving. A stable temperature, good air exchange and clean handling are therefore especially important.</p>

<h2>What can you expect during cultivation?</h2>
<p>True Albino Teacher generally grows more slowly than Golden Teacher, Thai or Cambodian. As a result, flushes may need more time to develop properly. Many growers choose this strain precisely because of its unique appearance and the special character of its albino genetics.</p>
<p>Under stable conditions, the grow bag can usually produce several flushes. On average, you can expect two to four harvest rounds, depending on care, hygiene and climate control.</p>

Critical is one of our most popular strains. She is winner of many awards in Spain and loved by many growers around the world. As such, she has robust growth and produces big yields. Our <strong>Royal Critical Automatic</strong> has been bred to resemble Critical. But easier to grow and quicker to harvest!
Royal Critical Automatic was created to retain the very best characteristics of the photoperiod Critical. But easier to grow and with a greatly shortened life cycle. Why wait extra weeks of flowering when you can harvest a Critical in no time? This feminised autoflowering cultivar contains the already hardened genetics of Critical. There is also ruderalis in the mix to speed things up.
With a THC content of 14%, Royal Critical Autoflowering is not as potent as the original, which some consumers like. This cross contains significant sativa and indica influences. This makes her suitable for daytime use to stay productive and focused, as well as for evening before you go to sleep. The high is characterised by the relaxing and euphoric effects of the original Critical. But with less risk of couchlock.
As the name implies, Royal Critical Automatic naturally progresses to flowering. No light schedule is required. This makes it an easy-to-grow plant, especially suitable for beginner growers who would like to give Critical a try. But experienced growers will also be pleasantly surprised by the large, compact buds. These spread intoxicating aromas at the end of the flowering period. Scents of wood, spice and sweetness dominate the profile of terpenes. Just 9 weeks after germination, this Critical variety is ready to be harvested.

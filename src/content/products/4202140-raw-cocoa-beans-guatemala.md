<p>These <strong>raw cacao beans from Guatemala </strong>are known for their pure and powerful character. They have been used for centuries within traditional rituals and are still an important part of ceremonial moments. Originating from the FEDECOVERA cooperative, these beans grow in fertile, volcanic soils and benefit from the region's ideal climate. This ensures a full, intense flavour and an authentic experience.</p>
<h3>Characteristics</h3>
<p>The cocoa beans are 100% unprocessed and unroasted, preserving their natural character. They are carefully fermented in wooden boxes and then slowly dried on solar power. Thanks to this approach, the beans retain their rich flavour profile and quality. Ideal for preparing cocoa drinks, processing in dishes or for ceremonial uses.</p>
<h3>Ingredients and/or composition</h3>
<p>100% raw cocoa beans from Guatemala.</p>
<h3>Use</h3>
<p>You can eat the cocoa beans directly or crumble them and add them to dishes. The flavour is intense and bitter, so light roasting may be an option for a milder flavour and better processability.</p>
<p>To make a cocoa drink, lightly roast the beans (10-20 grams), remove the skin and grind them into a paste. Mix this with warm (not boiling) water and stir well. As desired, you can add natural ingredients such as cinnamon, vanilla or chilli.</p>

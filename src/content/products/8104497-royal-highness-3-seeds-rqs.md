<strong>Royal Highness </strong>is a feminised hybrid composed of genes from Respect and Dancehall. Respect is a cross between Juanita la Lagrimosa (a CBD-rich strain) and Cannalope Haze (a fantastic sativa). Combined with Dancehall, a wonderful Spanish hybrid, this has produced a potent marijuana strain. This one has an almost even ratio of THC to CBD. That means she has a lot of medicinal potency. You can therefore use her for the relief of various ailments.
Like most CBD-rich strains, Royal Highness is not as psychoactive as the more potent, THC-rich strains. This is often better for medicinal users who want to remain fully functional. The strain provides a very clear, active high. In the process, you'll experience a slight stoned sensation to complete the experience. Such strains are often used for their anti-inflammatory, analgesic, anti-anxiety and anti-epileptic effects.
Royal Highness is an interesting strain that has the growth of an indica, but is more like a sativa in terms of effects. She grows about 80-110cm tall, which is quite manageable for most growers. The smell is a mix of haze, skunk and sweet fruits. The taste is mild, sweet and a little bit skunky in the process.
Royal Highness yields around 425-475 grams of good medicine per plant and has a flowering period of 8-9 weeks. The strain is especially developed for medicinal users.

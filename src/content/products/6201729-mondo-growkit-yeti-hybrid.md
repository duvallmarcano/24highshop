<h2>How do you use the Mondo Yeti Hybrid Grow Kit?</h2>
<p>For a good start, follow the well-known 9-step plan or <strong><a href="/en/">download the instructions in PDF format here to set up your grow kit:</a></strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Fill the grow tray with water, seal it with the lid and leave to soak for 12 hours.</li>
<li>Drain the water and allow the grow kit to drain thoroughly.</li>
<li>Rinse the lid and put it away.</li>
<li>Pour approximately 150 ml of clean tap water into the grow bag.</li>
<li>Place the grow kit in the bag and lightly spray the inside.</li>
<li>Fold the bag over twice (keeping the microfilter clear) and secure with a paperclip.</li>
<li>Place the kit in a bright spot out of direct sunlight (20–25 °C).</li>
<li>Leave the bag sealed until the first pre-pins are visible.</li>
<li>Once growth has started, air the bag daily and lightly mist it (1–2 sprays).</li>
</ol>
<p>The first mushrooms usually appear after about two weeks. Harvest them just before the membrane under the cap comes away by gently twisting it loose – do not pull.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<p>On average, you’ll see the first magic mushrooms appear within 10 to 21 days. Sometimes it happens sooner, sometimes it takes a little longer. Factors such as temperature (around 20–25 °C), light (no direct sunlight) and adequate air circulation make all the difference.</p>

<p>At the start, the grow bag is usually kept closed until you see the first pre-pins. Once the pre-pins start to grow, it’s important to air the bag briefly every day to let in fresh air. When doing so, lightly spray the inside of the bag (1–2 pumps), so that the mushrooms don’t get wet directly.</p>

<p>Many users get multiple flushes from a single grow kit, provided you clean up properly after harvesting and rehydrate the kit thoroughly. The exact number varies depending on the situation, but good hygiene, a stable temperature and sufficient humidity will help you get the most out of your kit.</p>

<p>It is best to harvest just before the thin membrane under the cap (the velum) starts to peel away. At this stage, the mushrooms are usually at their best in terms of shape and development. Gently twist them loose (clockwise) rather than pulling, to minimise damage to the mycelium.</p>

<p>First, check the basics: is the kit kept at around 20–25 °C, does it get daylight (without direct sunlight) and does the bag remain sealed during the initial phase? Also check whether the inside of the bag is slightly damp (no puddles of water). Sometimes a kit simply needs a little extra time. If you notice any discolouration or a strong, unpleasant odour, stop the process and contact customer services.</p>

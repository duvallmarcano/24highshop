<h3>Use</h3>
<ol>
<li>
<p><strong>Preparing</strong><br/>Inflate the tub (takes ±2 minutes). Clean your hands, arms and the inside and outside of the tub with 70% alcohol. Also treat the red plugs and place them in the openings.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Make mix</strong><br/>Mix your fully colonised grain spawn with a moist substrate mix (test with a squeeze: 1-2 drops of water is perfect).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Fill</strong><br/>Fill the tub with the mixture of spawn and substrate. Mix and press lightly to remove air bubbles.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Growth phase (7-14 days)</strong><br/>Store at 21-24°C. Wait until the mycelium has taken over about 80% of the substrate.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Harvesting</strong><br/>Replace the red plugs with the white filters. Ventilate for 10 seconds daily. Harvest when the membrane under the cap detaches.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Restart</strong><br/>Add 1-2 litres of water, leave for 2 hours, drain and restart. Good for 3-5 harvests.</p>
</li>
</ol>

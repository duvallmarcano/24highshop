<h2>How do you harvest El Curandero magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>El Curandero is harvested slightly differently from many other magic mushroom varieties. Harvest when the veil beneath the cap begins to peel away on about two-thirds of the mushrooms.</p>
<p>Gently push the mushrooms sideways so that they come loose, rather than twisting them upwards. It’s best to start in the centre of the grow tray and work your way outwards slowly. Try to damage the top layer of substrate as little as possible so that subsequent flushes can develop properly.</p>

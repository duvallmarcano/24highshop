<h3>Origin and characteristics</h3>
<p>Trichocereus Bridgesii Cristata originated in the arid mountainous regions of Bolivia. The 'cristate' or 'combed' effect occurs due to a natural genetic mutation, disrupting the normal growth patterns of the cactus. This results in a broad, wavy, and often irregular growth pattern, giving each plant a unique appearance. The cactus has a deep green colour and, if allowed to flower, can produce beautiful, large flowers.</p>
<h3>Cultivation and growth information</h3>
<p>The Trichocereus Bridgesii Cristata is a relatively low-maintenance plant, making it ideal for both novice and experienced plant lovers. Some care tips:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Light</strong>: This cactus likes a lot of sunlight. Place it in a sunny spot at home or in the garden where it receives several hours of direct sunlight.</li>
<li><strong>Water</strong>: As with most cacti, it is important to avoid overwatering. Allow the soil to dry completely between waterings. In winter, when the plant is dormant, watering should be limited even more.</li>
<li><strong>Soil</strong>: Use a well-draining cactus or succulent soil. A pot with drainage holes is essential to drain excess water.</li>
<li><strong>Temperature and humidity</strong>: Trichocereus Bridgesii Cristata does not tolerate frost. Make sure it is kept indoors or in a protected spot during colder months.</li>
</ul>

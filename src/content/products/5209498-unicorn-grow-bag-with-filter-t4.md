<h3>Ingredients and/or composition</h3>
<p>The bags are made from sturdy polypropylene (3 mil thick) and feature a high-quality filter patch for gas exchange. They do not contain substrate, spores or mycelium; you add these yourself.</p>
<h3>Use</h3>
<p>Fill the bag with a suitable substrate and sterilise it if necessary. Once the substrate has cooled, you can inoculate it with spores, liquid mycelium or grain spawn using a sterile procedure. Then seal the bag tightly and place it in a suitable location for colonisation.</p>
<p>Thanks to the filter patch, the interior remains protected from contamination whilst allowing sufficient air exchange. The bags can be reused several times, provided they are cleaned and treated correctly.</p>

<p>Wild Dagga also called<strong> </strong>Lion's Tail or Wild Cannabis has been used for centuries as a medicine and remedy for snake bites. This beautiful garden plant originates from Southeast Africa. The<strong> Wild Dagga</strong> make its entry into the Netherlands around 1650. During the VOC period, it was Jan Riebeek stationed in Africa who is said to have brought the plant to the Netherlands. This medicinal plant works positively in combating fever and headaches. Here in the Netherlands, the plant is mainly used as a substitute for cannabis because of its similar effects.</p>

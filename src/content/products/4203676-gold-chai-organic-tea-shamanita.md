<li>Place the Shamanita tea bag in the jug or pot. If desired, add the bag containing your chosen ingredient.</li>
<li>Carefully pour the hot water over the Shamanita tea bag in the jug or pot.</li>
<li>Leave the tea to infuse for about 7 minutes. The exact infusion time may vary depending on your personal preference, but do not leave it to infuse for too long to prevent the tea from becoming too strong. If you’ve added your own ingredient, don’t forget to remove the tea bag in good time.</li>
<li>Remove the Shamanita tea bag from the jug or pot and throw it away.</li>
<li>Serve the Shamanita tea hot in cups or glasses.</li>

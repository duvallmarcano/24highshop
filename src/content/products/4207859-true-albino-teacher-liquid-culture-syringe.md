<h3>Use</h3>
<p>1. Storage: After receipt, store the liquid culture syringe in the refrigerator between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius (36-46°F) if you do not intend to use it immediately. Proper storage helps maintain spore viability.</p>
<p>2. Preparation: Allow the syringe to reach room temperature before use. Shake the syringe vigorously to ensure even distribution of spores through the liquid culture.</p>
<p>3. Substrate selection: Choose your preferred culture medium: sterilized grains or all-in-one substrate culture bags. Make sure your chosen medium is prepared and ready for inoculation.</p>
<p>4. Injection: determine the right volume for injection based on the size of your culture bag:</p>
<ul>

</ul>
<p>5. Inoculation: Inject the liquid culture into the chosen substrate in a clean and sterile environment. Aim for even distribution to promote uniform growth.</p>
<p>6. Incubation: Place the inoculated substrate in a warm, dark environment suitable for mycelial growth. Growth should become visible within 1 to 2 weeks, with the slowest progress within a month.</p>
<p>7. Monitoring: Check regularly for signs of mycelium growth. Once the substrate is fully colonized, you can move on to the next stage of your growing process.</p>
<p>8. Storage (if not all used): If you do not use the whole syringe, store the remaining liquid culture in the fridge between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius. Proper storage can extend the viability of the liquid culture by 2 to 6 months.</p>

<h2>What is perlite?</h2>
<p>Perlite is a natural mineral formed from volcanic rock. When heated, the rock expands into light, porous granules. These granules do not absorb nutrients but play an important role in the structure of the substrate.</p>
<p>In growing environments, perlite is mainly used to keep the substrate loose and breathable, allowing water and oxygen to distribute evenly.</p>
<h2>How does perlite work in a substrate?</h2>
<p>When perlite is mixed into a substrate, small air chambers are created. These spaces allow excess water to drain more easily and prevent the substrate from becoming compact. Many growers experience more consistent moisture distribution and a reduced risk of overly wet areas.</p>
<h2>Use and mixing ratio</h2>

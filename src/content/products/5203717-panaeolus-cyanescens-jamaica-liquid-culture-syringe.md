<h3>Use</h3>
<p>Inoculating your substrate has never been easier. With a simple injection of Panaeolus cyanescens "Jamaica" Liquid Culture, you can watch the mycelial network multiply and transform your growing medium into a canvas on which mushrooms can flourish.</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Sterilisation or Pasteurisation</strong><br/>To grow Panaeolus cyanescens "Jamaica" Liquid Culture successfully, a clean and nutrient-rich substrate is essential. As this species grows mainly on manure, it is important to sterilise or pasteurise the substrate to kill unwanted bacteria and fungi.<br/><br/><strong>Pasteurisation (recommended)</strong>: Heat the substrate (such as horse or cow dung mixed with straw) to 60-70°C for 1-2 hours using steam or a hot water bath.<br/><strong>Sterilisation:</strong> Use an autoclave or pressure cooker to thoroughly sterilise the substrate, especially if using cereal substrates.<br/><br/></li>
<li><strong>Inoculation of the substrate</strong><br/>After pasteurisation, let the substrate cool to room temperature. You can then use the Panaeolus cyanescens "Jamaica" Liquid Culture to inoculate the substrate. Use a sterile syringe to spread the liquid culture evenly over the manure-based substrate. Work in a clean, sterile environment to avoid contamination.<br/><br/></li>
<li><strong>Incubation - Mycelium Colonisation</strong><br/>Now that the substrate has been inoculated, the mycelium needs time to disperse and fully colonise the substrate. Place the substrate in a dark and warm room with a temperature between 24-26°C. Within 10-14 days, the mycelium should become visible. Keep the substrate slightly moist, but avoid overwatering.<br/><br/></li>
<li><strong>Fruit Formation - Optimal Growth Conditions</strong><br/>When the substrate is fully colonised, move it to an environment with the right conditions for mushroom growth:<br/><br/><strong>Humidity</strong>: Maintain high humidity (90-95%) by misting regularly and providing adequate ventilation.<br/><strong>Temperature</strong>: Maintain a stable temperature between 22-26°C.<br/><strong>Lighting</strong>: Although Panaeolus cyanescens does not need intense light, indirect daylight or a weak grow light stimulates mushroom formation.<br/><br/></li>
<li><strong>Harvesting - When are the mushrooms ready?</strong><br/>After 2-4 weeks, mushrooms begin to form. Panaeolus cyanescens "Jamaica" is known for its small but vigorous fruiting bodies with a thin stem and a curly cap.</li>
</ol>

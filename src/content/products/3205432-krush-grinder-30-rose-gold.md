<p>The Krush Grinder 3.0 rose gold is an impressive loose-leaf grinder made from high-quality 6063 anodised aluminium. CNC milling of the material ensures that the Kube 3.0 is manufactured to perfection. The four-piece construction of this grinder is fully magnetically secured and features a sieve bowl with acme thread, making the removal of the shredded loose leaf very easy.</p>
<p>The bowl of the Kube 3.0 has the perfect capacity to catch your shredded loose leaf. The design of the bottom of the bowl features a stainless steel 50 mesh sieve, trapping only the finest material. The special tooth design of this grinder allows two consistencies to be milled; anti-clockwise for coarse and clockwise for fine.</p>

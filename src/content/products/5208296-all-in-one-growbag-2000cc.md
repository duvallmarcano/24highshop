<h3>Ingredients and/or composition</h3>
<p>The bag contains a bottom layer of sterile maize spawn and a top layer of substrate mix of coconut and vermiculite. Equipped with a microfilter (0.2 μm) and a self-healing injection port. Supplied without spores or liquid culture.</p>
<h3>Use</h3>
<p><strong>Inoculation:</strong><br/>Inject 2.5 to 5 ml of your spore solution or liquid culture through the injection port. Work sterile and wear gloves.</p>
<p><strong>Incubation:</strong><br/>Place the bag in a dark place at a temperature between 20 and 24 °C.</p>
<p><strong>Colonisation:</strong><br/>The mycelium first colonises the maize spawn and then spreads to the substrate. The bag remains closed during this process.</p>
<p><strong>Fruit formation:</strong><br/>When the contents are fully colonised, open the bag to start the fruiting stage.</p>
<p><strong>For subsequent flushes:</strong><br/>Preferably use a separate growbag with microfilter to maintain proper humidity and limit contamination.</p>
<h3>Contents</h3>
<p>2-litre bag with layered construction of sterile maize spores and substrate mix. Supplied without spores or liquid mycelium.</p>

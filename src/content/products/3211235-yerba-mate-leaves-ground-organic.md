<p><strong>Yerba mate</strong> is a traditional South American herbal drink that has been enjoyed for generations in countries such as Argentina, Paraguay, Uruguay and Brazil. The leaves come from the yerba mate plant (<em>Ilex paraguariensis</em>) and, after harvesting, are dried and ground for use as tea.</p>
<p>Yerba mate is also widely consumed outside South America and is popular with a broad audience, including some (elite) athletes. The leaves naturally contain caffeine and other plant compounds that are characteristic of yerba mate.</p>
<h3>Use</h3>
<p>Use yerba mate as a tea. Place approximately one tablespoon per cup in a tea strainer or filter and allow to infuse for 3 to 4 minutes in hot, but not boiling, water. The infusion time and dosage can be adjusted to suit your taste.</p>
<h3>Storage advice</h3>
<p>Store the product in a cool, dry place, in a sealed container, away from direct sunlight.</p>

<p>The <strong>Levenhuk Rainbow 2L PLUS Biological Microscope</strong> combines modern optics with a cheerful and sturdy design. This microscope is ideal for curious beginners, schoolchildren and students taking their first steps into the world of microscopy. The dual LED illumination allows you to view all kinds of objects, from transparent aquatic organisms to opaque materials such as paper and dust.</p>
<h3>Features</h3>
<p>This microscope offers magnifications from 64x to 640x with three objectives (4x, 10x and 40xs) and a WF16x eyepiece. The 40xs lens features a spring mechanism that prevents damage on contact with the specimen. The optics are made of high-quality glass with a multilayer coating for sharp and high-contrast images.</p>
<p>The top and bottom lighting (LED) allows you to properly illuminate different types of objects. The lights can be used separately or simultaneously and the brightness can be easily adjusted so you always have the best view. The sturdy metal housing ensures that the microscope can take a beating and last for years.</p>
<p>The monocular head is tilted 45° and fully rotatable (360°), ensuring comfortable use, even in groups. You can connect the microscope to mains power or use it with batteries - convenient for both indoor and outdoor use.</p>
<h3>Ingredients and/or composition</h3>
<p>The set includes the microscope with three objectives (4x, 10x and 40xs), a WF16x eyepiece, built-in LED lighting, a table with clamps, an aperture disk, condenser, mains adapter and an instruction manual. Also included is the Levenhuk K50 experiment kit for a flying start to experimentation.</p>
<h3>Use</h3>
<p>Place a specimen on the table, choose the desired lens, adjust the lighting and you are ready to explore. The experiment set includes handy tools and instructions for making your own specimens, so you can start exploring right away.</p>

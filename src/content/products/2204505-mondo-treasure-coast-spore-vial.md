<h2>How to prepare my Mondo Treasure Coast spore vial</h2>
<p>Before you start injecting your spores, you should first have prepared a substrate in a culture container or bag where you will drop these spores. The sterile Mondo spore vials are very easy to use.</p>
<ol>
<li>Put on gloves and a mouth mask</li>
<li>Shake the spore vial vigorously back and forth</li>
<li>Take out the injection needle and insert the needle through the rubber membrane</li>
<li>Now gently draw the liquid into the injection needle</li>
<li>Take out culture container or culture bag with the substrate</li>
<li>After filling the syringe, slowly press the plunger and drop each drop scattered on the substrate. The spot where the drop falls on the substrate is a potential mycelium growth point, by spreading it out you will optimise colonisation.<br/><br/></li>
</ol>

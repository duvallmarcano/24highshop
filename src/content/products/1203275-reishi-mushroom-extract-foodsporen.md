<p><strong>Reishi (<em>Ganoderma lucidum</em>)</strong>, also known as the lacquer mushroom, is a mushroom that has been used for centuries in traditional Asian herbal medicine. In China, this mushroom is known as ‘Lingzhi’, a name traditionally translated as ‘mushroom of immortality’. This name has historical and cultural significance and does not constitute a medical claim.</p>
<p>Foodsporen Reishi Extract is produced using a triple extraction method based on water and alcohol. This method has been developed to preserve a broad spectrum of natural compounds from the mushroom’s fruiting body.</p>
<p>Reishi naturally contains various bioactive compounds, including polysaccharides and triterpenes. Although these compounds are the subject of scientific research, there is currently insufficient evidence to establish any health benefits or medical effects of Reishi in humans. This food supplement is not intended to prevent, treat or cure any diseases.</p>

<p>Kanna (Sceletium tortuosum) is a small plant native to South Africa, where three hundred years ago the Khoikhoi chewed its roots for euphoric effects. The Dutch who used to come to South Africa logically called it 'kaugoed'. It grows to a length of about 30 cm with low branches and thick, fleshy leaves. The plant has beautiful small yellow flowers. Growing the <strong>Kanna</strong> plant is similar to growing a cactus and thus generally considered very easy.</p>
<h3>Ingredients</h3>
<p>Kanna (Sceletium tortuosum) seeds.</p>
<h3>Cultivation information</h3>
<p>Kanna seeds are very simple to grow. Put some cactus potting soil in a poke, lightly press the seeds into this and give some water. The germination process can take two weeks to two months.</p>
<h3>Storage advice</h3>
<p>Store this product in a cool and dark place such as a kitchen cupboard and out of reach of children.</p>

<h3>Warning</h3>
<p>Kanna (Sceletium toruosum) acts as a selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI). Do not combine with other SSRIs (Seroxat, Prozac) or MAOIs. If so, always consult your doctor first and read the leaflet of your medication to avoid health risks. Also, do not use Kanna if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.</p>

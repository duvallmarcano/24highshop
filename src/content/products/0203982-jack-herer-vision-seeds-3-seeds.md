A sativa-dominant strain, a cross between Skunk #1, Northern Lights #5 and Haze, <strong>Jack Herer</strong> was so named in honour of Jack Herer by Ben Dronkers, founder of the Sensi Seed Bank. Jack Herer is a very popular strain and won the High Times Cannabis Cup several years in a row.
The Jack Herer strain has a light green colour with light orange to brown hairs covering it. Amazing quality medicinal cannabis that produces dense, easy to work with buds. <br/>Taste and smell are skunky, sharp musky and spicy. It gives you a light-headed almost euphoric feeling that is felt almost immediately.<br/>Jack Herer is considered one of the best in the world.

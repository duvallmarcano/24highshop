<p><strong>Garlotty (Vision Seeds)</strong> is a feminised hybrid with a rich and aromatic character. This strain combines sweet and fruity notes with a subtle skunky undertone, making for a distinct flavour profile. Thanks to its stable genetics and reliable yields, Garlotty is an attractive choice for growers of all levels.</p>
<h3>Features</h3>
<p>Garlotty brings together the genetics of Chemdawg and Gelato in a balanced hybrid with an equal ratio of indica and sativa. The plant develops compact, resinous buds and is known for its consistent performance. With a flowering time of around 9 weeks, this strain offers a nice balance between quality and efficiency.</p>
<h3>Ingredients and/or composition</h3>

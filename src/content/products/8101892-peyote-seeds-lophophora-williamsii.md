Peyote (Lophophora Williamsii) seeds are vigorous and therefore excellent for growing. The seeds grow &amp; nbsp; slowly. It takes several years before they can be harvested. Peyote is one of the oldest psychedelic Cactus. Peyote cacti have been found in graves and in caves dating back to the period of 4000 BC. The Aztecs regarded the cactus as magical and divine. She used the Peyote to treat illness and to communicate with ghosts.  <h3> Instructions </h3> <p> Use a grow box. Fill plastic containers (5 cm) with sterilized soil, place the seeds on the soil and press gently. Place the trays in a layer of boiled water and wait until the soil on the surface becomes moist. Take out the jars and cover them with plastic wrap. Optimal temperature at this stage is between 18 ° C and 23 ° C. After a few days to weeks, green baby cacti will develop. After about 4 weeks you can start hardening. Prick 2 holes in the foil and then every other day. You can remove the plastic if there is an atmosphere under the foil as outside it. Water with boiled water or rainwater. Do not let it dry out, but also do not give too much water. After a year you can possibly repot them, be very careful with the pen root. If the cacti turn yellow they need more light, if the cacti turn red they need less water. </p> <h3> Storage advice </h3> <p> Store this product in a cool and dark place such as a kitchen cupboard and out of the reach of children. </p> <h3> Contents </h3> <p> Peyote Lophophora Williamsii - 10 seeds </p> <h3> Caution! </h3> <p> Mescaline cactus can be dangerous for health when these are combined with certain medications, alcohol or MAO inhibitors.

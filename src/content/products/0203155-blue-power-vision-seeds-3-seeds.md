<p>The <strong>Blue</strong> Power is undoubtedly one of the most beautiful and enjoyable cannabis hybrids we have to offer. The Blue Power gives generous yields, this beautiful indica gets beautiful bluish flowers that harden to a soft lavender blue.When you smoke it it feels smooth , with a very distinct fruity taste and aroma, a blueberry flavour is evident which of course can be directly traced back to its Blueberry heritage.The historical genetic makeup of Blue Power truly represents genetics from all over the world, from India, Thailand to Mexico to Colombia, this is mainly thanks to DJ Short who is on the genetic side with the development of the "Blueberry" line at the base of our "Blue Power".Although average in terms of THC levels, Blue Power has a very powerful uplifting effect, when you combine this with her high CBD levels, you get a strain that is extremely suitable for medical purposes. Her effect works wonders for people struggling with anxiety, stress or migraines and has even been used with great success to help manage the symptoms of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD): hyperactivity, impulsivity and inattention.</p>

<p><strong>Amnesia Haze Automatic</strong> is the result of years of experimentation. A cross of the original Amnesia Haze, which was bred in the 1990s in the Netherlands by Hy-Pro Seeds. The original Amnesia, also known as Amnesia Haze, Champagne Haze, The Bomb, Ultra Haze and many other names, is a widely loved classic Sativa variety.Amnesia Haze was bred in the 1990s in the Netherlands by Hy-Pro Seeds under the name "Amnesia". However, she goes by many different names. For instance, you may know her as the original Amnesia, as Amnesia Haze, Champagne Haze, The Bomb, Ultra Haze, or something completely different. One thing everyone agrees on is the enormous power of this sativa-dominant strain.Amnesia Haze Automatic's long, plumed buds spread an earthy and citrusy aroma. This gets even better with curing.

<h2>How do you use The Perfect Pair Grow Kits?</h2>
<p>Both grow kits are set up separately. Follow the same 9-step plan for each kit. You can also <strong><a href="/en/">download the Mondo Grow Kit manual as a PDF</a></strong>.</p>
<ol>
<li>Fill the grow tray with water, place the lid on top and leave the kit to soak for 12 hours.</li>
<li>Then pour out all the excess water from the grow tray.</li>
<li>Remove the lid, rinse it clean and set it aside.</li>
<li>Pour approximately 150 ml of tap water into the grow bag and place the grow kit inside.</li>
<li>Fold the top of the bag over twice. Keep the microfilter clear and seal the bag with the paperclip.</li>
<li>Place the grow kit in a bright spot out of direct sunlight.</li>
<li>Keep the temperature between 20 and 25 °C. Leave the bag sealed until the first pre-pins appear.</li>
<li>As soon as the pre-pins start to grow, briefly ventilate the bag every day. Lightly spray the inside of the bag no more than 1–2 times.</li>
<li>Harvest the magic mushrooms just before the membrane under the cap starts to peel away.</li>
</ol>

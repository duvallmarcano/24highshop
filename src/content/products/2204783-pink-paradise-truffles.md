<h2>What are Pink Paradise truffles?</h2>
<p>Pink Paradise truffles are <em>psilocybe sclerotia</em>, also known as magic truffles. These underground structures develop from the mycelium of certain <em>Psilocybe</em> species and naturally contain psilocybin. In the body this compound is converted into psilocin, which may influence perception, thoughts and emotions.</p>
<p>The Pink Paradise variety is cultivated by the well-known producer McSmart and is recognizable for its pink color and warm character. In terms of strength, this truffle usually falls within the medium to stronger range of the assortment.</p>

<strong>Royal Kush Automatic</strong> is just a little different from what fans of autoflowering varieties are used to. She is a cross between hardened ruderalis strains and a fast-flowering Kush. You can partly recognise the Kush influence by the plant's appearance. She is compact, but taller than many autoflowering varieties. She can grow up to 60-90cm tall.

Royal Kush Automatic is ready to harvest within just 8-9 weeks after planting the seeds. Towards the end of the flowering phase, she smells quite strong. The aroma she gives off in the process is rich, spicy and even a little fruity. The smell gets even better after drying and curing the plant. The buds are not as compact as other autoflowering varieties without sativa influences. All in all, this indica-dominant strain produces high-quality yields. In doing so, it produces up to around 170g/plant under ideal outdoor conditions.
And if it's not enough that Royal Kush Automatic is so easy to grow, all seeds are feminised. This makes the process even easier. The effect of this cannabis is both physical and uplifting. The high can best be described as bright and happy. In short, Royal Kush Automatic is a great strain. Especially for the novice grower, she is perfect to get acquainted with growing Kush.

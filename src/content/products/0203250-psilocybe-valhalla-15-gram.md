<h2>What are Psilocybe Valhalla truffles?</h2>
<p>Valhalla truffles are sclerotia from the Psilocybe family. Sclerotia are underground nutrient reserves of the mushroom. They naturally contain psilocybin, which is converted into psilocin in the body after ingestion.</p>
<p>Psilocin interacts with serotonin receptors and is associated with changes in perception, mood, and thought patterns. How this is experienced differs per person and depends on dosage, mindset (set), and environment (setting).</p>

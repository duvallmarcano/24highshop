<p><strong>Biscotti Auto (Vision Seeds)</strong> is an autoflowering feminised strain with a sweet, dessert-like aroma and compact growth. <h3>Features</h3>
<p>Biscotti Auto combines the genetics of Cookies and Gelato #41 Auto to create a stable autoflower with a predominantly indica character. The plant remains compact and produces resinous buds within a flowering period of around 8 to 9 weeks. Thanks to its user-friendly characteristics, this strain is suitable for both novice and experienced growers.</p>

With the growing trend towards healthy and sustainable consumption, açaí powder is now rapidly gaining popularity on the global market. But what makes this powder so special? Açaí berries are a nutritional powerhouse. They contain antioxidants, fibre, omega-3 fatty acids, vitamins and minerals. The antioxidants in the açaí berry, primarily anthocyanins, are known for their ability to neutralise free radicals in the body. These free radicals can cause cellular damage, which may contribute to ageing and various diseases. This <strong> freeze-dried açaí powder</strong> is rich in essential minerals such as magnesium, zinc and iron, and also contains many phytochemicals.

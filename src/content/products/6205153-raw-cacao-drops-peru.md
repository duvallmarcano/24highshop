<h2>Usage</h2>
<p>The cocoa drops are easy to measure out and melt. You can add them directly to hot drinks, smoothies, baked goods or other dishes.</p>
<p>To melt the cocoa, it is recommended to do so in a bain-marie or at a very low temperature, so that the cocoa retains its texture and flavour.</p>
<p>To make a hot cocoa drink, heat water or plant-based milk over a low heat. Then add the desired amount of cocoa drops and stir until they have completely dissolved. You may wish to add spices or natural sweeteners such as cinnamon, vanilla or chilli.</p>

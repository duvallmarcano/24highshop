<h2>Instructions for the Mondo B+ mushroom grow kit</h2>

<p>Try to keep the temperature at 24 degrees. In any case, no lower than 18 degrees. We highly recommend using a thermomat. Light plays an important role in growing mushrooms. Make sure there is no direct sunlight shining on the growing container, but make sure it gets a few hours of sunlight or artificial light per day. This is necessary so that the mycelium understands that they have reached the surface. Moisture is important for growing mushrooms, as is fresh air.<br/><br/>Do not spray directly on the growing container. Spraying against the inside of the grow bag is therefore the best solution. Do this with 1 spray. After spraying, close the nursery bag immediately with the paperclip. When the veil between the cap and stem has just torn, it's time to harvest the mushrooms. Don't wait too long as soon as you see this. You can pick a mushroom by turning it clockwise. Don't pull on it, you might damage the mycelium, and you don't want that for the next flush.</p>

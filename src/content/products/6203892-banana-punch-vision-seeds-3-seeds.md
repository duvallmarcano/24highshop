<p><strong>Banana Punch (Vision Seeds)</strong> is a feminised hybrid with a rich genetic background and a distinctly fruity character. Combining sweet aromas with distinct banana notes, this strain delivers stable performance for growers looking for quality and nice yields. Thanks to its balanced genetics, Banana Punch is suitable for a variety of growing conditions.</p>
<h3>Features</h3>
<p>Banana Punch brings together the genetics of Banana OG and Purple Punch in an almost perfectly balanced hybrid. It develops compact, resinous buds and is known for its consistent results and attractive yields. With a flowering time of around 8 to 9 weeks, this strain is suitable for growers who want to combine quality with an efficient growing cycle.</p>
<h3>Ingredients and/or composition</h3>

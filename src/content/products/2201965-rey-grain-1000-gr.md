<h2>What is rye grain?</h2>
<p>Rye grain is a type of grain that is naturally rich in carbohydrates and fibre. In mushroom cultivation, it is mainly used as <em>a spawn substrate</em>: a growth medium on which mycelium can grow before being transferred to a larger substrate.</p>
<p>This variety is 100% natural and contains no additives or treatments. This makes it suitable for growers who want to work as naturally as possible.</p>
<h2>Why do growers choose rye grain?</h2>

<ul>
<li>Solid grain structure</li>
<li>Good moisture absorption</li>

<h2>How do you use rye grain as a substrate?</h2>
<p>Rye grain is usually used in the preparatory phase of the cultivation process. It requires some attention, but offers a great deal of control over the quality of your crop.</p>
<ol>
<li>Soak the grain in water to hydrate it.</li>
<li>Boil or steam it briefly so that the moisture is absorbed properly.</li>
<li>Sterilise the grain (for example, using a pressure cooker).</li>
<li>Allow it to cool, then inoculate it with mycelium.</li>
</ol>
<p>Always work as cleanly as possible to prevent contamination. Many growers use rye grain as a starting point before transferring the mycelium to bulk substrates.</p>

<p><strong>Cooking with Magic Mushrooms</strong> is an inspiring cookbook by <strong>David Connell</strong> for anyone who wants to incorporate magic mushrooms creatively into dishes and drinks. The book contains an extensive collection of recipes, ranging from snacks and drinks to flavourful dishes, all accompanied by clear step-by-step instructions.</p>
<p>As well as the recipes, the book covers ingredients, cooking techniques and handy tips for the kitchen. Thanks to its clear explanations, photos and illustrations, it is suitable for both novice and experienced home cooks.</p>

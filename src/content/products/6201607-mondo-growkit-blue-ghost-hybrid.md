<h2>How do you use the Blue Ghost Grow Kit?</h2>
<p>For a good start, follow the well-known 9-step plan or <strong><a href="/en/">download the instructions in PDF format here to set up your grow kit</a></strong>:</p>
<ol>
<li>Fill the grow tray with water, seal it with the lid and leave to soak for 12 hours.</li>
<li>Drain the water completely and allow the grow kit to drain thoroughly.</li>
<li>Rinse the lid and set it aside, clean.</li>
<li>Pour approximately 150 ml of clean tap water into the grow bag.</li>
<li>Place the grow kit in the bag and lightly spray the inside.</li>
<li>Fold the bag over twice (leaving the microfilter exposed) and secure with a paperclip.</li>
<li>Place the kit in a bright spot out of direct sunlight at 20–25 °C.</li>
<li>Leave the bag sealed until the first pre-pins are visible.</li>
<li>From then on, air it briefly every day and spray lightly (1–2 times).</li>
</ol>
<p>The first mushrooms usually appear after about two weeks. Harvest them by gently twisting the mushrooms loose just before the membrane under the cap comes away.</p>

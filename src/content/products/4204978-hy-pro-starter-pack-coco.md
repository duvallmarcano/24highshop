<p>The <strong>Hy-Pro Starter Pack COCO</strong> is a comprehensive package for growers using coco as a growing medium. This medium is popular for its durability and excellent moisture-holding properties. The package contains products specifically designed to support the growth and health of plants in such substrates. Here is a brief overview of the contents and how each product contributes to plant well-being:</p>
<h3>HY-PRO Root Stimulator Coco</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Before use:</strong> Shake the Rootstimulator Coco.</li>
<li><strong>Growth period:</strong> Mix 2.5 ml per 1 litre of water.</li>
<li><strong>Flowering period:</strong> Mix 5 ml per 1 litre of water.</li>
<li><strong>Application:</strong> Add this mix to every watering.</li>
</ul>
<h3>HY-PRO Grow Nutrition Coco A/B</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Before use:</strong> Shake the Grow Nutrition Coco A/B.</li>
<li><strong>Dosage:</strong> Mix 5 ml Coco A and 5 ml Coco B per 1 litre of water.</li>
<li><strong>Extra:</strong> When using your substrate repeatedly, also use Hy-pro Cal-Mag.</li>
<li><strong>Application:</strong> Suitable for all plants in coco peat - for both indoor and outdoor use. Always read the instructions on the label before use.</li>
</ul>
<h3>HY-PRO Epic Blast</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Before use:</strong> Shake the Epic Blast - Coco.</li>
<li><strong>Dosage:</strong> Mix 1 ml per 1 litre of water during the flowering period.</li>
<li><strong>Application:</strong> Add Epic Blast after the 4th week of flowering. Suitable for all coco plants - both indoors and outdoors.</li>
</ul>
<h3>HY-PRO Spraymix</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Before use:</strong> Shake the Hy-pro Spraymix.</li>
<li><strong>Dosage:</strong> Mix 5 ml in 1 litre of water during the growing period.</li>
<li><strong>Application:</strong> Use several times a week, spray under and above the leaf. Suitable for all cocopeat plants - both indoors and outdoors. Spray in the morning or evening to avoid midday sun.</li>
</ul>
<h3>HY-PRO Generator</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Before use:</strong> Shake the Hy-pro generator.</li>
<li><strong>Dosage:</strong> Mix 1.5 ml in 10 litres of water during both the growing and flowering periods.</li>
<li><strong>Application:</strong> Suitable for all plants - both indoors and outdoors.</li>
</ul>
<p>This starter kit provides comprehensive support for the development and well-being of plants in cocopeat, from root stimulation to flowering and growth nutrients. Carefully following these instructions can help achieve optimal results in your garden, whether indoors or outdoors.</p>

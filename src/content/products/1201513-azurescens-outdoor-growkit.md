<h2>How do you set up the Azurescens Outdoor Grow Kit?</h2>
<h3>Step 1 – Prepare the wood chips</h3>
<p>Fill a plastic container of about 1 litre about two-thirds full with small wood chips. Add boiling water so that the wood chips are completely submerged.</p>
<h3>Step 2 – Soak the wood chips</h3>
<p>Leave the wood chips to soak for about 12 hours or overnight. Then drain off all the excess water.</p>
<h3>Step 3 – Loosening the mycelium</h3>
<p>Wash your hands thoroughly. Open the grow kit and carefully loosen the mycelium using a clean fork or similar utensil.</p>
<h3>Step 4 – Mixing the mycelium</h3>
<p>Add the contents of the grow kit to the damp wood chips and distribute the mycelium as evenly as possible throughout the material.</p>
<h3>Step 5 – Covering</h3>
<p>Place a layer of damp brown cardboard over the mixture. Place the lid loosely on the container so that dust is kept out, but air circulation is still possible.</p>
<h3>Step 6 – Colonisation</h3>
<p>Leave the container at room temperature in a clean environment for about one month. When the wood chips have largely turned white due to the mycelium, the material is ready for outdoor use.</p>
<h3>Step 7 – Treat the remaining wood chips</h3>
<p>Place the remaining wood chips in a large bucket or pan and pour boiling water over them.</p>
<h3>Step 8 – Soak again</h3>
<p>Leave these wood chips to soak overnight as well, then drain the water completely.</p>
<h3>Step 9 – Prepare the outdoor bed</h3>
<p>Fill the cardboard box with both the colonised wood shavings and the newly prepared damp wood shavings.</p>
<h3>Step 10 – Mix everything together</h3>
<p>Mix the material gently and evenly using a clean fork or similar tool.</p>
<h3>Step 11 – Burying it outdoors</h3>
<p>Bury the open cardboard box in a shady spot, for example under a shrub or tree. If you wish, you can add a thin layer of damp soil, about 1 cm deep, and scatter grass seed over the top.</p>

<h2>How do you harvest Azurescens magic mushrooms?</h2>
<p>Check the mushrooms regularly during the fruiting period. A common indicator that it’s time to harvest is when the membrane under the cap starts to loosen.</p>
<p>Gently grasp the stem, slowly twist the mushroom left and right, and carefully pull it upwards. Work as cleanly as possible and avoid unnecessarily damaging the surrounding mycelium.</p>

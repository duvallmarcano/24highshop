<h3>Use</h3>
<p><strong>Inoculation:</strong><br/>Inject 2.5 to 5 ml of spore solution or liquid culture through the injection port. Work hygienically and use gloves.</p>
<p><strong>Incubate:</strong><br/>Store the bag in a dark place at 20 to 24 °C.</p>
<p><strong>Colonise:</strong><br/>Once the mycelium has spread about 7 to 10 cm, you can gently knead the bag to speed up the process.</p>
<p><strong>Use in monotub:</strong><br/>When the entire contents are colonised (white of mycelium), mix 1 part spawn with 5 parts substrate for further growth.</p>
<p>After complete colonisation, multiple harvests are possible.</p>

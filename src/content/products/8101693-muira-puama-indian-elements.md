<p>Also known as 'potency wood', <strong>Muira Puama Indian Elements</strong> is traditionally used for its special properties. This powerful plant from the Amazon region grows into a small tree about 4 metres tall. Especially the bark and roots of Muira Puama (Ptychopetalum Olacoides) are harvested and ground into powder. Perfect for making a hearty herbal tea.</p>

<p>Mexican Tarragon (Tagetes lucida) also known as: Yauhtli, Dragonafrika or Mexican Dragon, is a frost-sensitive plant with orange flowers that can induce potent lucid dreams. (A luicidal dream, is consciously experiencing and directing your dreams). The Aztecs crafted this potent plant into ritual incense and called it Yauhtli. They also ground the Terragon into powder and blew it into the face of the person chosen to be sacrificed to the gods. It is suspected that this was done because of the intoxicating and anxiety-inducing effects of the <strong>Mexican Tarragon</strong>. The Huichol Indians were masters in the use of Entheogens. These Mexican indigenous people used the Tarragon as well as Peyote mainly in ceremonies to induce visions. Tagetes lucida is said to contain a chemical compound very similar to that of Salvinorin A (Salvia). Salvia is one of the most powerful psychedelic plant on earth.</p>

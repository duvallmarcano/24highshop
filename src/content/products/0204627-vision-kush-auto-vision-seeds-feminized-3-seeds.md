<p>Classic genetics feminised on autopilot - photoperiod schedules out the window! <strong>Vision Kush Auto</strong> was created by crossing Tom Kush OG with Vision Jack Auto. This resulted in a very potent and productive autoflowering strain with a THC content of 19% and yields of a whopping 400 g/m2.It is not a strain for inconspicuous purposes, it can grow quite large and as tall as two metres if not contained/controlled - one of the reasons why we don't recommend this strain for indoors. She can be controlled quite easily, however, Vision Kush Auto is a tolerant strain. It produces strong physical and mental effects and a "couch-lock" warning is appropriate.</p>

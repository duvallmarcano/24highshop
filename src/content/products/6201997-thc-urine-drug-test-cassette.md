<h2>What is a THC test cassette?</h2>
<p>A THC test cassette is a rapid urine test that reacts to the presence of THC (the active substance in cannabis). By applying a few drops of urine to the cassette with a pipette, the testing process starts automatically. Within a short time, a clear result appears in the cassette's window.</p>
<p>This variant is often chosen by people looking for a convenient and accurate testing method, without complicated steps.</p>
<h2>How does the test work?</h2>
<p>The test has a limit of <strong>50 ng/ml</strong>. This means that the test reacts when the amount of THC in the urine exceeds this value.</p>
<p>The result is visible within <strong>5 to 8 minutes</strong>. THC is detectable in urine for <strong>3 to 5 days</strong> on average, but may be longer in case of long-term or intensive use.</p>
<h2>Use</h2>
<ul>
<li>Collect urine in a clean cup</li>
<li>Use the pipette to apply a few drops of urine to the test cassette</li>
<li>Lay the cassette flat</li>
<li>Read the result after 5 to 8 minutes</li>
</ul>
<p>Do not read the result later than indicated to maintain a reliable interpretation.</p>
<h2>What can you expect from the result?</h2>
<p>The test gives a visual indication via lines in the test window. Many users experience this display as clear and easy to read. The test is intended as an aid to personal judgment and not as a substitute for laboratory analysis.</p>

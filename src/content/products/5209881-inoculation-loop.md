<p><strong>Inoculation Loop</strong> is an essential tool for anyone working with spores. This metal inoculation loop - also known as a smear loop - helps you carefully scrape and accurately apply spores to an agar plate. Perfect for a controlled and hygienic procedure during mushroom cultivation.</p>
<h3>Features</h3>
<p>Made of durable NiCr 80/20 heating wire, this inoculation loop can withstand high temperatures and long-term use. You can also easily sterilise it in an autoclave, making it a safe and reusable instrument. Thanks to its compact shape, it fits comfortably in your hand and you can work extremely precisely - ideal for both novice and experienced growers.</p>
<h3>Ingredients and/or composition</h3>
<ul>
<li>Grafting eye diameter: Ø 4.0 mm</li>
<li>Length: 50 mm</li>
<li>Material: NiCr 80/20 (nickel-chrome)</li>
<li>Autoclavable</li>
<li>Use</li>
</ul>
<p>Use the inoculation loop to transfer mushroom spores to a Petri dish or agar plate in a sterile manner. Heat the inoculation loop before use to sterilise it and allow it to cool briefly before contacting spores.</p>

<p>Chocolate lovers will be surprised and enchanted by growing <strong>Chocolate Haze </strong>cannabis seeds. A phenotype of the strain Chocolope, this strain is resilient and produces a sweet and blissful high that leaves the smoker feeling uplifting, sunny and radiant. This unique strain will give you buds within 8 - 10 weeks that you can share with your friends soon after.Chocolate Haze, as a member of the Haze family, is a Sativa-dominant strain. It consists of 95% Sativa and 5% Indica. Haze strains were originally grown in California in the 1970s before crossing the world and becoming popular in Amsterdam. Chocolate Haze seeds have a THC content of up to 20% and give, even the most experienced smokers, a very powerful high.Experienced growers will harvest 450 - 500 grams of dried buds per plant outdoors. This resilient crop is easy to grow outdoors and will be ready for harvest in October or November. Due to its huge size, it is not recommended to grow indoors. This large, bushy plant is best cared for by experienced growers who know how to catch any possible fungal attack early. Indoors the plant can reach a height of 160 cm, outdoors it can grow up to 200 cm.</p>

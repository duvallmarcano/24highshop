<h3>Usage</h3>
<ol>
<li>Bring 1.5 litres of water to the boil in a kettle or pan. Once the water starts boiling, remove it from the heat and let it cool for a few minutes to about 80 degrees Celsius. This is the right temperature for the Shamanita tea.</li>
<li>Place the sachet of Shamanita tea in the jug or pot. If necessary, place the sachet with your addition to it.</li>
<li>Carefully pour the hot water over the sachet in the jug or pot.</li>
<li>Let the tea steep for about 7 minutes. The exact steeping time may vary depending on your personal preference, but don't let it steep too long to prevent the tea from becoming too strong. Do you have your own addition? Then don't forget to remove the tea bag in time.</li>
<li>Remove the Shamanita tea bag from the jug or pot and discard it.</li>
<li>Serve the Shamanita tea hot in cups or glasses.</li>
</ol>

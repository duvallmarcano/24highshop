<p>You are seeing the number <strong>420</strong> more and more often online and in the cannabis world. Think of terms such as  <em>420 friendly</em>, <em>420 time</em> or <em>420 blaze</em>. But what exactly does 420 mean, and where does this code come from?</p>
<blockquote>420 refers to the time 4:20 PM (16:20) and is recognized worldwide as the moment when cannabis enthusiasts come together to smoke a joint and relax.</blockquote>
<p>Although the term is now known around the world, many people do not know the story behind 420. In this blog, you will discover the origin of 420, how it became a global phenomenon, and which misconceptions exist.</p>

<h2>What does 420 mean?</h2>
<p>The term <strong>420</strong> refers to the time <strong>4:20 PM (16:20)</strong>. According to the most well-known origin story, this code comes from California in the 1970s. A group of students from San Rafael High School, known as the  <em>Waldo's</em>, used to meet every day at 4:20 PM to look for and smoke cannabis together.</p>
<p>Although they never found the legendary abandoned cannabis plant they were searching for, their meetup turned into a tradition. Eventually, this habit spread among other cannabis users, and 420 became a global term.</p>
<blockquote>420 symbolizes coming together, relaxation, and sharing a moment with like-minded people.</blockquote>
<p>Even today, many cannabis enthusiasts plan their smoke session around 4:20 PM. The term is also used to indicate that a location, event, or accommodation is cannabis-friendly, such as a  420 friendly smoking area.</p>
<h2>420 lifestyle: from subculture to global phenomenon</h2>
<p>The popularity of 420 grew rapidly when the magazine <strong>High Times</strong> gave attention to this tradition in the 1990s. As a result, 420 became part of global cannabis culture.</p>
<p>April 20 (4/20 according to the American date format) grew into an international day when cannabis enthusiasts come together. On this date, events are organized and people gather to enjoy cannabis and raise awareness for legalization.</p>
<p>Today, 420 is no longer just a time of day, but a complete lifestyle. You can see it reflected in clothing, accessories, festivals, and online communities.</p>

<h2>420 events and cannabis culture worldwide</h2>
<p>Various events are organized around 420 worldwide. One well-known example is the <strong>Global Marijuana March</strong>, also known as the Million Marijuana March (MMM) or Global Cannabis March (GCM). This movement started in 1999 in New York and is now organized in hundreds of cities.</p>
<p>During these events, both recreational and medicinal cannabis users come together to celebrate the culture and raise awareness about cannabis regulation.</p>
<p>Well-known 420-related events include:</p>
<ul>
<li>Global Marijuana March</li>
<li>Cannabis Liberation Day</li>
<li>Ganja Day</li>
<li>J-Day</li>
<li>World Cannabis Day</li>
</ul>
<p>These events highlight how cannabis use is becoming increasingly accepted around the world. Many users are ordinary people who simply want to relax after a busy workday.</p>
<h2>Misconceptions about the meaning of 420</h2>
<p>There are many myths online about the origin of 420. Below are some well-known misconceptions and the correct explanation:</p>

<h4>Myth</h4>
<ul>
<li>420 is Bob Marley's date of death</li>
<li>420 is the number of active compounds in cannabis</li>
<li>420 is a police code for cannabis use</li>
<li>April 20 is the best date to plant cannabis</li>
</ul>

<h4>Fact</h4>
<ul>
<li>Bob Marley died on May 11, 1981</li>
<li>Cannabis contains more than 500 chemical compounds</li>
<li>There is no official police code 420</li>
<li>Planting time depends on climate and location</li>
</ul>

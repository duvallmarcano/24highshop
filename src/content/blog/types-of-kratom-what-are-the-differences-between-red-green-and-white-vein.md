<h2>What are the best-known types of kratom?</h2>
<p>Red Vein, Green Vein and White Vein are among the best-known types of kratom. These names refer to the colour of the leaf veins of the <em>Mitragyna speciosa</em> plant. The tree is native to South-East Asia, including Indonesia, Thailand and Malaysia.<br/><br/>Read more here about <a href="/en/">exactly what kratom is and how it works.</a></p>
<h3>What do the different types of kratom refer to?</h3>
<p>The names Red Vein, Green Vein and White Vein refer to the colour of the leaf veins. However, a kratom variety is not determined by that colour alone. The plant’s origin, the soil in which it grows, the time of harvest and the way in which the leaves are dried and processed can all influence the final composition. As a result, even two products with the same label may differ from one another.</p>
<h3>Why are there different types of kratom?</h3>
<p>Kratom naturally contains various alkaloids, including mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine. The ratio of these to other alkaloids can vary from plant to plant and from harvest to harvest. In addition, factors such as climate, soil and the processing of the leaves play a role. This results in different types of kratom, each with their own characteristics. Scientific research into the precise differences between Red Vein, Green Vein and White Vein is still limited.</p>

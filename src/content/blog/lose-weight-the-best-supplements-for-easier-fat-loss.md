In fact, there are several plants that can help you lose weight. Okay, it is often not a panacea like Ozempic. But these plants can help you with appetite suppression, <a href="/en/">more energy</a> (useful if you want to exercise) and burning fat.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>Ephedra: the banned appetite suppressant and energy booster</h2>
<p>Slimming and energy pills used to contain the extract of the Ephedra plant. This plant can actually give you a lot of energy and curb your appetite. Its effects were compared to amphetamine. No children's stuff, in other words. For this reason, it is forbidden to use it in supplements. Growing it yourself is still allowed. <h2>Yerba mate: easier weight loss with tea as a supplement</h2>
<p>Several studies show that the plant <a href="/en/">Yerba Mate</a>, also found in various supplements and in tea, can help you lose weight. In a 12-week <a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4583719/">study</a>, participants were given either a Yerba Mate supplement or a placebo. The group put on the Yerba Mate lost more fat and had a smaller waist circumference. Furthermore, 'mate' is also extremely healthy, by the way. It gives you a fresh energy kick and is full of antioxidants, super healthy substances.</p>
<h2>Guarana: more energy, less fat cells?</h2>
<p>Guarana is another common ingredient in uplifting and slimming supplements. It is originally a berry plant eaten by indigenous tribes in the Amazon for health and in ceremonies. It gives you more energy, stamina and focus. And that, of course, is useful if you want to start exercising to lose weight. A supplement like guarana will simply keep you going longer. But there is another interesting benefit of this supplement for those who want to lose weight... Indeed, it seems to be able to counteract weight gain at the <a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30868680/">cellular level</a> by pressing the right buttons, in short. It also contributes to more stable sugar levels, which can help prevent type 2 diabetes.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>Coke nut: lose weight faster and burn more fat</h2>
<p>Last but not least among the uppers that help lose weight is the supplement <a href="/en/">cola nut</a>. Cola nut is the seed found in an African tree. The name of the drink 'cola' is derived from this, as this was in fact once an ingredient in Coca Cola. Cola nut contains stimulating compounds such as caffeine and colanine, which to a slight extent support your fat burning. It can be bought as <a href="/en/product/8108832-cola-nut-indian-elements">a powder</a> and in <a href="/en/product/8101817-cola-nut-indian-elements-capsules">capsules</a>.</p>

<h2>5-HTP: supplement with losing weight as an unexpected side effect</h2>
<p>Not into uplifting supplements, but still want weight-loss support? <a href="/en/">5-HTP</a> is a supplement that works on your serotonin levels, and this can help you lose fat. Most people use it to improve their mood. For instance, it is useful after a wild weekend to counteract the famous Tuesday dip. But also effective for depressive symptoms. Another effect that 5-HTP has is that it helps with weight loss. This is because serotonin is also involved in your appetite. And so it inhibits your cravings, so you (probably) eat less.</p>

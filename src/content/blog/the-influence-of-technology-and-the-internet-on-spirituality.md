<p>Before the advent of exact sciences, technology and the internet, everything in the world was pure guesswork for us humans. Falling stars, thunderstorms, bad harvests, dreams, diseases: it was all a mystery and mysticism. Often such an event was given a spiritual explanation, which with today's eyes turned out to be nonsense. Is the advent of technology the end for spirituality? Certainly not! Read here what opportunities technology and the internet offer us spiritual people.</p>
<h2>Spirituality in an age of science and technology</h2>
<p>The meaning of spirituality is different for everyone. Spirituality is all about your own spiritual experience. Religion and belief in the divine can fall under spirituality, but so can belief in invisible forces, special gifts, guides, visions, extraterrestrial life or ancestors. At first glance, it might seem that technology has nothing to do with this. Or even that tech and the internet spell the end for spirituality. But there are several reasons why this is not so.</p>
<p>You might indeed think that with the development of technology, there is much less room for spirituality. You could also say that technology and the internet actually offer new opportunities for those who want to engage in spirituality. After all, there is more opportunity to connect yourself, even if it is online.</p>

<h3>AI technology and spirituality</h3>
<p>In recent years, AI, or artificial intelligence, has been the big technological breakthrough. AI offers opportunities for people who are spiritual too. Combine ChatGPT with Bible texts, and you get <a href="https://biblegpt-la.com/">BibleGPT</a>: Bible texts based on your input. A great example of how you can use AI technology to make spirituality or religion more accessible to a wide (and often young) audience.</p>
<p>Even the great New Age thinker <a href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/deepak-chopra-artificial-intelligence-digital-dharma/">Deepak Chopra</a> is a fan of AI. He is positive about various possibilities of AI. For instance, you can use it for self-reflection, as a personal coach and even as a spiritual guide, he says. In his words, ""You have access to all the spiritual greats of humanity since the beginning of history". However, you do have to use an AI tool like Gemini or ChatGPT in the right way, by asking it the right questions. He compares AI to Aladdin's lamp. You have to use it in the right way to get what you want. By the way, Deepak Chopra has created a nice AI tool himself, merging the spiritual with modern technology: <a href="http://deepakchopra.ai/">DeepakChopra.ai</a>. In this tool, you can ask your questions and even share photos, upon which a profound answer á la Deepak comes out.</p>
<h3>Making spiritual friends online</h3>
<p>Online spiritual communities abound thanks to the internet. On forums and websites, you can very easily meet new people and expand your network. Through the internet, others inform you about spiritual events such as festivals, spiritual ceremonies, workshops and other gatherings. Or you can book a zoom-call with a medium or a <a href="/en/">ceremony</a> in just a few clicks. And of course, you will find all kinds of spiritual information on our website, e.g. <a href="/en/">spiritual tripping</a>, and spiritual cleansing. Thus, thanks to technology and the internet, spirituality and a spiritual community is never far away.</p>

<h3>More room for spirituality thanks to technology</h3>
<p>The more we know, the more we know what we don't know. It is even said that the more we know, the more we begin to <a href="https://decorrespondent.nl/2050/een-ode-aan-de-twijfelaars-met-een-slag-om-de-arm/74153875-18a8-056d-2c03-0d4755ba7bf1">doubt</a>. With the advent of science, we know about everything. And with the advent of technology and the internet, everyone knows about everything. However, many things remain that we cannot explain with science and technology-and therein lies spirituality! Inexplicable phenomena thus become extra beautiful or spiritual, because you can give them your own meaning.<br/><br/></p>

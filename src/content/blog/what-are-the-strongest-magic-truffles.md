<h2>What are magic truffles?</h2>
<p>Magic truffles are underground fungal structures that contain psilocybin, the same naturally occurring psychoactive compound found in magic mushrooms.</p>
<p>Although magic mushrooms and magic truffles are often compared, there are some differences. Truffles develop underground as compact nutrient reserves (sclerotia) and can be legally sold in the Netherlands.</p>

<p>The use of mind-altering substances goes back thousands of years. What motivates people to use such substances? Although many people use drugs recreationally, there are other motivations too. Here we discuss four common reasons why people may use drugs. However, keep in mind that this article is informative only, with no recommendations for use.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>What is being 'High'?</h2>
<p>With mind-altering drugs, many people think of getting 'high'. When someone is 'high', the substance affects neurotransmitters in the brain, which can alter mood and emotions. These effects can be induced by drugs as well as by natural activities such as exercise, meditation or <a href="/en/">breathing exercises</a>. In this article, we focus on the use of drugs, but also stress that there are always risks involved.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Reasons for drug use</h2>
<h3>1. Recreational Use</h3>
<p>Some people use drugs for pleasure or relaxation, especially during social occasions such as festivals or evenings with friends. It is important to stress that recreational use of drugs, such as cannabis, MDMA or alcohol, can always involve health risks. It can lead to dependence, negative mental effects and physical discomfort. Therefore, caution and responsible use are important.</p>
<h3>2. Performance enhancement</h3>
<p>In certain situations, people use stimulants such as caffeine or, in some cases, <a href="/en/shop/mushrooms/microdosing">microdoses</a> of psychedelic mushrooms. Caffeine is a legal substance that can increase alertness and focus. Microdosing is being investigated by some for possible benefits on concentration and mood. However, the scientific evidence for this is still limited, as discussed in a recent <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-01811-4">publication in Nature</a>. Always consider speaking to a medical professional before trying such methods.</p>

<h3>3. Self-medication</h3>
<p>Some people use drugs to relieve symptoms without medical approval, such as cannabis for pain or stress. Although cannabis can be therapeutic in certain cases and is legal in some countries, self-medication carries risks. For instance, long-term use can lead to dependence and adverse side effects. Always consult a doctor before considering using drugs for self-medication, and only use substances recommended by a professional. The risks of long-term use are also detailed by organisations such as the Trimbos Institute. With uncontrolled use, specific dangers can occur such as the potentially adverse psychological and physical effects of long-term exposure to psychoactive substances.</p>
<h3>4. Personal Development and Spirituality</h3>
<p>Some people use mind-altering substances for personal growth or spiritual purposes. Psychedelics are used in certain cultures to expand consciousness, which can contribute to self-reflection and introspection. However, it is essential to remember that this kind of use also carries risks, such as amplifying negative emotions or causing psychological symptoms. Using psychedelics without professional guidance is therefore not recommended.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Safety and Risks</h2>
<p>Whatever your reason for using mind-altering substances, it is important to pay attention to safety. All substances, both legal and illegal, can have harmful side effects. Long-term use can affect the health of the body and mind. If you are in doubt about the safety or use of a drug, seek help from a medical professional. The Trimbos Institute offers reliable information on the effects and risks of various substances. Note the legal restrictions on possessing or using drugs in different regions.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Medical Disclaimer</h2>
<p>The use of drugs, including cannabis, can lead to side effects and health risks, especially with long-term or frequent use. While some people may benefit from therapeutic use, it also carries potential risks. Always discuss with a doctor before considering drugs for medical purposes.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Conclusion</h2>
<p>The use of mind-altering drugs is complex and should be considered responsibly. This article provides information but not recommendations for use. Visit a recognised source or a medical professional for further guidance if you have any questions about the safe use of these substances.<br/><br/></p>

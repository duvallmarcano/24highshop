She conquered the whole world and thus became one of the foundation strains of modern cannabis breeding. In recent years, various cultivation and breeding techniques have resulted in different Northern Lights varieties. All with the best features of this legend, but each with something extra unique. We sell Northern Lights cannabis seeds from two different and very well-known seed suppliers. Although they are the same species, the genetics of both are slightly different. <h3>4. Quick One by Royal Queen Cannabis Seeds</h3>
<p>The Quick One is, as the name suggests, an extremely fast-growing autoflowering variety. She was one of the first autoflowering varieties. At the time of its introduction, it was one of the fastest growing cannabis plants in the world. The Quick One descends from Canadian genetics and is based on the original autoflowering strain Lowryder. She was bred by crossing Williams Wonder x Northern Lights with Ruderalis. The ruderalis has given the plant its autoflowering properties. That means it will flower automatically without changing the light schedule. These genetics are at the origin of almost all autoflowering cannabis strains out there today. Compact, fast growing plant with medium yield! The Quick One cannabis seeds from RQS are all feminized seeds. After planting, they become strong female cannabis plants. These are ready for harvest after about 8 weeks! Some plants take 9 weeks to fully mature, but that's still pretty fast. Especially when you consider that most photoperiod cannabis alone takes 9 weeks to flower.

<h2>Supplements that help you sleep better</h2>
<p>Frighteningly, many people use sleep medication to catch more hours of sleep or to suffer less from worrying in bed. The downside of these medications is that you quickly become dependent on them. After a short period of use, you cannot really do without them. Sleep then becomes a real struggle. If you want to get rid of sleep medication, fortunately there are all kinds of <a href="/en/shop/healthshop/sleep">supplements</a> that can help you sleep better.</p>
<h3>Melatonin as a sleep aid: when it works and when it doesn't</h3>
<p>Melatonin is the best-known supplement that can help you sleep better. The nice thing about melatonin is that it is a substance produced by the body. To be precise, it is a hormone that your body produces itself at dusk. However, that internal sleep rhythm can get disrupted, so you can give it a helping hand by taking melatonin. Melatonin supplements are also recommended when you have jet lag (or want to prevent it). This way, you reset your internal clock to the right rhythm with this supplement. Be sure to try our <a href="/en/product/3209726-daily-sleep-gummies-180-grams">gummies with melatonin</a> if you could use help with this.</p>

<h3>5-HTP and sleep: How it affects serotonin and contributes to better sleep</h3>
<p>Like melatonin,<a href="/en/">5-HTP</a> is a substance found in the body. Many people who buy this supplement from our shop use it to improve their mood. Thus, it is a well-known tactic against that Tuesday dip after a wild weekend. 5-HTP works positively on your mood because it stimulates the production of serotonin, or the happiness hormone. In addition, it can also improve your sleep as serotonin is also involved in that process.</p>
<h3>Valerian root for insomnia: effects, side effects and optimal dosage</h3>
<p>Besides bodily substances, there are all kinds of <a href="/en/">herbs that can promote sleep</a>. Lavender is a classic. And your grandmother will no doubt recommend herbal tea  with chamomile when you tell her you can't get to sleep. <a href="/en/product/8101452-valerian-indian-elements">Valerian</a> is another herb that improves your (night) rest. Used for hundreds of years by indigenous peoples, this little plant has now found its way into the supplement market. Handy, because this way you always have a natural sedative at hand. Valerian has a calming and sleep-promoting effect because it slows down the breakdown of the substance GABA. That way, it also inhibits anxiety and stress, making it easier to fall asleep.</p>
<h3>How CBD can potentially improve your sleep: Benefits, uses and risks</h3>
<p>CBD is short for cannabidiol. This substance is found in cannabis. It doesn't get you high, but it can make your life better in other ways. For instance, it is used for pain, but also against tension. As a result, this supplement can also help you sleep better by relaxing and having less trouble with your body. <br/><br/></p>

<h3>Scientific evidence on supplements for sleep problems</h3>
<p>Want to know how melatonin, 5-HTP, valerian and CBD really work? Here, we explain it scientifically:</p>
<ul>
<li>Melatonin and sleep: Studies show that melatonin is effective in relieving jet lag and restoring disturbed sleep rhythms<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24383580/">(Source: PubMed</a>).</li>
<li>5-HTP and serotonin production: Studies suggest that 5-HTP increases serotonin levels, which may have a positive effect on mood and sleep<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28260715/">(Source: PubMed</a>).</li>
<li>Valerian and GABA: Clinical studies confirm that valerian slows down the breakdown of GABA, a neurotransmitter that promotes relaxation<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16004965/">(Source: PubMed</a>).</li>
<li>CBD and sleep quality: Scientific literature suggests that CBD can reduce anxiety and improve sleep, especially in individuals with sleep disorders<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32448746/">(Source: PubMed</a>).</li>
</ul>

<p>Want to keep up to date with all the news around cannabis and its uses? Then visit one of these cannabis fairs in Europe this year. It's the way to keep abreast of the latest developments, network or get inspired. Attend interesting lectures, learn more about the versatility of cannabis products in workshops or immerse yourself in the latest trends. Just enjoying all the beauty that cannabis has to offer? Then trade fairs are the place to be. Luckily, there are some great events on the calendar again next year. From big business events to laid-back festival-like gatherings, in this article you will read which cannabis fairs in 2023 you should not miss.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>Cannabis fairs in Europe 2023</h2>

<h3>Spannabis Barcelona 2023</h3>
<p>One of the more popular events in 2023 is the Spannabis fair in Barcelona. This major cannabis fair will get a major update in 2023 and will be attended by scientists, medics, politicians and key representatives in the cannabis sector. You can attend conferences, exchange interesting information with other participants or make your move at the book section. There is an abundance of cannabis products from <a href="/en/">seeds</a>, <a href="/en/product/1210259-cbd-sleep-well-gummies-30-gr">sleeping caps</a> to <a href="/en/shop/seedshop/grow-supplies">growing equipment</a> for your plants. Besides being a very interesting cannabis fair, they also organise the Spannabis Champions Cup in which various prizes are awarded during a big party. This cannabis fair will be organised on <strong>10-11-12 March 2023 </strong>in Barcelona. <a href="https://magneticket.com/en/merchant/16">Tickets for Spannabis 2023 can be bought here</a>.</p>
<h3>CBD Hemp Fair Barcelona 2023</h3>
<p>Fan of Spain? Then the CBD Hemp Fair might be for you. This fair focuses particularly on the increasingly popular CBD. Investment bank Cowen points out in a report that the cannabidiol industry could grow from $2000 million dollars today to $16000 million dollars by 2025. Definitely an interesting trend to keep an eye on, then! At this cannabis fair, you will learn all the ins and outs about <a href="/en/shop/cbdshop/cbd-vitamins">CBD products</a>. From live demonstrations, music performances to conferences, they are all there. Networking opportunities are also part of the programme. This fair will be held on<strong> 29 and 30 September 2023 </strong>in Barcelona. <a href="https://cbdbusinessfair.com/en/home/">Tickets will be available soon and buy here</a>.</p>
<h3>CannaTrade Zurich 2023</h3>
<p>With more than 150 exhibitors from all over the world, the CannaTrade in Zurich is one of the biggest fairs in the list. From quality plants to <a href="/en/shop/cbdshop/cbd-oil">liquid products</a> and from stylish hemp clothing to cannabis games, you'll find it all there. It really is a fair with something for everyone. As a cannabis enthusiast, you are most welcome here and can relax in the Chillout-Area or eat your fill at the <a href="/en/shop/headshop/cannabis-edibles">Hemp Food</a> Festival. The Business Lounge is also a great place to make business contacts. The atmosphere is undoubtedly pleasant and worth a visit. The cannabis fair will take place on <strong>12 - 13 - 14 May 2023 </strong>in Zurich. <a href="https://www.seetickets.com/ch/event/cannatrade-2023/halle-622/2471525">Tickets for CannaTrade 2023 can be bought here.</a></p>
<h3>The CBD Show London 2023</h3>
<p>A great international cannabis fair in 2023 is the CBD Show in London. You'll find global vendors with exclusive information and products here. There are several seminars given by experts, influencers and exhibitors that you, as a cannabis enthusiast, can simply attend for free. This event is also very interesting for business as there will be as many as 5,000 suppliers present from start-ups to wholesalers, investors and pharmacists. Get ready for this beautiful 2023 event, where connection and diverse cultures take centre stage. The cannabis show can be attended on<strong> 22 and 23 May 2023</strong> in London. <a href="https://rasbmedia.com/cbd-show/cbd-show-tickets/">Tickets for the 2023 CBD show can be booked here.</a><br/><br/></p>
<h3>Mary Jane Berlin</h3>
<p>The last cannabis fair in our list is the Mary Jane fair to be held in Berlin in 2023. It is a fairly informal fair that you can also think of as a festival. The green power plant, as they call it at Mary Jane, can be used for an awful lot of things. From cosmetics to <a href="/en/shop/cbdshop/cbd-edibles">food</a> and building materials to medicine. Stock up with some nice seeds or treat yourself to a new <a href="/en/shop/headshop/grinders">grinder</a>. You'll find a diversity of products. For entrepreneurs, there is a nice atmosphere where support is key. You get the chance to introduce your products and build a nice network with an open-minded audience. The cannabis fair will take place on<strong> 23-24-25 June 2023 </strong>in Berlin. <a href="https://maryjaneberlin.ticket.io/y7wu2yng/tcehwz/">Tickets can be found here</a>.</p>

<h2>Caffeine: everything you need to know</h2>

<p>Caffeine is a stimulant that has several effects on your body. First, it naturally gives you more energy. It can also give your mood a positive boost. In addition, for most people, it improves their concentration. This is because caffeine acts on the receptors of the neurotransmitter adenosine. And let adenosine be the very substance that normally makes you slump.</p>
<p>This also brings us to the downside of (too much) caffeine: restlessness and difficulty <a href="/en/shop/healthshop/sleep">falling</a> asleep. Do you feel that coffee and other caffeinated drinks give you trouble sleeping? Not surprising... Caffeine can be active in your body for as long as 12 hours! If you suffer from this easily, be sure to read this article. Further on, you will find several good alternatives to caffeine and coffee; healthy replacements that will help you get to sleep.</p>
<h3>What is caffeine in?</h3>
<p>Caffeine is not only found in coffee and energy drinks, but also in products like tea and chocolate. Although you'll get most of your caffeine intake from 'black gold' and typical energy products, cups of tea also add up nicely. A mug of tea easily contains 20-30 mg of caffeine, which is half that of a cup of coffee. As a tea lover, you still get quite a lot of caffeine. If coffee makes you nervous, check your tea intake before writing off this healthy good. Go for <a href="/en/product/1206313-medicbd-green-tea-box-of-20-teabags-75mg-cbd">herbal teas</a>, for instance; these teas contain hardly any caffeine. That way, you can still enjoy your cappuccino shots.</p>
<h3>Coffee is healthy - and decaf a healthy alternative</h3>
<p>It may surprise you -especially if it makes you nervous- but coffee is fundamentally healthy. For instance, it contains antioxidants and combats cardiovascular disease, diabetes and Alzheimer's. The sweet spot to grab these health benefits is 3-4 cups a day. That can also be decaf coffee, by the way: it has the same positive effects on your body.<br/><br/></p>

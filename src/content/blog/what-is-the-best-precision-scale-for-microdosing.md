<h2>Why a precision scale is important for microdosing</h2>
<p>When microdosing, the quantities are so small that even a difference of a few tenths of a gram can be noticeable. That's why accurate weighing plays an important role in every microdosing routine.</p>
<p>The most important part of microdosing is already in the name: the dosage. With such small amounts, a regular kitchen scale or tabletop scale is usually not accurate enough.</p>
<p>A kitchen scale is perfectly suitable for weighing flour, butter, or chocolate. A difference of 1 or 2 grams makes little difference in those cases. With products containing psilocybin, however, things are different. That's why a precision scale capable of measuring to at least 0.01 grams is recommended for microdosing.</p>
<p>With a good microdosing scale, you'll know exactly how much truffle or magic mushroom you're using. This helps you keep your dosage consistent and reduces the chance of unexpected effects.</p>

<h2>How do you weigh truffles for microdosing?</h2>
<p>Before weighing truffles or magic mushrooms, it's important to determine which dosage is right for you. This depends on factors such as the type of product, whether it is fresh or dried, the product's potency, and your personal sensitivity.</p>
<p>The right amount depends not only on the weight, but also on the product itself and your personal sensitivity. If you'd like to understand why people can respond so differently, read <a href="/en/">individual sensitivity</a>.</p>
<p>You can also use the <a href="/en/">Magic Mushrooms &amp; Truffles Dosage Calculator</a> to calculate an appropriate amount. Be sure to pay close attention to the difference between fresh and dried products. You can also read our blog about <a href="/en/">the differences between fresh and dried magic mushrooms</a>.</p>
<h3>Step 1: Check your scale</h3>
<p>First, check whether your precision scale is working correctly. You can do this using <a href="/en/product/1205204-calibration-set-1g5g10g20g">calibration weights</a>. These weights are manufactured with high precision and help you verify whether your scale is still measuring accurately.</p>
<h3>Step 2: Use a clean weighing tray</h3>
<p>Place a clean container or a <a href="/en/product/9109783-weighing-cup-set-5-pcs">weighing cup</a> on the scale. Then reset the scale to zero using the tare function. This ensures that only the weight of the truffles or magic mushrooms is measured, not the container.</p>
<h3>Step 3: Add small amounts</h3>
<p>Place a small amount of truffles or magic mushrooms in the container and check the weight. Is it too much? Carefully remove a small piece. Too little? Add a small amount. Work slowly and accurately.</p>
<h3>Step 4: Also check pre-packaged microdoses</h3>
<p>Even with <a href="/en/product/0201218-microdosing-psilocybin-truffles-3-pack">pre-packaged microdoses</a>, it can be wise to verify the weight. This allows you to know exactly how much you're using and helps you maintain better control over your microdosing routine.</p>
<h2>Safety and accuracy when microdosing</h2>
<p>Microdosing requires care and attention. Only use products that you know and trust, start with a low dose, and don't increase your dosage without careful consideration. Also take your mood, environment, and any medication into account. If you're unsure, it's wise to seek advice from a qualified professional.</p>
<p>A precision scale is only one part of responsible microdosing. Factors such as set, setting, individual sensitivity, and gradually building up your dosage also play an important role in maintaining a consistent experience. Being aware of these factors makes it easier to determine which dosage suits you and how your body responds.</p>
<p>Also make sure to check the laws in the country where you are. Magic truffles, magic mushrooms, and psilocybin products are not subject to the same regulations everywhere.</p>

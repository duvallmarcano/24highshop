<p>A stoner room is the ideal place to use weed. A comfortable place to relax, be yourself and have everything you need. Of course, you can also get high on the beach or at a party. But sometimes you want to retreat to your own special room. What do you find in such a stoner room and how do you create it? We explain in this blog.<br/><br/></p>
<h2>What are the advantages of a stoner room?</h2>
<p>Do you use weed with some regularity? Wouldn't it be nice to have a specially decorated room dedicated to your cannabis use? It would be totally awesome if you could have a separate room for that. But of course, if you don't have that luxury, you could turn an existing room, such as a bedroom, into a stoner room.</p>
<p>Getting high then really becomes much more enjoyable: you know you are in a safe and comfortable place and you have everything a cannabis user could possibly need. Use our 7 handy tips to create a stoner room.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>7 Tips for creating a dedicated cannabis room</h2>
<p>Do you want to create the ideal room to use weed? We've collected a few tips to get you started. But do you have any other ideas? Unleash your creativity to make this room truly your ideal place to float to higher realms.</p>
<h3>1. Mood lighting</h3>
<p>Atmospheric lighting is the first step in creating a nice stoner room. Go for lamps with soft light, for a dreamy atmosphere in your room. Of course, you can go for more unusual lighting. Think of a projector that can make stars appear on the walls and ceiling or a cosy lava lamp. Be aware that the lighting in your room can have a lot of influence on your mood and also on how much you can relax. So make sure the lighting is soothing.</p>
<h3>2. Ensure good ventilation</h3>
<p>Many stoner rooms are no more than dark rooms without (open) windows or doors, where the smoke lingers like a dense fog. This may seem cosy, but it is better for your health if there is some ventilation. Of course, curtains may be closed, but make sure there is a window ajar for some essential fresh air.</p>
<h3>3. Important accessories for your stoner room</h3>
<p>The ideal stoner room is of course equipped with all the handy accessories you need when you smoke (or vape). First, of course, you make sure there are some nice <a href="/en/shop/headshop/smoking-accessories-ashtrays">ashtrays</a>, so you don't have to leave butts in flowerpots, cans or on the floor. Choose stylish ashtrays that can stand like jewellery in your room.</p>
<p>And of course, you'll find other <a href="/en/shop/headshop/smoking-accessories">smoking accessories</a> in your unique stoner room, such as rolling papers, lighters, rolling trays, scales and grinders. And if you or your friends use them, you can choose colourful or stylish shishas, <a href="/en/shop/headshop/bongs">bongs</a> or vaporizers. And, of course, you'll have your supply of the best <a href="/en/">weed</a> there.</p>
<h3>4. More atmosphere with candles and incense</h3>
<p>The right lighting goes a long way to creating a nice, relaxed atmosphere. But you can do even more. Light a few candles to create an even softer lighting that is sure to put everyone in a relaxed state. You could also opt for scented candles, which instantly add a pleasant scent to your room. This can counteract the somewhat heavy and overpowering smell of cannabis. Incense is also suitable for this purpose, by the way.</p>
<h3>5. Chairs to relax in</h3>
<p>It goes without saying that you make sure you have comfortable chairs or a couch. This is the ideal place to kick back, relax and completely lose yourself in the high. Provide a soft sofa or chair that is comfortable to lounge on. Such a lounge area does not have to be expensive: you can go for new, but a second-hand chair or sofa can also be a good option.</p>
<h3>6. Music, games, creativity</h3>
<p>Make sure your room also has everything you need to keep you busy. If you are creative, make sure your musical instrument is in your room, or drawing paper and pencils or paints to indulge in. A music system is also a good idea, as listening to music is a great experience when you are high. If you love gaming or watching movies, arrange a television and a game console. That way, you will have everything in your room to keep you (and your friends) occupied for hours.</p>
<h3>7. Food and drink</h3>
<p>A nice snack and/or drink is also something you want to have on hand. Maybe you can even arrange for a mini fridge. But at least having a bowl of snacks or bottles of your favourite drink ready to go is a good idea. Then you won't have to leave your room again because you got hungry or thirsty.<br/><br/></p>

<h2>Create your unique stoner room</h2>
<p>With these tips, you will surely succeed in starting to create your own stoner room. This is quite a process, it doesn't have to be finished in one go. You'll probably come up with other cool ideas. Maybe you want a fun wallpaper, buy some more cute posters and want a nice thick, cheerful coloured rug on the floor. It's best if your room helps you feel at ease, that you feel at home, and that you can really unwind there. Then you really will be able to experience the best highs there.</p>
